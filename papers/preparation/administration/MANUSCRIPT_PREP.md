# Administration, Organization, and Instruction Structure — Manuscript Preparation

Canonical scope: `ADMIN-001–003`.

## Central question

What is lost when instruction, interpretation, discretion, execution, result, delegation, review, revision, and error correction are treated as one organizational act?

## Recommended angle

**Instruction, Discretion, Revision, and Provenance in Organizational Decision Chains: A DSD Structural Audit**.

## Core mechanisms

- instruction != interpretation != execution != result;
- office/role != authority for the specific act;
- delegation is typed by direction, scope, object, and condition;
- a revision does not erase the existence of the prior instruction;
- correction and responsibility/provenance are distinct.

## Source freeze

Separate public administration, military, corporate, and other institutional families rather than treating one procedure as universal.

## Overclaim guards

Do not infer command from status alone, and do not reconstruct the original instruction solely from the final outcome.
