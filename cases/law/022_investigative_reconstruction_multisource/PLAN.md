# LAW-011 / Global Case 022 — Investigative Reconstruction from Multiple Imperfect Sources

Status: active first-pass cross-jurisdiction analysis.

Primary purpose: reinterpretation + falsification + predefinition audit.

## 1. Source problem

A past event cannot be treated as though the present investigator, witness, victim, suspect, court, or DSD analyst automatically possesses a complete event-state description.

Investigation instead works with surviving traces, perceptions, memories, records, physical evidence, source provenance, and procedural rules.

The motivating analogy is **triangulation**, but the analysis must test whether that analogy is too strong.

## 2. Strong candidate under attack

Candidate chain:

`historical event -> witness/trace observations -> collected records -> cross-source reconstruction -> institutional factual finding`.

Strong claims to falsify:

- a victim's present memory is direct ground truth of the past event;
- a suspect/offender's present memory is direct ground truth of the past event;
- more agreeing witnesses always means more independent information;
- physical evidence is contamination-free and self-interpreting;
- each new source monotonically shrinks the candidate-history set;
- investigation is literally geometric triangulation;
- a final institutional factual finding uniquely reconstructs the historical event;
- failure of one witness or evidence route proves the opposite event history.

## 3. External witness families

1. National Academies / National Research Council, *Identifying the Culprit* — perception, memory, confidence and eyewitness identification limits.
2. UK College of Policing — non-leading questioning, source confusion, co-witness information exposure, allowing uncertainty.
3. US National Institute of Justice — crime-scene preservation, contamination/loss risk, and combining physical evidence with case information and witness statements.
4. German StPO §160 — duty to investigate both incriminating and exonerating circumstances and preserve evidence at risk of loss.

## 4. DSD questions

- Can Formation preserve source-, role-, time- and provenance-sensitive records without pretending they are historical ground truth?
- Does the Formation stage chain become misleading if identified with a non-monotonic investigative belief-update process?
- Does observer-dependent describability offer only a structural analogy, or a justified application bridge?
- Can source dependence and contamination be represented without inventing DSD truth weights?
- Is Static Aggregation unjustified unless an external reliability/likelihood model is supplied?
- Does any direct contradiction with Formation remain after historical event, perception, memory, record, reconstruction and finding are typed separately?

## 5. Required order

`strong candidate -> counterpressure -> source witnesses -> revised model -> DSD mapping -> contradiction audit -> generalization status`.

Analysis and design remain separate. Do not force investigation into a DSD stage chain, and do not redesign DSD merely to mimic legal procedure.
