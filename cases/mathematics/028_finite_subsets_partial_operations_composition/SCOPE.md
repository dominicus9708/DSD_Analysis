# Scope

## Included

- finite subsets of an admitted channel set,
- union and disjoint union,
- closure and partial-operation questions,
- finite sums in the supplied term space,
- homomorphism-style preservation equations,
- injectivity and non-injectivity,
- structure-preserving maps at Formation level,
- finite counterexamples and witness constructions.

## Excluded from MATH-001

- empirical or physical interpretation of channel terms,
- countably infinite aggregation except as a boundary note,
- dynamical evolution,
- gravitational or cosmological projection,
- a claim that DSD creates a new branch of algebra,
- replacement of standard algebraic terminology with DSD terminology.

## Boundary rule

If an exact comparison requires changing finite sets into multisets, sequences, free commutative monoids, modules, or another source object, record that as `additional encoding`, not as a direct correspondence.
