# LAW-011 Result — Investigative Reconstruction from Multiple Imperfect Sources

Status: first-pass cross-jurisdiction analysis complete.

Primary purpose: reinterpretation + falsification + predefinition audit.

## 1. Answer-first result

The motivating triangulation analogy survives only in a narrow sense.

Investigation can be understood as using multiple source streams to constrain candidate reconstructions of a past event, but real sources are not exact independent sensors and investigative updating need not be monotone.

The strongest surviving candidate is:

**where an investigative or adjudicative source system distinguishes the past event, contemporaneous perception, later memory, recorded account, physical/digital trace, source provenance and dependence, uncertainty, investigative reconstruction, and institutional factual determination, preserve those states separately. Multiple reports or traces constrain reconstruction only through source-specific provenance, contamination/dependence, reliability/validity, procedural-use and interpretive rules. Do not treat witness memory, report count, physical evidence, or final institutional finding as direct omniscient access to the historical event.**

Compactly:

`historical event != perception != later memory != recorded account/trace != reconstruction != institutional finding`.

## 2. Eyewitness counterpressure

National Academies evidence shows that perception is affected by viewing conditions and that memory is malleable, reconstructed, updated and distorted over time.

Therefore:

`event state != what a person perceived`;

`what was perceived != what is later remembered`;

`later confidence != direct accuracy measure`.

This applies to any witness role. The analysis does not create a special DSD truth privilege for victim, suspect, police officer, or other observer.

## 3. Source dependence counterpressure

UK College of Policing guidance shows that misleading questions, feedback, co-witness discussion and later information exposure can alter accounts and create source confusion.

Therefore:

`number of witness reports != number of independent observations`.

Agreement among reports may be genuine independent corroboration, but agreement alone does not establish independence.

A source-dependence/provenance relation must be preserved where relevant.

## 4. Uncertainty is a legitimate state

College of Policing guidance recommends not forcing specificity when a witness cannot provide it and summarizes evidence that allowing `don't know` can improve accuracy relative to forced specificity.

This directly strengthens the project's predefinition result:

`uncertain != affirmative`;

`uncertain != negative`;

`uncertain != zero information`.

Do not fill uncertainty merely to complete a record.

## 5. Physical evidence is not self-interpreting ground truth

NIJ crime-scene guidance warns that non-methodical investigation risks contamination or loss and recommends combining scientific assessment of physical evidence with case information and witness statements.

Therefore:

`physical trace exists != trace preserved without contamination != trace interpretation uniquely determines event history`.

Physical evidence can be powerful, but provenance, preservation and the interpretive model remain separate coordinates.

## 6. Investigation is not accusation confirmation

German StPO §160 requires the prosecution office to investigate both incriminating and exonerating circumstances and preserve evidence at risk of loss.

This provides a source-law witness against:

`initial suspicion -> search only for supporting information`.

The initial accusation/suspicion and the later investigative reconstruction must remain separate.

## 7. Triangulation analogy — retained and rejected parts

### Retained

Different source streams can constrain the space of plausible histories and expose incompatibilities, dependencies or missing information that one source alone does not reveal.

### Rejected

The following geometric reading is too strong:

- all sources are independent;
- all measurements are exact;
- each new source only shrinks the candidate set;
- enough sources guarantee one unique true history;
- agreement count directly measures truth.

A naive monotone model

`H_(k+1) = H_k intersection Constraint(E_k)`

is not universally adequate.

If an earlier report is later found contaminated, dependent, misrecorded, or misunderstood, candidate histories may need to be reopened.

Use instead a general source-indexed update:

`H_(k+1) = U(H_k, E_k, Gamma_k)`

where `Gamma_k` carries provenance, dependence, reliability/validity, procedural status, and revisions to earlier source assessments.

## 8. Relation to LAW-001 through LAW-010

LAW-001: failure of one proof route does not create the opposite fact.

LAW-002: historical event/reference, source/record and adjudicative state are not identical.

LAW-004: evidence route and factual determination are connected through source-sensitive interfaces rather than one mandatory pipeline.

LAW-011 extends those results backward into the investigative epistemic layer:

`past event != what was observed != what is remembered != what is recorded != what is reconstructed`.

It also adds a new cross-case refinement:

**source multiplicity is not the same as source independence.**

## 9. DSD Formation result

Formation remains useful as a typed preservation discipline.

It can help preserve:

- source identity and role;
- separate observation/account/trace records;
- partial or not-yet-formed states;
- source-specific provenance annotations;
- distinct procedural regimes and uses.

But do not identify:

`historical truth != Formation complete descriptor`;

`witness memory != DSD observer state by identity`;

`investigative reconstruction != Formation composition by default`;

`investigative update != Formation stage chain`.

The last point is important. Investigation can be non-monotonic if an earlier source assessment is revised. Formation should therefore not be used directly as a belief-revision theory.

No direct Formation contradiction was found after this boundary was imposed.

## 10. Other DSD layers

### Axis-Property

Not required. The source materials supply no realized-axis interpretation.

### Static Aggregation

Not required. Witness reliability, source dependence, forensic validity and competing histories do not come with one universal scalar weighting scheme.

A separate probabilistic/statistical model would be required for numerical combination.

### Dynamics

Potentially useful only for recording time-indexed memory/account/reconstruction states.

It does not derive memory distortion, contamination, source dependence, or legal investigative rules.

## 11. Predefinition significance

LAW-011 strengthens four project rules.

1. **Do not predefine the historical event from the first account.**
2. **Do not force uncertainty into a positive or negative value.**
3. **Do not count dependent reports as independent corroboration without a source-dependence analysis.**
4. **Do not treat a later institutional finding as proof that the institution possessed omniscient access to the past event.**

The investigation starts from incomplete source states, not from analyst-owned truth.

## 13. Boundary statement

LAW-011 does not prove a universal criminal-investigation algorithm, does not claim that victims or suspects are generally unreliable, does not reduce truth to institutional procedure, and does not turn DSD into an eyewitness-memory or forensic-science theory.

It shows that the distinction among historical event, perception, memory, record, trace, source dependence, reconstruction and institutional finding is independently useful and that several tempting DSD-style simplifications fail under external evidence.
