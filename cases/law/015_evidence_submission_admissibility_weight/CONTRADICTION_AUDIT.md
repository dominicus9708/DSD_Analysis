# LAW-004 Direct Contradiction Audit

## C1 — Existing evidence must already have a legal-use value

Rejected.

Source procedure distinguishes existence from offer/application and from later use/investigation. DSD partiality can preserve a not-yet-reached status without forcing a value.

## C2 — Submitted evidence must be admitted/used

Rejected.

Criminal Procedure Act Article 294 permits submission/application, while separate evidentiary rules can bar use. Civil Procedure Act Article 290 can also leave an applied-for item uninvestigated when the statutory conditions are met.

No DSD rule identifies a source-side submission event with a later legal-use conclusion.

## C3 — Inadmissible evidence should be encoded as channel absence

Rejected as an application error.

The source legal event and evidence item can exist and be procedurally offered even when the legal system bars substantive evidentiary use. Encoding the entire item as absent would erase source information.

The correct application preserves an existing evidence-use record with an explicit negative/not-usable status at the relevant legal layer.

## C4 — Same collection defect must have the same admissibility result

Rejected.

Supreme Court 2024Da222212 demonstrates that civil admissibility depends on the applicable statutory/procedural route. A special evidentiary prohibition excluded one category, while another unlawfully collected category was not automatically excluded and was accepted after balancing.

The apparent conflict disappears when `collection defect`, `special exclusion rule`, `procedural regime`, and `admissibility result` are separated.

## C5 — Admissible evidence must have positive or sufficient probative weight

Rejected.

Criminal Article 308 and Civil Article 202 place probative evaluation downstream. Admissibility/use is a threshold question; weight and ultimate fact-finding remain separate.

## C6 — Legal probative weight should map directly to DSD Static Aggregation weight

Rejected as an unsupported bridge.

The legal sources do not supply a universal additive scalar coefficient, and the DSD static paper explicitly treats later coefficients/weighted statistics as application-supplied postprocessing. No direct identity is justified.

## C7 — Final factual equality implies identical evidence structure

Rejected.

Different evidence sets may support the same factual conclusion. The DSD Formation and Static Aggregation papers independently warn that downstream composite/aggregate equality is weaker than equality of the underlying typed structure.

This is structural correspondence, not proof that legal fact-finding is a DSD sum.

## Audit result

No direct contradiction found after matching:

- evidence-use instance,
- proposition/purpose,
- proceeding,
- procedural regime,
- stage/status,
- source-law rule.

The main risks were naive encodings that collapsed submission into admissibility, inadmissibility into nonexistence, or legal probative force into a DSD numerical weight.
