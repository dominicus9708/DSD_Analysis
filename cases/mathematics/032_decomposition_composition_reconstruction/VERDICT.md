# Verdict

**DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE — WITH FORWARD/INVERSE AND SUPPORT-TAG QUALIFICATIONS**

The DSD reconstruction layer is mathematically standard.

- fixed-support record recovery is ordinary injectivity of a sum map;
- direct sums give the standard unique-decomposition specialization;
- Stage-VII finite support recovery is a distinct-subset-sum problem and is exactly controlled by signed `{-1,0,1}` relations;
- support tags preserve information that a numerical aggregate cannot retain when selected zero is allowed.

No DSD contradiction was found.

The main rejected overclaims are:

- forward closure uniqueness implies inverse uniqueness;
- direct-sum/linear independence is necessary for finite-support recovery;
- a numerically recovered zero coordinate tells whether the channel was absent or selected with zero contribution.
