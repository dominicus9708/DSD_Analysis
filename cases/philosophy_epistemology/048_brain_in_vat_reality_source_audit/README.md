## Three-part structure

PHIL-005 keeps three questions separate.

1. **Generic skeptical BIV** — different external worlds may share one subject-accessible descriptor; this is a non-injective reconstruction problem.
2. **Physical implementation** — exact phenomenological/subject-level indistinguishability requires a declared equivalence signature and an explicit constitutive bridge from stimulation/interface variables to every admissible discriminator.
3. **Putnam 1981** — semantic externalism may shift the reference of `brain` and `vat` in the always-envatted case, but the further object-level conclusion `I am not a BIV` requires a justified regime-sensitive disquotation bridge.

## Main classifications

### Generic skeptical BIV

**Precedent convergence.** The thought experiment itself intentionally instantiates:

`same accessible projection + different full world`.

This is structurally aligned with DSD's reduced/full reconstruction discipline.

### Physical BIV equivalence

**Conditional only.** Equality of selected electrical stimulation does not by itself entail equality of all biological, sensory, agency, source-monitoring, and temporally integrated discriminators. Current neurotechnology does not establish a complete BIV-equivalence bridge.

A purely logical thought experiment may nevertheless stipulate full equivalence directly.

### Reality/source monitoring

Research supports a multi-cue distinction between internally generated and externally triggered experience. Source-monitoring errors and externalizing biases are associated with hallucinations in several clinical studies, while sleep/wake transitions alter environmental coupling. These findings support the *shape* of an external-coupling/source-monitoring hypothesis but not a universal medical claim.

### Putnam semantic BIV

The reference-shift component survives and continues the PHIL-004 precedent-convergence family. The stronger anti-skeptical conclusion remains dependent on a semantic-regime/disquotation bridge, matching established Brueckner-style circularity objections.
