# LAW-007 Plan — Status, Capacity, Power, Exercisability, Exercise, and Effect

Status: completed first-pass universal analysis.

## Question

Test whether a cross-system legal structure survives the distinction among holding a legal status/capacity/right/power, the present ability to exercise it, actual exercise, and downstream legal effect.

## Universal candidate before counterpressure

`status/capacity/power != exercisability != exercise != effect`.

## Counterpressure

Do not assume that every legal system uses these words as disjoint ontological categories. In particular, CRPD doctrine uses `legal capacity` broadly enough to include both holding rights and acting under law, while still distinguishing support and safeguards for its exercise.

Therefore the surviving candidate must be vocabulary-neutral:

**when the source system distinguishes possession/recognition, present exercisability, enabling conditions/support/authority, actual exercise, and downstream effect, preserve those distinctions and infer transitions only through the source rule that licenses them.**

## Witness families

1. CRPD Article 12 and General Comment No. 1 — recognition of legal capacity, legal standing/legal agency, support and safeguards for exercise.
2. UNIDROIT Principles — right existence, exercisability, notice-based exercise, limitation and enforcement.
3. Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties Articles 6-8 — State capacity, representative authority/full powers, unauthorized act, subsequent confirmation.

## DSD audit

Formation is the primary comparison layer. Axis-Property, Static Aggregation, and Dynamics are not required for the core result.

Do not identify legal capacity with Formation admission, legal power with realization, non-exercise with undefinedness, or ineffective exercise with channel absence.
