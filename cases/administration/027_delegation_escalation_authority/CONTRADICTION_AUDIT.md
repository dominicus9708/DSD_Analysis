# Contradiction Audit — ADMIN-002

## H1. Higher formal rank always determines operative decision authority

**Rejected.**

NASA explicitly separates Programmatic Authority from Technical Authority and independent review roles. FEMA delegations can define bounded incident authority. Army doctrine delegates decision authority to subordinates in specified areas.

## H2. Delegating authority necessarily delegates responsibility/accountability identically

**Rejected as a universal claim.**

Army ADP 6-0 expressly states that commanders can delegate authority but cannot delegate responsibility.

This is a source-specific rule, not a universal theorem; its analytical force is that authority and responsibility cannot be identified by default.

## H3. One organization has one simple authority chain

**Rejected.**

NASA governance deliberately creates multiple authority/check-and-balance chains.

## H4. Dissent/escalation is equivalent to disobedience

**Rejected.**

NASA has formal dissenting-opinion processes with protected upward escalation.

## H5. Transfer of command deletes prior organizational state

**Rejected.**

FEMA ICS requires briefing, documentation, communication of the effective transfer time, and continuity of incident information.

## H6. Review authority = execution authority

**Rejected as a universal identification.**

NASA independent review/Technical Authority structures exist precisely to provide review or concurrence separate from programmatic execution.

## H7. Delegated authority is unbounded unless revoked

**Rejected.**

FEMA delegation guidance explicitly defines duration, limits, fiscal and tactical constraints; Army doctrine speaks of specified areas and retained sole-responsibility decisions.

## DSD audit

### Formation

Can preserve role- and source-sensitive authority states, but legal/organizational authority is not created by Formation admission or role tagging.

### Axis-Property

Not required. A hierarchy or authority graph is not a realized-axis structure merely because it is ordered.

### Static Aggregation

Not required. Authority/responsibility/review strength does not come with one common scalar.

### Dynamics

May record delegation start/end, transfer effective time, escalation, review, or authority change, but cannot generate those rules.

## New DSD application risk

A relational order or hierarchy can tempt an analyst to treat `higher/lower` as a realized axis or as a universal authority ordering.

ADMIN-002 rejects that shortcut.

## Direct contradiction status

No direct DSD core contradiction found after separating organizational source rules from DSD representation.

The application boundary becomes stricter: **formal hierarchy alone cannot justify authority, responsibility, review, or execution transitions.**
