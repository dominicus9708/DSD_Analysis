# ADMIN-003 / Global Case 028 — Review, Revision, Reopening, Resumption, and Error Correction

Status: first-pass cross-domain falsification plan.

## Question

When an organizational directive, plan, clearance, or controlled configuration is already approved or being executed, how should later review, correction, amendment, suspension, resumption, supersession, implementation, verification, and closure be distinguished?

## Strong hypotheses to attack

1. Once approved, an organizational decision can only progress monotonically toward completion.
2. Any revision invalidates and replaces the entire earlier directive.
3. Error correction and substantive policy/mission change are the same operation.
4. Discovery of an error automatically authorizes the corrected action.
5. Review or approval is equivalent to implementation.
6. Implementation is equivalent to verification, release, or closure.
7. A resumed operation is simply the unchanged continuation of the pre-hold state.
8. If two processes reach the same final outcome, their revision/correction histories are organizationally interchangeable.

## Witness families

- U.S. Army fragmentary orders (FRAGORDs) modifying existing OPORDs and explicitly preserving unchanged portions.
- FAA amended clearances, correction/readback rules, revised instructions, and route resumption language.
- FEMA/NIMS Incident Action Planning cycles that execute, evaluate, validate/modify, and revise plans across operational periods.
- NASA configuration/change-control and corrective-action processes separating proposal, evaluation, disposition, authorization, implementation, verification, release, and closure.
