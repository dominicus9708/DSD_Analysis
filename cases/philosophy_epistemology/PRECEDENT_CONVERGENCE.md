# Philosophy / Epistemology — Precedent-Convergence Cases

This note tracks cases in which an established external argument is not primarily a target for DSD criticism, but instead independently exhibits a structural discipline strongly aligned with the Formation Axiom System, Axis-Property Axiom System, or related DSD reconstruction rules.

This category is intentionally narrow.

A case belongs here only when:

1. the external argument predates DSD;
2. its own source-level reasoning survives the DSD audit;
3. the survival is explained by a structural rule that independently resembles a DSD rule;
4. the correspondence does not require relabeling the external theory as DSD;
5. no historical-priority or novelty claim is made for DSD over that external argument.

## PHIL-004 / Global 047 — Putnam's Twin Earth

Classification: **precedent convergence**.

Putnam's Twin Earth argument is not treated primarily as an objection to defeat.

Its central structure independently anticipates several disciplines congenial to DSD:

- equality of an internal/reduced description does not determine equality of a fuller relation-sensitive structure;
- linguistic reference may depend on environmental, historical, or communal inputs not contained in the speaker's narrow internal descriptor;
- narrator-level environmental information and subject-accessible information must not be conflated;
- equal surface form does not reconstruct complete semantic/reference structure;
- the umbrella label `meaning` can hide multiple distinct semantic properties unless the relevant signature is fixed.

A compact DSD-style comparison is:

`I(O_E) = I(O_T)`

while

`E(O_E) != E(O_T)`.

If broad reference is defined by a signature such as

`R_B = g(I, U, E, H, C)`,

then

`R_B(O_E) != R_B(O_T)`

is structurally coherent. Environment is part of the constitutive semantic input; this is not an inverse inference from internal state to environment.

The DSD `Semantic-Signature Fork` is therefore recorded as a **typed formal restatement/sharpening of a structure already present in the precedent argument**, not as a refutation of Twin Earth.

The hypothetical inference

`same narrow/internal state -> same broad reference`

is retained only as a subordinate negative-control check on DSD itself.

## PHIL-005 / Global 048 — Brain in a Vat

Classification: **partial precedent convergence with separate implementation and empirical layers**.

### A. Generic skeptical BIV — reconstruction non-injectivity

The generic skeptical setup deliberately constructs different full worlds whose subject-accessible projections are the same:

`A_Q(W_N, .) = A_Q(W_B, .)`

while

`W_N != W_B`.

This is a historical example of reduced/full non-injectivity. It aligns with DSD reconstruction discipline rather than being something DSD should reject merely because the external worlds differ.

The broader epistemological conclusion that external-world knowledge therefore fails is not supplied by non-injectivity alone; it requires a separate epistemology of evidence and knowledge.

### B. Putnam's always-envatted semantic BIV

Putnam's causal/reference move continues the PHIL-004 structure: the same surface string may have different semantic/reference conditions under ordinary-English and vat-English regimes because causal history differs.

This aligns with DSD's requirement to preserve regime, typed property inputs, and full structure rather than identify surface equality with semantic identity.

The stronger anti-skeptical step from a semantic result about `I am a BIV` to the object-level conclusion `I am not a BIV` requires an additional regime-sensitive disquotation/truth-condition bridge. Brueckner-style objections to that bridge are established literature, so this part is historical convergence rather than novelty.

### C. Physical-realization layer — not precedent convergence

PHIL-005 does **not** classify the claim `electrical stimulation can produce complete BIV indistinguishability` as precedent convergence.

That claim requires:

- a declared subject-accessible equivalence signature;
- a relevant time interval;
- a constitutive bridge from interface variables to every declared discriminator;
- inclusion of any empirically justified reality/source-monitoring cues.

A logical thought experiment may stipulate full equality directly. Current neurotechnology does not establish complete biological BIV equivalence.

### D. Reality/source-monitoring layer — empirical context, not precedent evidence

Reality/source-monitoring literature is retained as empirical comparison for how internally generated and externally driven information may be distinguished through multiple cues. It is not counted as a DSD theorem, a medical generalization, or an additional precedent case.

## Evidence-family grouping

The first-pass synthesis groups precedent results conservatively.

### Family P1 — semantic externalism / relation-sensitive reference

Includes:

- PHIL-004 Twin Earth;
- PHIL-005 Putnamian semantic BIV component.

These are **one precedent family**, not two independent evidence nodes.

### Family P2 — reconstruction non-injectivity

Includes:

- PHIL-005 Generic BIV's `different full worlds / same accessible projection` construction.

This is structurally distinct from semantic externalism and may be retained as a separate precedent mechanism.

## Evidence-accounting rule

Precedent convergence is useful evidence of structural compatibility and independent recurrence, but it is not evidence that:

- Putnam or earlier skeptics were using DSD;
- DSD is historically prior;
- DSD axioms are ontologically true;
- semantic externalism follows from DSD alone;
- generic BIV non-injectivity proves radical skepticism;
- a physically complete BIV is technologically realizable.

The appropriate claim is narrower:

**an established external argument independently preserves a structural distinction that is also enforced by DSD.**

Also:

`multiple retained results from one source case != multiple independent evidence cases`.

Evidence independence is assigned by genuinely distinct external formal families or structural mechanisms, not by how many sub-results an audit produces.
