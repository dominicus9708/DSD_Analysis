# LAW-011 Source Notes — Investigative Reconstruction from Multiple Imperfect Sources

Status: source pass complete for first analysis.

## 1. National Academies — eyewitness perception, memory and confidence

Source: National Research Council, *Identifying the Culprit: Assessing Eyewitness Identification* (2014).

Official source:
https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/18891/chapter/2

Relevant findings:

- visual perception is affected by illumination, viewing duration, distance, stress/emotion, and distracting elements;
- memory is malleable and can be forgotten, reconstructed, updated and distorted;
- later information can alter memory content;
- courtroom confidence can diverge from initial confidence and is not a simple accuracy measure;
- standardized procedures, early confidence documentation and controlled identification procedures are recommended because procedure itself can affect later reports.

Structural use:

`past event != witness perception != later memory != later confidence statement`.

The witness is a source with an evolving memory state, not a lossless recorder.

## 2. UK College of Policing — leading questions and source confusion

### Non-leading questioning

Official source:
https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/non-leading-approach-questioning

The evidence summary states that misleading questions can cause witnesses to report information they did not witness and can reduce recall accuracy. Feedback about other witnesses can also alter later accounts.

Structural use:

`recorded account != untouched event memory`.

Interview procedure can become part of the provenance of the account.

### Clarifying sources of information

Official source:
https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/clarifying-sources-information

The guidance notes that exposure to other sources can create confusion between what the witness personally saw/heard and what was learned later.

Structural use:

`number of reports != number of independent observations`.

### Information exposure

Official source:
https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/advice-information-exposure

The evidence summary reports that co-witness discussion or misleading written narratives can lead witnesses to report information they did not directly observe.

Structural use:

agreement among accounts may arise partly from shared post-event information.

### Allowing uncertainty

Official source:
https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/allowing-uncertainty

The guidance recommends not pushing a witness who cannot be specific. Experimental evidence summarized there indicates that allowing `don't know` responses can improve accuracy relative to forced specificity.

Structural use:

`uncertainty != failure that must be filled with a positive or negative answer`.

This is a direct predefinition node.

## 3. US National Institute of Justice — physical evidence preservation and multimodal assessment

Official source:
https://nij.ojp.gov/topics/articles/crime-scene-investigation-guides-law-enforcement

The NIJ guide warns that non-methodical crime-scene work risks contamination or loss of evidence and recommends combining scientific assessment of physical evidence with case information and witness statements.

Structural use:

- physical evidence is not assumed contamination-free merely because it is physical;
- evidence provenance and preservation matter;
- investigation may integrate heterogeneous source modalities rather than treating one source type as total ground truth.

Therefore:

`physical trace != self-interpreting historical truth`.

## 4. German StPO §160 — investigation is not one-sided confirmation

Official German source:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stpo/__160.html

Official English translation:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_stpo/englisch_stpo.html

Section 160 requires the public prosecution office to investigate the facts to decide whether charges should be brought, ascertain both incriminating and exonerating circumstances, and secure evidence at risk of loss.

Structural use:

A source legal regime can require the investigative process to search for information that challenges the initial suspicion rather than merely confirming it.

This defeats the model:

`initial suspicion -> evidence collection only in support of suspicion`.

## 5. Source-family synthesis

The independent source families support the following distinctions:

`historical event`

`!= contemporaneous perception`

`!= later memory`

`!= interview-produced/recorded account`

`!= physical trace and its preservation state`

`!= investigative reconstruction`

`!= institutional factual determination`.

They also show that source dependence, contamination, procedure, uncertainty and alternative hypotheses must remain explicit.

## 6. Limits

These sources do not establish that every criminal investigation uses one formal reconstruction algorithm.

They do not justify one universal numerical reliability score for witnesses, physical evidence or source independence.

They do not prove that a mathematical intersection of evidence constraints monotonically converges to the historical truth.

The triangulation analogy remains heuristic unless a separate probabilistic or geometric model is supplied.
