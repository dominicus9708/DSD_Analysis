# LAW-008 Plan — Rule Conflict, Exception, and Priority

Status: active -> first-pass universal analysis.

Primary purpose: reinterpretation.
Secondary purposes: coherence, predefinition audit, conflict-resolution boundary, universal-claim falsification.

## Universal candidate to test

Do not treat two differently worded or differently directed rules as one unresolved contradiction merely because both can be mentioned in relation to the same broad topic.

A source-faithful conflict analysis must first preserve at least:

- rule identity;
- legal subject/actor;
- regulated act or proposition;
- scope and applicability conditions;
- time;
- parties/jurisdiction/regime;
- exception or exclusion conditions;
- source-supplied hierarchy, priority, harmonization, speciality, temporal, or invalidity rules.

Only after these are matched should an unresolved conflict candidate be asserted.

## Counterpressure

The initial slogan `conflict -> one rule wins` is intentionally attacked.

Possible counterexamples:

1. both norms are interpreted harmoniously and no choice is required;
2. a special rule governs without extinguishing the general rule;
3. a later treaty governs only for some party relations;
4. a hierarchy rule makes one obligation prevail but does not itself declare the other instrument nonexistent;
5. conflict with a peremptory norm produces invalidity rather than mere priority;
6. the source system leaves a genuine unresolved normative conflict.

## Witness families

1. Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (VCLT), especially Articles 30, 31, 53.
2. United Nations Charter Article 103.
3. International Law Commission, 2006 Fragmentation Conclusions, especially harmonization, relationships of conflict, and lex specialis.

## DSD questions

- Can Formation preserve two valid/applied source rules as distinct typed records without prematurely normalizing them?
- Does rule priority correspond to channel deletion? Expected answer: no.
- Does an exception correspond to defined zero or absence? Expected answer: no universal identity.
- Is Axis-Property required? Expected answer: no unless a separate realized-axis semantics is supplied.
- Is Static Aggregation required? Expected answer: no.

## Completion test

LAW-008 is complete when:

- a conflict predicate is narrowed by counterpressure;
- multiple source-side conflict consequences are distinguished;
- finite witnesses defeat `CONFLICT={YES,NO} -> one universal consequence` encoding;
- direct contradiction with Formation is audited after type/scope/regime matching;
- generalization status is recorded separately from source completion.
