# LAW-003 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Plan

Status: active revalidation.

Original `RESULT.md` remains the historical Korean first pass.

## Candidate under test

Initial candidate:

`underlying person/entity != operative role != authority != act != attributed effect`.

This must not be treated as a rigid universal five-step pipeline.

## Strong forms to attack

1. `no actual authority -> no possible downstream effect or attribution`;
2. `actual authority -> all related acts/effects are authorized`;
3. `same person -> same legal capacity/role`;
4. `office/organ status -> every act of that person/entity is attributable`;
5. `unauthorized act -> legally nonexistent event`;
6. `later confirmation/ratification merely reveals that authority was already present`.

## Independent witness families

- UNIDROIT Principles, Chapter 2 Section 2: authority, apparent authority, unauthorized acts, ratification, termination;
- Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties 1969, Articles 7-8: representation/full powers and subsequent confirmation;
- ILC Articles on Responsibility of States for Internationally Wrongful Acts, Articles 4, 7 and related commentary: organ status, official capacity, ultra vires attribution;
- original Korean Civil Act/Supreme Court materials retained as domestic witness only.

## Required distinctions

Preserve separately where the source does:

`person/entity`

`role/capacity`

`actual authority / scope`

`appearance / reliance / official capacity`

`performed act`

`ordinary effect`

`attribution`

`ratification / later confirmation`

`time/regime`.

## DSD audit

Use Formation only as a typed role/non-totalization discipline. The current Formation channel `c=(p,a,lambda,v,rho)` retains role in operational identity and permits role multiplicity. Do not identify a legal person, authority, legal act, attribution rule, or ratification with a Formation primitive or stage.

Axis-Property remains unnecessary unless an independently justified realized-axis interpretation appears.

Dynamics may be relevant only for time-indexed ratification/confirmation, but legal retroactivity/effect remains an external rule.

## Decision rule

A contradiction counts only after person/entity, legal capacity, act token, authority scope, attribution rule, temporal status, and legal regime are matched.

Final order:

`candidate -> counterpressure -> independent witnesses -> DSD mapping -> contradiction audit -> generalization status`.
