# LAW-008 Scope

## Universal structural candidate

A rule difference is not automatically an unresolved legal contradiction, and a legal conflict does not determine one universal consequence.

Preserve source-side:

- rule identity;
- validity/in-force status;
- applicability;
- subject/actor/action/context;
- time;
- parties/jurisdiction/regime;
- exception/scope conditions;
- harmonization, speciality, hierarchy, temporal or invalidity relation;
- downstream consequence.

Only the source system may license a transition from these coordinates to a resolved legal outcome.

## Witness status

Independent source families used:

- VCLT Article 30 — party/compatibility-sensitive successive-treaty relation;
- UN Charter Article 103 — priority of Charter obligations in conflict;
- VCLT Article 53 — invalidity for conflict with jus cogens;
- ILC Fragmentation Conclusions — interpretation/conflict distinction, harmonization, lex specialis and persistence of general law.

## Generalization status

`universal candidate supported by multiple independent international-law structures; active falsification remains open`.

This is not a proof of one universal conflict-of-laws algorithm.

## DSD boundary

Formation supplies typed/status separation only.

It does not supply legal hierarchy, priority, exception, invalidity or conflict resolution.

Axis-Property and Static Aggregation are not required for the first-pass result.
