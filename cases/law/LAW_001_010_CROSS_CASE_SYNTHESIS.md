# LAW-001 through LAW-010 Cross-Case Synthesis

Status: first legal-domain cross-case synthesis complete.

Scope: LAW-001 through LAW-010, including the completed cross-jurisdiction revalidations of LAW-001 through LAW-004.

This synthesis does not create a new legal doctrine and does not treat DSD as a foundation of law. It records which structural candidates survived independent legal counterpressure, which stronger DSD-style application assumptions were rejected, and which operating constraints should govern later DSD Analysis.

## 1. Answer-first result

The strongest recurring result is not a universal legal pipeline, office taxonomy, evidence sequence, authority model, sanction theory, or normative algorithm.

The strongest surviving cross-case discipline is:

**preserve source-defined distinctions and interfaces, and do not assign either a positive or negative downstream status, infer an upstream identity from a downstream effect, or normalize several source-distinct coordinates into one global value unless the source system or an independently justified external bridge supplies the rule that performs that transition, transfer, identification, exclusion, aggregation, or substitution.**

Compactly, for source-distinct states `S_i` and `S_j`:

`S_i != S_j`

does not by itself imply disjointness, ordering, causation, or a mandatory transition.

A transition or identification must be rule-indexed:

`S_i --R--> S_j`

where `R` is supplied by the source regime or by a separately justified external model.

The cross-case analyses further show that the topology need not be linear. A source system may contain branches, substitutions, bypasses, later confirmations, exceptions, review loops, or multiple actors/functions. The safer general representation is therefore a source-indexed interface graph rather than one universal stage chain.

## 2. Cross-case results that survived

### 2.1 Identity is not inferred from downstream effect

LAW-001 shows that nonaction can receive a default, sanction, assent effect, quorum consequence, or another result without becoming an affirmative, negative, opposite, or invalid formed act by identity.

LAW-003 shows that a later attributed effect does not prove that ordinary authority originally existed.

LAW-004 shows that a final factual determination does not uniquely reconstruct one ordinary submitted-evidence path.

LAW-005 through LAW-010 repeatedly preserve the same directionality boundary across defect/effect, normativity, power/exercise, conflict/priority, irreversibility/safeguard, and responsibility/sanction/remedy.

Cross-case rule:

**downstream consequence does not by itself rewrite upstream identity.**

### 2.2 Positive and negative pre-filling are both unjustified without a rule

Earlier predefinition criticism could be read mainly as a prohibition on prematurely assigning a positive downstream value.

The law cases require a stronger two-sided rule.

Do not predefine:

- `nonaction -> opposition`;
- `submitted -> admissible`;
- `authority defect -> permanent no effect`;
- `rule not prohibiting -> globally permitted`;
- `right possessed -> successfully exercised`;
- `rule difference -> unresolved contradiction`;
- `responsibility established -> one required sanction`.

But also do not predefine the opposite negatives:

- `no ordinary authority -> no possible source-defined attribution`;
- `no ordinary party-submitted evidence -> fact cannot be procedurally established`;
- `one proof route failed -> opposite fact proved`;
- `one conflict-resolution route absent -> no other source route exists`.

Cross-case rule:

**do not pre-fill either a positive or negative downstream status before the governing source route is identified.**

### 2.3 Non-identity is weaker than disjointness

LAW-010 makes this explicit, but the rule applies across the sequence:

`A != B`

does not imply

`A intersection B = empty`.

Roles, functions, sanctions, remedies, prevention, evidentiary purposes, institutional actors and legal effects may overlap, combine, or be allocated many-to-many while remaining non-identical.

DSD Analysis should therefore use non-collapse as the default claim, not mutual exclusion unless the source independently supports exclusion.

### 2.4 Actor identity does not determine function, role, authority, or effect

LAW-002 rejects one-office-per-function modeling.

LAW-003 rejects actor/office title as a complete authority/effect state.

Together they establish the source-sensitive mapping:

`actors/institutions <-> functions/roles`

as potentially many-to-many.

Any exclusivity claim must come from the source rules.

### 2.5 Source rules, not DSD, generate legal transitions

Across the sequence, legal transitions are externally supplied:

- nonaction defaults and sanctions;
- burdens and proof thresholds;
- apparent authority and ratification;
- evidence exclusion, judicial notice, admissions and court-initiated evidence;
- invalidity, defeasibility, recognition and enforcement;
- permissions, prohibitions, exceptions and derogations;
- exercise, enforcement and effect of rights or powers;
- harmonization, priority and invalidity in rule conflict;
- provisional safeguards and finality gates;
- sanction, reparation, rehabilitation and non-repetition.

DSD may preserve the before/after states and the interface carrying the rule, but it does not create the legal validity, moral justification, empirical probability, or institutional authority of the transition.

### 2.6 No universal scalar aggregation follows from evaluative language

The sequence repeatedly contains terms that invite numerical collapse: probative value, severity, proportionality, social cost, grief, legitimacy, irreversibility, safeguard strength, punishment and restoration.

No law case supplied a common codomain plus measurement rule plus aggregation operator sufficient to identify those concepts with Channel-Indexed Static Aggregation.

Therefore:

`legal/social evaluation != DSD analytic weight`

without a separately justified bridge.

### 2.7 Source topology is graph-like, not universally linear

LAW-001 allows explicit defaults and alternative nonaction consequences.

LAW-002 distributes functions across actors and stages.

LAW-003 contains ordinary authority, apparent authority, ultra vires attribution and later ratification/confirmation routes.

LAW-004 contains party evidence, court-initiated evidence, judicial notice, admission, notoriety and exclusion routes.

LAW-005 through LAW-010 add review, exceptions, priority, remedies and later status changes.

The common lesson is:

**stage distinction does not imply one mandatory real-world stage order.**

When source structure branches or loops, the DSD application must preserve that topology rather than repairing it into a chain.

## 3. Strong application assumptions rejected by external law

The legal campaign did not merely find correspondences. It forced DSD-style application assumptions to shrink.

### LAW-001

Rejected:

- nonaction never has consequences;
- silence can never receive assent effect;
- presumption of innocence means every subsidiary burden always lies on prosecution.

### LAW-002

Rejected:

- fixed seven-office `W + S + P + D + J + R_E + R_L` taxonomy;
- prosecutor office = guilt-only function;
- court = passive evidence consumer;
- victim/source = prosecution component;
- one actor/institution = one procedural function.

### LAW-003

Rejected:

- binary `authority yes/no -> effect yes/no`;
- authority for one purpose = authority for all related acts;
- unauthorized act = non-event;
- ultra vires conduct = necessarily unattributable;
- later confirmation proves original authority.

### LAW-004

Rejected:

- universal five-stage evidence pipeline;
- party-submission-only path to factual determination;
- relevance = actual use;
- admissibility = proof;
- inadmissibility = source-item absence;
- unlawful acquisition = one universal exclusion result.

### LAW-005

Rejected:

- one universal `defect -> invalidity/no effect` map.

### LAW-006

Rejected:

- one fixed global deontic value generated from one rule's applicability/failure;
- non-prohibition as automatic universal permission.

### LAW-007

Rejected:

- recognized right/power = present exercisability = exercise = effect.

### LAW-008

Rejected:

- rule difference = unresolved contradiction;
- conflict = automatic priority/invalidity/deletion under one universal algorithm.

### LAW-009

Rejected:

- `greater irreversibility -> universally stronger safeguards` as a monotone theorem.

### LAW-010

Rejected:

- one universal doctrinal taxonomy requiring responsibility, punishment, restoration, compensation, rehabilitation and prevention always to be separately instantiated;
- one-dimensional `justice done` or `social cost` output.

These negative results are part of the evidence for analysis/design separation: source systems were allowed to defeat the stronger application model.

## 4. What DSD contributed to the legal analyses

The strongest contribution was not derivation of legal rules.

It was a reusable audit discipline for asking:

1. Which source states are being silently identified?
2. Which downstream state has been pre-filled before the source transition rule is identified?
3. Is a missing state being confused with zero, absence, invalidity, acquittal, inapplicability or another source concept?
4. Is an actor or office name being used as a substitute for function, role or authority analysis?
5. Is a final effect being used to back-infer the identity of the input or earlier state?
6. Is a source-specific branch being rewritten as a universal linear stage?
7. Is evaluative language being converted into a scalar merely because a mathematical aggregation tool exists?
8. Is the analyst importing external ground truth or a desired normative conclusion into a source regime that does not possess it?

This is best characterized as typed non-totalization and interface auditing.

## 5. What law contributed back to DSD

The legal witnesses materially sharpened the DSD application discipline.

They established that:

- roles must not be overloaded with authority, function and effect;
- actor/function mapping can be many-to-many;
- a source state can have alternative downstream routes;
- lack of one ordinary route does not prove the absence of all routes;
- later effect does not rewrite original historical status unless a source rule expressly supplies a retroactive legal effect;
- source distinctions may be graph-structured rather than stage-linear;
- non-identity does not require mutual exclusion;
- legal uncertainty is not automatically DSD `undefined`;
- legal inadmissibility or ineffectiveness is not automatically DSD absence;
- the existence of DSD Dynamics does not authorize the analyst to invent an external transition law;
- the existence of Static Aggregation does not authorize scalarization of law, emotion or social cost.

Thus the external field did not merely confirm DSD; it constrained how DSD may be used.

## 6. Refined predefinition-justification rule

The law campaign supports the following refinement:

**A prior definition is not unjustified merely because it is prior. It becomes analytically suspect when it silently excludes source-permitted alternatives, promotes or demotes a state without the rule that performs that change, fills a positive or negative downstream value before the relevant route is established, or back-infers an upstream identity from a later effect.**

Operational form:

1. Preserve the source state as typed.
2. Preserve the applicable regime, role, purpose, time and actor/function allocation.
3. Identify the source relation rule `R`.
4. Apply only the transition, transfer, identification, exclusion, substitution or aggregation that `R` actually licenses.
5. Leave all other statuses unpromoted rather than filling them from intuition.
6. Do not infer that a missing ordinary route means all alternative routes are absent.
7. Do not use a later effect to rewrite earlier identity unless the source expressly creates a legal retroactivity rule, and even then distinguish legal retroactive effect from historical state.

## 7. Analysis/design separation rule

Analysis and design are separate operations.

### Analysis

- Do not aim for agreement between DSD and the external field.
- Do not aim for contradiction between them.
- Preserve the external field's own terminology, distinctions, exceptions and counterexamples.
- Preserve a genuine contradiction if one remains after type, role, regime and interface alignment.
- Treat `no meaningful DSD mapping` as a legitimate result.

### Design

- Do not create an unnecessary DSD concept merely to compete with a source concept that already performs the needed work.
- Do not deliberately design DSD to conflict with a functioning external structure without an independent structural reason.
- Do not redesign DSD after the fact merely to force agreement with every source system.
- When external counterpressure defeats an application assumption rather than a DSD axiom, narrow the application assumption and preserve the negative result.

Compact rule:

**analysis does not preselect agreement or conflict; design avoids gratuitous conflict; neither may erase an independently found contradiction for convenience.**

## 8. DSD layer limits after the law campaign

### Formation

Useful for typed status, role-sensitive identity, partiality, and non-totalization.

Not a legal admissibility rule, proof rule, authority rule, validity rule, or moral decision procedure.

### Axis-Property

Not triggered merely by words such as hierarchy, capacity, power, severity or authority.

A realized-axis interpretation must be independently supplied.

### Static Aggregation

Not a default model of evidence weight, sentencing, balancing, social cost, emotion, legitimacy, irreversibility or justice.

A common codomain and aggregation rule must be independently justified.

### Dynamics

May record source-defined temporal change but does not create the legal, empirical or normative transition rule.

## 9. Epistemic extension suggested but not yet established as a separate law result

The legal sequence, especially LAW-002 and LAW-004, motivates a further distinction:

`historical event state != surviving trace/record != current reconstruction != institutional factual determination`.

This should not yet be promoted to a new universal theorem merely from the present synthesis.

A future investigation can test investigation/inquiry as a multi-source reconstruction problem, including:

- imperfect or absent perception at the time of the event;
- memory loss and distortion;
- dependence among witness sources;
- physical traces that support several causal histories;
- competition among candidate reconstructions;
- institutional proof thresholds that do not claim omniscient access to the historical event.

Any ideal complete description should be treated only as a comparison ideal, not as an analyst-accessible ground truth in real legal cases.

## 10. Final DSD risk classification after LAW-001 through LAW-010

### Direct axiom contradiction found

None established by the completed law analyses after explicit typing and bridge correction.

### High-risk application errors

- linearizing a source graph;
- overloading role tags;
- identifying source concepts with DSD absence/undefined/zero without a bridge;
- treating DSD as the generator of source transition rules;
- treating Dynamics as a legal/empirical causal law;
- invoking Axis-Property from metaphorical hierarchy/power language;
- scalarizing legal/social values through Static Aggregation without measurement rules;
- treating non-identity as mutual exclusion;
- using an ideal or toy-model ground truth as if real observers or institutions possess it.

### Main current characterization

**DSD is strongest here as a tool for disciplined decomposition, non-identification, interface tracking and predefinition audit. Its value depends heavily on application discipline. The law campaign gives no basis for claiming that DSD determines legal truth, legal validity, moral correctness, or institutional outcomes.**
