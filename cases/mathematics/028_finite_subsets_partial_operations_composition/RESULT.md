## Required final structure

### 1. Standard mathematical finding

State the source theorem, definition, or counterexample without DSD terminology.

### 2. DSD finding

State the exact Formation / Axis-property / Static-aggregation structure being compared.

### 3. Correspondence verdict

Choose exactly one primary classification:

- direct correspondence,
- partial correspondence,
- correspondence after explicit additional encoding,
- non-correspondence.

### 4. Counterexample boundary

Record the smallest failure case and the assumption that fails.

### 5. DSD consequence

Separate:

- support for an existing DSD distinction,
- newly discovered restriction on DSD application,
- genuine contradiction with a DSD axiom or theorem,
- no material consequence.

### 6. Cross-domain synthesis note

Only after the case is complete, record whether the result repeats a structural distinction already found in logic, law, language, administration, or another independent domain. Repetition is evidence of recurrence, not proof by analogy.
