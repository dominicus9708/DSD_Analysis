# LAW-014 Model — Source-Defined Collective Decision Formation

## 1. Do not identify individual and collective states

A source-faithful record may require:

`(member, eligibility, participation, vote, abstention/nonparticipation, quorum, issue_category, threshold, weighting, blocking_or_veto_rule, body_result, second_body_result, presentment_or_confirmation, final_institutional_act, attribution, time, regime)`.

Not every source uses every coordinate.

## 2. Core distinctions

`individual vote != body decision`.

`body decision != final institutional act`.

`quorum != approval threshold`.

`abstention != nonparticipation`.

`abstention != NO vote identity`, even where a source rule gives abstention the same downstream counting effect as a NO for one calculation.

`institutional act != every member's personal endorsement`.

`same vote profile under different rule != necessarily same collective result`.

## 3. Collective-decision graph

Use a source-indexed graph:

`G_C = (V_C, E_R)`.

Edges may include participation eligibility, quorum formation, vote counting, weighted/double-majority calculation, veto/blocking relation, inter-body concurrence, presentment, approval, veto override and institutional attribution.

No universal simple-sum edge is assumed.

## 4. Aggregation caution

A voting system may itself define a numerical or Boolean aggregation operator. That source-defined operator can be represented mathematically.

But:

`source voting aggregation != DSD Static Aggregation by identity`.

And the existence of a count/percentage does not justify a universal social-choice or DSD scalar model beyond the source rule.
