## Research question

Determine the exact algebraic relation between finite DSD channel-family composition and standard structures on finite subsets, partial operations, additive codomains, and structure-preserving maps.

## Primary questions

1. What algebraic structure is naturally carried by `P_fin(C_L)` under union?
2. Under what conditions does `Comp(F) = sum_{c in F} T(c)` preserve a union-like composition?
3. Does overlap of finite subsets obstruct a homomorphism law through double counting?
4. Which alternative source structure, if any, makes `Comp` a genuine homomorphism without loss of DSD channel identity?
5. How should `F1 != F2` with `Comp(F1) = Comp(F2)` be classified relative to kernels, quotients, invariants, and information loss?
6. How do Formation forward maps, embeddings, and strict equivalence compare in strength with standard homomorphisms and isomorphisms?

## Working hypotheses to attack, not assume

- H1: `Comp` is a monoid homomorphism from `(P_fin(C_L), union)` to `(W_L, +)`.
- H2: H1 fails in general but becomes valid on disjoint finite families.
- H3: a multiset or free-commutative-monoid encoding yields a stronger algebraic correspondence than plain finite-set union.
- H4: equal aggregate output is strictly weaker than structural equality and strict descriptive equivalence.

Each hypothesis must be independently tested by proof or counterexample.
