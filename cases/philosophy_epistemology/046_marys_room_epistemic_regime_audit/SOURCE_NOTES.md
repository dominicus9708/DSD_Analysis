## Primary-source reconstruction

Jackson's mature 1986 formulation stipulates that pre-release Mary knows all physical facts about other people and their environment, including completed physics, chemistry, neurophysiology, causal and relational facts, and functional roles.

After release and first color experience, Jackson claims Mary learns something about the experiences of others. The intended argument is not merely that Mary's own post-release physical state is new. Jackson explicitly separates that trivial temporal change from the target claim about facts concerning other people's already existing experiences.

A compact reconstruction is:

1. Before release Mary knows every physical fact about other people.
2. After release Mary gains knowledge concerning what their color experiences are like.
3. Therefore her pre-release knowledge was incomplete.
4. Since every physical fact was already known, the newly known fact is not physical.
5. Therefore physicalism is false or incomplete.

Jackson also explicitly addresses the ability reply. He grants that Mary acquires new abilities such as imagining and remembering red experiences, but argues that this is not all she gains. For PHIL-003 Argument 1 the DSD audit therefore does not rely on an ability-only response.

## Argument 1 — external debate families

The mature literature distinguishes at least the following families:

- Ability Hypothesis: Lewis / Nemirow — new know-how or abilities rather than new propositional facts.
- Acquaintance Hypothesis: direct acquaintance rather than a new fact.
- New Knowledge / Old Fact: Mary may acquire genuinely new propositional knowledge under a new phenomenal concept or representation while the truthmaker/fact was already physically known under another representation.
- Phenomenal-concept / new-mode-of-presentation approaches.
- Challenges to the complete-physical-knowledge premise and to the inference from epistemic non-deducibility to metaphysical nonphysicality.

Argument 1 converges most strongly with the New Knowledge / Old Fact family, not merely with the Ability Hypothesis.

## Argument 2 — dynamic extension scope

Argument 2 is not presented as Jackson's own second argument and does not replace Argument 1.

It asks a separate question that becomes visible when `all physical facts` is time-indexed in an evolving world:

`complete physical knowledge at t0 !=> complete physical knowledge at every later t`.

The DSD dynamics source justifies the formal discipline of treating dynamic states as explicit time-indexed slices rather than silently promoting a statement about one slice to every later slice.

The dynamics source also supports only a **conditional** propagation strengthening. A finite structural-information front requires explicit localization, metric time, a supplied evolution/constitutive model, and a local support-faithful representation. Therefore PHIL-003 does not identify this structural bound with the speed of light, biological perception latency, or any empirical cognitive-information speed.

Argument 2 is currently a project-constructed dynamic extension. No dedicated historical-priority search has yet been performed for time-indexed/diachronic variants of Mary's Room, so historical novelty is not claimed.

## DSD source constraints used by both arguments

1. Formation is typed and domain-specific applications require additional interpretation maps.
2. A property name alone has no mathematical content; typed profile, carrier, domain, assignment, and coherence data must be supplied.
3. Equal reduced output does not reconstruct complete underlying structure without an additional reconstruction/injectivity theorem.
4. Static aggregation is evaluated on a fixed descriptive regime/static slice and does not itself provide temporal update or propagation rules.
5. Structural dynamics uses time-indexed admissible states and does not silently turn one static slice into an entire trajectory.
6. Finite propagation is conditional on additional dynamic/localization/representation assumptions.

No primitive DSD symbol is identified with consciousness, qualia, knowledge, physicalism, human cognition, or empirical signal propagation. PHIL-003 uses application-level epistemic and temporal encodings only.
