For each prediction, assign one:

- `C1` established external match;
- `C2` established family, DSD gives finer/different formalization;
- `C3` DSD missed a major external objection or predicted the wrong target;
- `C4` DSD issue survives but no materially equivalent precedent was located in the dedicated search;
- `N/A` prediction did not engage the source at the correct level.

## G. Novelty escalation for C4 only

A C4 result is only a lead. Before a novelty claim:

1. broaden database and keyword search;
2. search neighboring terminology, not only DSD wording;
3. search older literature and citations of the source argument;
4. test the strongest likely rebuttal;
5. verify that the result is not a disguised version of an established objection family.
