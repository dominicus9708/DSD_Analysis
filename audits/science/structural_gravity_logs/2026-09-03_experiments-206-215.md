# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 206–215

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: conditional field-energy/bookkeeping audit

## Experiment 206 — Energy bridge is additional structure

A progression-field equation alone does not determine a physical energy functional. Such a functional is introduced only as a downstream physical specialization.

## Experiment 207 — Progression-field stiffness

For dimensionless \(\psi\), take the static field energy

\[
E_\psi
=
\frac{\mathcal T_\psi}{2}
\int |\nabla\psi|^2d^3x.
\]

Dimensional consistency gives

\[
[\mathcal T_\psi]
=
\frac{\text{energy}}{\text{length}}
=
\text{force}.
\]

This is a concrete field-gradient stiffness, not yet identified with any prior axis-property label.

## Experiment 208 — Same-metric matter coupling

If the same progression geometry fixes the matter source coupling so that

\[
\mathcal T_\psi\nabla^2\psi
=
c_*^2\rho,
\]

then comparison with

\[
\nabla^2\psi
=
4\pi\Lambda_t\rho
\]

gives

\[
\Lambda_t
=
\frac{c_*^2}{4\pi\mathcal T_\psi}.
\]

## Experiment 209 — Location of the missing anchor

The previously abstract universal tension/force anchor is now localized as the absolute normalization \(\mathcal T_\psi\) of the progression-field gradient term. Under this specialization, the missing \(L/M\) scale is equivalent to the missing absolute field stiffness.

## Experiment 210 — Universal self-mass coupling

Let the static field energy density be

\[
u_\psi
=
\frac{\mathcal T_\psi}{2}|\nabla\psi|^2
\]

and conditionally identify its physical mass density by

\[
\rho_\psi
=
\frac{u_\psi}{c_*^2}.
\]

If this mass sources progression geometry with the same universal source rule,

\[
\nabla^2\psi
=
\frac{c_*^2}{\mathcal T_\psi}
(\rho+\rho_\psi)
=
4\pi\Lambda_t\rho
+
\frac12|\nabla\psi|^2.
\]

## Experiment 211 — Dimensionless nonlinearity fixed

Under these assumptions, the earlier arbitrary self-source coefficient becomes

\[
\gamma=\frac12,
\]

independent of \(\mathcal T_\psi\). Self-consistent bookkeeping can therefore fix a dimensionless nonlinear coefficient while leaving the absolute stiffness/coupling undetermined.

## Experiment 212 — Absolute no-go survives

\(\mathcal T_\psi\) remains a free dimensionful coefficient. Hence

\[
\Lambda_t
=
\frac{c_*^2}{4\pi\mathcal T_\psi}
\]

is not numerically recovered by self-coupling consistency alone.

## Experiment 213 — Bounded-source regularization

A point-source profile

\[
\psi\sim\frac{\Lambda_tM}{r}
\]

gives

\[
\int |\nabla\psi|^2d^3x
\sim
\int_0\frac{dr}{r^2},
\]

which diverges at the origin. A finite bounded source with regular central density has

\[
\nabla\psi=O(r)
\]

near the center and can make the self-field integral finite.

Thus boundedness has a natural regularization role distinct from quantizing length or mass.

## Experiment 214 — Weak-field parameter reappears physically

The self-field mass correction scales relative to matter mass as

\[
\frac{M_\psi}{M}
\sim
\Lambda_t\frac{M}{R}
\]

up to dimensionless profile factors. Therefore

\[
\epsilon_S
=
|\Lambda_t|\frac{M}{R}
\]

controls the relative size of self/backreaction in this specialization.

## Experiment 215 — Verdict

- boundedness as literal mass/length quantization: REJECTED;
- boundedness as finite-source self-field regularizer: CONDITIONAL YES;
- dimensionless self-coupling fixed by universal energy bookkeeping: CONDITIONAL YES;
- absolute field stiffness \(\mathcal T_\psi\): OPEN;
- absolute \(\Lambda_t\): OPEN.

Rather than searching arbitrary \(L/M\) combinations, the structural-gravity branch can ask whether DSD axis/formation dynamics independently derive the progression-field stiffness \(\mathcal T_\psi\) without importing the desired coupling.
