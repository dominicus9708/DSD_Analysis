# LAW-003 Scope Reclassification

Status: active scope note; original RESULT preserved.

## Universal structural claim

The primary candidate is:

`underlying person/entity != operative role != authority != act != attributed effect`.

The same person or entity may act under multiple capacities, and an underlying relationship does not automatically totalize into every authority, every act, or every downstream attribution.

## Jurisdictional witness

The Korean Civil Act and Supreme Court materials used in LAW-003 are a jurisdictional witness showing one detailed implementation through agency, mandate, representation, apparent-agency rules, authority scope, and ratification.

Those doctrines are not treated as the source of the universal structural claim and are not presumed to exist with identical categories in other jurisdictions.

## Generalization status

`universal candidate under active falsification`.

The Korean witness demonstrates that a mature legal system can require the non-collapse, but cross-jurisdictional corroboration or counterexamples are still needed before any stronger universality claim.

## DSD status

The original type-audit result is preserved: the application carrier should be a role/context-sensitive legal act-instance rather than the natural person alone. Formation role identity is sufficient for the main comparison; Axis-Property remains unnecessary without realized-axis semantics.
