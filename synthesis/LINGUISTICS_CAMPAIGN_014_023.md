## First-pass findings

### Status and applicability

The campaign repeatedly requires source-theory distinctions among states such as:

`unknown != unavailable != undefined != defined false/zero != graded value != multiple candidates unresolved`.

These labels are not merged unless the linguistic theory being modeled supplies a rule that identifies them.

### Interpretation and bridge rules

Semantic typing, reference, accommodation, pragmatic inference, disambiguation, discourse update, felicity conditions, institutional licensing, relation resolution, and information-structure interpretation remain explicit source-theory operations.

DSD can record the input/output stages and their typing, but it does not manufacture the linguistic bridge rule.

### No retrospective completion

A later successful state does not automatically reconstruct the earlier state that preceded it. Examples include later accommodation, later disambiguation, or later discourse accessibility. Each reverse inference needs its own source-supported condition.

### Directional relations

When a linguistic or institutional-language relation is ordered, its direction and typed argument roles must be preserved. Relation structure is not safely reduced to an unordered set of participants or a collection of unary labels without justification.

### Reduction and information loss

A reduced output can be useful without being a complete representation of the source expression or information structure. Before reconstructing source structure from a summary, the discarded distinctions must be identified.

## DSD layer usage

### Formation Axiom System

Principal DSD layer for the campaign. It is used for typed carriers, staged formation, partial assignment, role identity, and distinctions among unavailable/undefined/defined states.

It is not treated as a grammar, lexical semantics, reference theory, truth theory, pragmatic theory, or discourse theory.

### Axis Property Axiom System

Used selectively where the source phenomenon genuinely requires relation-sensitive properties over formed/tagged entities, especially ordered role or authority relations.

It is not required for every linguistic case.

### Channel-Indexed Static Aggregation

Used only where reduced representation or aggregation raises an explicit information-loss question. Equal reduced outputs are not assumed to reconstruct the full linguistic source structure.

### Structural Reorganization Dynamics

Not identified with linguistic dynamic semantics. Discourse update remains governed by explicit linguistic update rules unless a separate reason justifies use of the physical/mathematical dynamics framework.

## Scope of the result

The campaign supports DSD as a preservation/staging aid across the selected linguistic phenomena when source-theory distinctions are retained.

It does **not** establish:

- a DSD grammar of natural language;
- a unique DSD semantics of reference, presupposition, implicature, ambiguity, vagueness, focus, or discourse;
- a derivation of speech-act or institutional felicity conditions from DSD;
- empirical validation of linguistic predictions;
- proof of DSD axioms from linguistic agreement.
