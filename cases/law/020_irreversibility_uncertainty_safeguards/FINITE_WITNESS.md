# LAW-009 Finite Witness — Irreparability and Safeguard Non-Totalization

## 1. Purpose

Construct a small witness family showing that neither

`IRREVERSIBLE = YES/NO`

nor a single monotone safeguard level preserves the selected legal structures.

## 2. Witness states

Let the witness family contain six typed cases.

### A. Capital charge with ordinary-trial stage still incomplete

- capital exposure: yes
- guilt finally established: no
- appeal/review complete: no
- execution authorized: no
- safeguards: defence preparation, counsel, fair-trial guarantees, later review gates

### B. Death sentence after final judgment but review/clemency route still pending

- sentence exists: yes
- execution event: not yet authorized under the selected safeguard architecture
- review/pardon status remains legally relevant

This defeats `sentence exists -> execution now`.

### C. ECHR complaint with imminent irreparable harm risk

- merits finally decided: no
- irreparable-harm risk: source-recognized
- interim preservation measure: potentially available under Rule 39

This shows a pre-final safeguard designed to preserve the practical value of later adjudication.

### D. ECHR complaint with asserted harm but Rule 39 threshold not met

- underlying complaint may still exist
- interim measure: unavailable

This defeats `irreparable allegation -> automatic interim measure` and shows that the source requires additional conditions and rigorous examination.

### E. ICJ dispute with risk of irreparable prejudice and urgency

- final merits judgment: no
- provisional measure: potentially available
- function: preserve claimed rights against irreparable prejudice before final decision

### F. ICJ dispute where irreparable prejudice condition may be discussed but urgency is not established

- final merits judgment: no
- provisional measure: not justified under the source test

This defeats `irreparability alone -> provisional measure`.

## 3. Compression failure

A binary carrier

`Irrev(c) in {0,1}`

cannot distinguish:

- irreversible consequence already imposed;
- risk of future irreparable harm;
- historically irreversible event but restorable consequence;
- adjudicatively irreversible transition before final review;
- source-recognized irreparable risk with urgency;
- source-recognized risk without another required condition.

Likewise a universal safeguard scalar

`S(c) in {LOW,HIGH}`

cannot preserve the different source mechanisms:

- heightened proof/fair-trial protection;
- mandatory appeal/review;
- clemency/pardon opportunity;
- stay or interim measure;
- jurisdiction-specific urgency threshold;
- no interim protection despite unresolved merits.

## 4. Minimal conclusion

The witness family supports only the narrower structure:

**irreparability can be a legally relevant trigger or intensifier for ex ante preservation and heightened safeguards, but the existence, form and strength of the safeguard remain source-rule indexed and cannot be inferred from irreparability alone.**
