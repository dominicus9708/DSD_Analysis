# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 110–115

Date: 2026-09-03

## Experiment 110 — calibrated physical mass does not remove the dimensional no-go

Assume physical source mass \(M\) is already absolutely calibrated by a non-gravitational procedure. Let the remaining independent dimensional source data be \(M,L\), one or more velocity scales, and dimensionless/typed structural descriptors \(\Pi\).

Any candidate with dimensions \(L/M\) built from these data must contain the source mass and length in the combination

\[
\Lambda(S)=\frac{L}{M}F(\Pi,\text{dimensionless velocity ratios},\ldots).
\]

Thus removing the mass-calibration rescaling freedom does not create a source-independent mass–geometry conversion.

**Verdict:** absolute-normalization non-identifiability survives even with calibrated \(M\), unless an additional universal dimensionful anchor is present.

## Experiment 111 — dimensionful critical amplitude cannot be produced by a scale-free relation alone

A universal relation

\[
M_c(L)=\mu_*L
\]

contains a dimensional amplitude

\[
[\mu_*]=M/L.
\]

A dimensionally homogeneous transition law cannot compare the mass and length sectors in this form without a coefficient or reference quantity carrying the required cross-dimension. Deriving exponent 1 therefore does not derive \(\mu_*\).

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED.

## Experiment 112 — homothety compensation test

For a 3D fixed-shape/fixed-density homothetic family,

\[
L\to\alpha L,\qquad M\to\alpha^3M,
\]

and unchanged dimensionless structure \(\Pi\),

\[
\Lambda(S)=\frac{L}{M}F(\Pi)
\]

transforms as

\[
\Lambda\to\alpha^{-2}\Lambda.
\]

A universal \(\Lambda_*\) would require \(F\) to acquire compensating scale dependence \(F\sim\alpha^2\). Defining such absolute dependence requires an additional reference scale, so the compensation mechanism itself reintroduces a dimensionful anchor.

**Verdict:** scale-invariant dimensionless structure cannot compensate source-specific \(L/M\) scaling — CONFIRMED.

## Experiment 113 — dimensional completion by external anchors

If an independently normalized universal physical scale is supplied, the missing dimension can be completed. Examples:

- universal mass \(m_*\) with \(\hbar,c\):
  \[
  \Lambda_*\sim\frac{\hbar}{m_*^2c};
  \]
- universal tension/force scale \(\mathcal T_*\):
  \[
  \Lambda_*\sim\frac{c^2}{\mathcal T_*};
  \]
- impedance-like \(Z_*[M/T]\):
  \[
  \Lambda_*\sim\frac{c}{Z_*}.
  \]

These are dimensional-completion examples, not DSD-internal derivations.

## Experiment 114 — bounded spectrum plus an external action constant

If

\[
k_n\sim L^{-1},\qquad \omega_n\sim c/L,
\]

then adding an external action constant such as \(\hbar\) gives

\[
m_n\sim\frac{\hbar}{cL},
\]

which remains source-size dependent. Spectral discreteness plus an action constant does not generate a source-independent \(L/M\) anchor unless a universal \(L\) is separately selected.

**Verdict:** REJECTED as a universal-anchor route by itself.

## Experiment 115 — consolidated conditional theorem candidate

Under the ingredient set:

1. source-specific calibrated \(M,L\);
2. dimensionless/typed structural descriptors;
3. velocity scales only;
4. bounded-state/spectral data generated from the source scale;
5. no independently normalized universal dimensionful anchor;
6. no genuine scale-generating anomaly/transmutation mechanism;

DSD may determine branch structure, critical exponents, normalized ratios, propagation bounds, and source-detail corrections, but it cannot identify a unique source-independent coefficient with dimensions \(L/M\).

Equivalently, the static progression-geometry coupling

\[
K_*=c_{\rm kin}^2\Lambda_*
\]

cannot be assigned a unique absolute value from this ingredient set alone.

**Verdict:** CONDITIONAL NO-GO THEOREM CANDIDATE — strongly supported by Experiments 39–115.
