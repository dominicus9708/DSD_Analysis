## Experiment 359 — Second-order expansion of the axis-informed threshold operator

Use the static axis metric branch

\[
h_A=e^{\beta_A\mathcal A},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}\mathcal A=0,
\]

and the exact transformed self-field equation

\[
\Delta_{h_A}U+\frac{\rho}{2\mu_0}U=0.
\]

Since

\[
h_A^{ij}=\left(e^{-\beta_A\mathcal A}\right)^{ij}
=\delta^{ij}-\beta_A\mathcal A^{ij}
+\frac{\beta_A^2}{2}(\mathcal A^2)^{ij}+O(\beta_A^3),
\]

the positive elliptic operator

\[
L_{\beta_A}:=-\partial_i(h_A^{ij}\partial_j)
\]

has the expansion

\[
L_{\beta_A}=L_0+\beta_A L_1+\beta_A^2L_2+O(\beta_A^3),
\]

with

\[
L_0=-\Delta,
\qquad
L_1=\partial_i(\mathcal A^{ij}\partial_j),
\qquad
L_2=-\frac12\partial_i((\mathcal A^2)^{ij}\partial_j).
\]

For a fixed normalized density shape \(d\), the critical problem is the generalized ground-state eigenvalue problem

\[
L_{\beta_A}u=\lambda(\beta_A)d\,u.
\]

The collapse compactness remains

\[
\epsilon_c(\beta_A)=\frac23\lambda(\beta_A).
\]

## Experiment 360 — First-order selection rule

For a spherical baseline source, the ground state \(u_0=u_0(r)\) is an \(l=0\) mode. Take a pure axis quadrupole

\[
\mathcal A^{ij}(x)=a(r)S^{ij},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}S=0.
\]

Then

\[
\langle u_0,L_1u_0\rangle
=-\int \mathcal A^{ij}\partial_i u_0\partial_j u_0\,d^3x=0
\]

by the spherical average of \(S_{ij}n^in^j\). Hence

\[
\boxed{\lambda_1=0},
\qquad
\boxed{\epsilon_c'(0)=0}.
\]

Moreover,

\[
L_1u_0
\propto
\left[\frac{d}{dr}(a u_0')-\frac{a u_0'}r\right]
S_{ij}n^in^j,
\]

so the first correction mixes the ground state only with the \(l=2\) sector in this fixed-orientation radial-envelope specialization.

## Experiment 361 — General second-order threshold formula

Normalize the unperturbed eigenfunctions in the weighted product \(\langle u_m,d u_n\rangle=\delta_{mn}\). Since the first-order shift vanishes, standard simple-eigenvalue perturbation gives

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_2
=
\langle u_0,L_2u_0\rangle
-
\sum_{n\neq0}
\frac{|\langle u_n,L_1u_0\rangle|^2}
{\lambda_n-\lambda_0}
}.
\]

Equivalently, with the reduced resolvent,

\[
\lambda_2
=
\frac12\int \nabla u_0\cdot\mathcal A^2\nabla u_0\,d^3x
-
\langle L_1u_0,(L_0-\lambda_0d)^{-1}_{\perp}L_1u_0\rangle.
\]

The two terms have opposite signs:

- the direct metric-exponential term is nonnegative;
- mode relaxation/mixing lowers the eigenvalue.

Therefore the sign of the second-order threshold shift is **not fixed by tracelessness and spherical symmetry alone**.

## Experiment 362 — Fully admissible axis-quadrupole toy

Choose the maximal rank-one-compatible traceless axis tensor

\[
S=\operatorname{diag}(-1/3,-1/3,2/3),
\]

and radial envelope

\[
a(s)=1-s^2,
\qquad 0\le s\le1.
\]

Then

\[
Q(s)=\frac13I+a(s)S
\]

is positive semidefinite with \(\operatorname{tr}Q=1\) throughout the source: at the center it is the rank-one state \(\operatorname{diag}(0,0,1)\), and at the boundary it returns to isotropy.

For the uniform critical sphere \(\lambda_0=\pi^2/4\), the \(l=2\) radial correction was solved numerically with regularity at the center and the exterior decaying-multipole boundary condition

\[
v'(1)+3v(1)=0.
\]

The second-order coefficient is

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon_c(\beta_A)
=\frac{\pi^2}{6}
+4.5506920089\times10^{-4}\,\beta_A^2
+O(\beta_A^3)
}.
\]

For this admissible toy the shift is positive: weak axis anisotropy raises the critical compactness and is therefore locally stabilizing.

The coefficient is normalization- and profile-dependent and is **not** a universal DSD number.

## Experiment 364 — Axis-only threshold-crossing criterion at fixed matter \(M,R\)

For fixed matter mass, radius, and density shape define

\[
\epsilon=\frac{M}{4\pi\mu_0R}
\]

and the instantaneous spectral margin

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{\rm spec}(t)
:=\epsilon_c[h_A(t),d]-\epsilon.
}
\]

Then

\[
\Delta_{\rm spec}>0
\]

marks a subcritical static branch, while

\[
\Delta_{\rm spec}=0
\]

is the spectral threshold.

Axis reorganization alone can move a fixed \(M,R,\rho\) source across the static threshold **if and only if** the path \(h_A(t)\) changes \(\epsilon_c\) across the fixed value \(\epsilon\).

Near isotropy,

\[
\epsilon_c(\beta_A)
=\epsilon_0+\epsilon_2\beta_A^2+\cdots.
\]

If \(\epsilon_2<0\), increasing anisotropy can drive a previously subcritical source toward criticality. If \(\epsilon_2>0\), weak anisotropy stabilizes and isotropization can instead lower the threshold.

## Experiment 366 — Exact instantaneous eigenvalue-rate criterion

Because \(\det h_A=1\), the spatial volume element is fixed in this branch. For a simple normalized ground state \(u_1(t)\), fixed matter density shape, and time-dependent axis metric,

\[
\boxed{
\dot\lambda_1(t)
=
\frac{\int \dot h_A^{ij}\partial_i u_1\partial_j u_1\,d^3x}
{\int d\,u_1^2\,d^3x}.
}
\]

Hence the instantaneous axis reorganization lowers the critical threshold exactly when the weighted metric-rate contraction is negative.

At the isotropic spherical state with a pure traceless quadrupole, this integral vanishes, reproducing the first-order selection rule.

## Experiment 368 — A dynamical action that exactly reproduces the static self-field branch

A consistent hyperbolic completion can be constructed in the transformed field \(U=e^{-\psi/2}\). Take, as a **downstream structural-gravity specialization**,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_U
=
2\mu_0\left[(\partial_tU)^2-c_*^2h_A^{ij}\partial_iU\partial_jU\right]
+c_*^2\rho U^2.
}
\]

Its Euler–Lagrange equation is

\[
\boxed{
\frac1{c_*^2}\partial_t^2U
-\Delta_{h_A}U
-\frac{\rho}{2\mu_0}U
=0.
}
\]

The static equation is exactly

\[
\Delta_{h_A}U+\frac{\rho}{2\mu_0}U=0,
\]

the branch used in Experiments 344–358.

In terms of \(\psi=-2\ln U\), the same action becomes

\[
\mathcal L_\psi
=
\frac{\mu_0}{2}e^{-\psi}
\left[(\partial_t\psi)^2-c_*^2|\nabla\psi|_{h_A}^2\right]
+c_*^2\rho e^{-\psi}.
\]

Its weak-field expansion recovers, up to an irrelevant constant,

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm weak}
\simeq
\frac{\mu_0}{2}
\left[(\partial_t\psi)^2-c_*^2|\nabla\psi|^2\right]
-c_*^2\rho\psi.
\]

Thus the nonlinear static branch and the earlier one-parameter weak progression-field action can be embedded in one explicit dynamic toy action.

### Status

This action is **not derived from the DSD axioms**. It is a consistency-preserving structural-gravity specialization.

## Experiment 370 — Spectral threshold becomes a dynamical instability threshold in the U-action branch

Define the instantaneous spatial operator

\[
\boxed{
H_A
:=-\Delta_{h_A}-\frac{\rho}{2\mu_0}.
}
\]

For time-independent \(h_A,\rho\), the dynamic field equation is

\[
\frac1{c_*^2}U_{tt}+H_AU=0.
\]

At the static threshold, the lowest spectral point reaches zero. Under the usual compact-source spectral assumptions, a supercritical source develops a negative mode

\[
H_A\phi=-\kappa^2\phi,
\qquad \kappa>0.
\]

The corresponding modal amplitude obeys

\[
\ddot q-c_*^2\kappa^2q=0,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{q\sim e^{\pm c_*\kappa t}}.
\]

Hence the static spectral crossing is a genuine **linear dynamical instability threshold** in this explicit U-action specialization.

## Experiment 373 — Adiabatic versus nonadiabatic crossing

The instantaneous static threshold describes dynamics faithfully only when axis reorganization is slow compared with the relevant field-adjustment/spectral timescales. Rapid projector reorientation can mix modes and must be solved as the full time-dependent hyperbolic problem.

Thus two regimes must remain distinct:

1. **adiabatic reorganization** — track the instantaneous lowest spectral mode and margin;
2. **nonadiabatic reorganization** — solve the coupled time-dependent axis/field system.

The static crossing result alone does not prove that a rapidly reorganizing source follows the quasistatic branch.
