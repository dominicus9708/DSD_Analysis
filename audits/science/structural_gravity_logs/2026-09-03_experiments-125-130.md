# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 125–130

Date: 2026-09-03

## Stage
Multi-cone reachability and causal-arrival audit.

## New result
For a multi-component structural-gravity system, the characteristic set may contain several cone families. The existence of a fastest cone does not by itself imply that the progression response \(\psi\) propagates on that cone. If \(\psi\) belongs to a slower block that is not dynamically reachable from the faster block, then the global structural-information bound may satisfy

\[
c_{\rm info}>c_{\rm info}^{(\psi)}.
\]

To identify the progression update speed with the outer characteristic cone, a reachable mode or coupling path must connect that cone to the exact \(\psi\)-distinguishability support.

For a static progression metric

\[
ds^2=-N(x)^2c_*^2dt^2+h_{ij}dx^idx^j,
\]

let \(K=\operatorname{supp}\Delta S\) be the changed source region. If the progression sector uses the same null cone, the earliest causal-arrival candidate is

\[
T_g(x,K)=\inf_{\gamma:K\to x}\int_\gamma\frac{d\ell_h}{Nc_*}.
\]

For \(N=1\) and flat \(h\), this reduces to \(\operatorname{dist}(x,K)/c_*\). A strict subcone gives a larger arrival time. An anisotropic cone generally requires a direction-dependent travel-time functional rather than this isotropic optical form.

For infinitesimal source changes, the background progression metric may be used for the linearized arrival cone. For a finite change that also modifies \(N\) or \(h\), the causal domain is solution-dependent and must be computed from the evolving \(g_{\rm prog}(t,x)\); one must not reuse a fixed preselected \(r/c\) delay.

A conditional common-cone closure requires:

- C1: a progression geometry \(g_{\rm prog}\);
- C2: \(\operatorname{Char}(P_\psi)=\operatorname{Null}(g_{\rm prog})\);
- C3: propagation-complete covector directions;
- C4: a local/support-faithful \(\psi\) representation;
- C5: a reachable sharp/saturating \(\psi\) mode on the cone boundary.

Under C1–C5,

\[
c_{\rm info}^{(\psi)}=c_{\rm kin}
\]

is a conditional closure. To identify the global \(c_{\rm info}\) with this value, every other structural sector must have no supercone relative to the progression cone.

## Verdict

- fastest system cone automatically sets \(\psi\) update speed: **REJECTED**
- mode reachability/coupling is required: **CONFIRMED**
- static optical-distance source-removal bound: **CONDITIONAL**
- fixed \(r/c\) delay under finite geometry-changing source removal: **REJECTED**
- C1–C5 imply sector-specific equality \(c_{\rm info}^{(\psi)}=c_{\rm kin}\): **CONDITIONAL**
- sector-specific equality automatically implies global equality: **REJECTED**

## Impact on the current logic chain
The progression-sector propagation problem can now be separated cleanly from the global DSD information bound. The physically relevant structural-gravity update speed is \(c_{\rm info}^{(\psi)}\), while the global \(c_{\rm info}\) is only equal under a no-supercone condition across all other sectors.
