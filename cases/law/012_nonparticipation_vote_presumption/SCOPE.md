# LAW-001 Scope Reclassification

Status: active scope note; original RESULT preserved.

## Universal structural claim

The primary cross-jurisdictional candidate is:

`nonaction != opposite action`

and, in responsibility-attribution settings:

`failure to establish responsibility != proof of the opposite factual proposition`.

Institutional eligibility, participation, formed action, valid input, and downstream result should remain distinct unless an explicit rule connects them.

## Jurisdictional / institutional witnesses

The parliamentary voting rules and presumption-of-innocence materials used in LAW-001 are concrete witnesses. The Republic of Korea constitutional rule is a domestic witness, not the source of the universal claim.

## Functional interpretation

The presumption segment is now read through the general responsibility-attribution framework: an attribution function may advance a responsibility claim, a defence function may challenge its formation conditions, and the judgment function applies the governing proof/decision rule.

## Generalization status

`universal candidate under active falsification`.

LAW-001 by itself does not establish universality. Its source materials support one or more implementations of the distinction.

## DSD status

The original finding is retained: DSD can preserve nonformation, defined choice, invalid/abstaining status, and downstream result without deriving the normative burden rule.
