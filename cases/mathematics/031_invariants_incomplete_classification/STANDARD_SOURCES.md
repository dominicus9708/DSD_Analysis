# Standard Mathematics Sources

## 1. Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Invariant

URL: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Invariant

Used for the standard definition: an invariant is a map constant on equivalence classes, and classification seeks complete systems of invariants that distinguish inequivalent objects.

## 2. Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Normal form

URL: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Normal_form

Used for the quotient/classification viewpoint and for canonical/normal-form examples. The entry identifies Jordan normal form as the normal form for matrix similarity and discusses complete classification data in standard linear settings.

## 3. Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Jordan matrix

URL: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Jordan_matrix

Used for the fact that, when the relevant polynomial splits, Jordan block data determine matrix similarity up to block order.

## 4. Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Law of inertia

URL: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Law_of_inertia

Used as a second positive example showing that a restricted signature can admit a finite complete classification datum.

## Source discipline

These references establish standard classification concepts. They are not treated as evidence for DSD-specific claims. DSD conclusions are checked independently against the project papers.
