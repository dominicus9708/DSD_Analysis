# LAW-005 / Global Case 016 — Procedural Formation, Validity, Invalidity, and Effect

Status: active analysis.

Primary purpose: reinterpretation.

Secondary purposes: coherence, predefinition audit, regime-boundary analysis, universal-claim falsification.

## 1. Universal structural candidate

This case does not begin from one jurisdiction's categories of `void`, `voidable`, `invalid`, or `unenforceable`.

The candidate claim is weaker and more general:

`attempt/candidate act != formed act != operative/effective act != later defect/remedy status != recognition/enforcement status`

unless an explicit rule identifies two or more of those stages in the selected regime.

The claim does not require every legal system to instantiate every stage.

## 2. Main question

When a legal or institutional act is attempted, formed, made effective, challenged, avoided, annulled, suspended, recognized, or enforced, do source systems preserve more than one status coordinate, or is a one-bit `valid/invalid` model sufficient?

## 3. Counterpressure before witnesses

The candidate fails or must be weakened if an independently specified legal regime requires all legally relevant distinctions to collapse into one status without loss of source meaning.

The following possibilities must be tested rather than assumed away:

1. formation and validity may coincide by rule;
2. a defect may prevent formation entirely;
3. a formed act may exist but be avoidable or defeasible;
4. an act may be binding but not yet enforceable in another regime;
5. a defect may affect only part of an act;
6. a later act may avoid, terminate, suspend, set aside, confirm, or cure an earlier status;
7. the word `invalid` may refer to different source-side consequences in different systems.

## 4. Witness classes

Use structurally independent witness families rather than a single national code:

- CISG / UNCITRAL: formation is governed while validity is expressly outside the Convention's ordinary scope;
- UNIDROIT Principles: conclusion, binding character, avoidance, illegality effects, and conditions are separately regulated;
- UNCITRAL Model Law on International Commercial Arbitration: award, binding recognition, enforcement, refusal, suspension, and setting-aside-related statuses are separated.

National law may be added later as a jurisdictional witness, but is not the foundation of this case.

## 5. DSD scope

Formation Axiom System: primary comparison layer.

Axis-Property System: not presumed necessary.

Static Aggregation: not required.

Dynamics: not required for the basic status distinction; only needed if a later study models the transition itself rather than static pre/post descriptors.

## 6. Failure criteria

Record a direct contradiction candidate only if, after the source act, stage, role, regime, and time are matched, the source requires an identification or separation incompatible with Formation structure.

Record `no meaningful mapping` if a legal status is purely normative and the DSD analogy would add no structural content.
