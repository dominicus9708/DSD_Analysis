# LAW-004 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Source Notes

Status: source audit complete for first cross-jurisdiction refresh.

## 1. United States — Federal Rules of Evidence

Current US Courts rules page:
https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-evidence

Useful rule text mirrors:
- Rule 401: https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_401
- Rule 402: https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_402
- Rule 403: https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_403

Source-side findings:

- Rule 401 defines relevance relationally to a fact of consequence.
- Rule 402 makes relevant evidence generally admissible, but expressly subjects that result to the Constitution, statutes, the Evidence Rules and other Supreme Court rules.
- Rule 403 permits exclusion of relevant evidence where probative value is substantially outweighed by specified dangers/costs.

Therefore:

`relevant != necessarily admitted/used`.

Also:

`admissibility != sufficiency/final finding`.

### Judicial notice

Ninth Circuit current model instruction:
https://www.ce9.uscourts.gov/jury-instructions/criminal/chapter-2/2-4-judicial-notice/

The federal judicial-notice structure permits a court to notice an adjudicative fact meeting Rule 201 conditions without ordinary evidence being formally presented on that point.

Therefore the rigid route

`party submission -> admitted evidence -> factual proposition`

is not universal even within US federal procedure.

## 2. Germany — ZPO

Official BMJ/BfJ text:

### § 144 ZPO — inspection and experts
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__144.html

The court may order an inspection and obtain expert assistance, and can require a party or third party to produce an object for that purpose, subject to source-defined limits.

This is a direct counterexample to a pure party-submission-only evidence model.

### § 286 ZPO — free evaluation of evidence
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__286.html

The court decides whether a factual assertion is true or untrue by free conviction considering the entire content of the proceedings and the result of any evidence-taking; statutory evidence rules bind only where the Code says so.

This shows that final fact evaluation is not reducible to a universal item-by-item scalar weight stage.

### § 288 ZPO — judicial admission
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__288.html

Facts alleged by one party do not require proof insofar as admitted by the opponent in the source-defined manner.

### § 291 ZPO — notorious facts
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__291.html

Facts notorious to the court require no proof.

These routes directly falsify the strong statement:

`every factual finding must be generated by a submitted evidentiary item`.

## 3. England and Wales — Civil Procedure Rules Part 32

Official Justice UK text:
https://www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil/rules/part32

Rule 32.1 gives the court power to control:
- the issues on which evidence is required;
- the nature of evidence required;
- the way evidence is placed before the court.

Rule 32.1(2) expressly permits the court to exclude evidence that would otherwise be admissible.

Therefore:

`otherwise admissible != necessarily used in the proceeding`.

The Part also regulates service and use of witness statements, showing that possession/existence, service, and actual trial use can be distinct states.

Practice Direction 32:
https://www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil/rules/part32/pd_part32

Agreed hearing bundles may be admissible as evidence of contents subject to court order or party objection. This further shows that procedural route and source rule matter to use status.

## 4. ECHR Article 6 doctrine

Official HUDOC/Knowledge Sharing material states that Article 6 guarantees fairness but does not itself lay down universal admissibility rules; admissibility and evidentiary assessment are primarily matters of national law, while Strasbourg reviews fairness of the proceedings as a whole.

Representative HTML source:
https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/docx/html/body?id=001-205536&library=ECHR

Current criminal Article 6 guide:
https://ks.echr.coe.int/documents/d/echr-ks/guide_art_6_criminal_eng

Source-side implication:

There is no Convention-level universal theorem:

`unlawfully obtained -> inadmissible`.

Nor does the ECHR impose one universal doctrinal boundary between admissibility, weight and fairness for every domestic system.

This is important counterpressure against turning one jurisdiction's evidence vocabulary into the universal LAW-004 taxonomy.

## 6. Source synthesis

The independent sources jointly support a narrower structural rule:

- information/material existence is not the same as procedural use;
- legal use can be generated or blocked by different source-defined routes;
- relevant or otherwise admissible material may still be excluded;
- some facts require no ordinary proof because of judicial notice, notoriety or admission;
- courts may initiate or control evidence acquisition in some regimes;
- admissibility vocabularies and exclusion consequences vary by regime;
- final factual determination does not reconstruct a single universal five-stage path.

The common structure is therefore a **source-indexed evidence/fact interface graph**, not a mandatory linear pipeline.
