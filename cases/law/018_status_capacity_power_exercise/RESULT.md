# LAW-007 Result — Legal Status, Capacity, Power, Exercisability, Exercise, and Effect

Status: first-pass universal analysis complete.

Primary purpose: reinterpretation.

Secondary purposes: coherence, predefinition audit, status/power boundary analysis, universal-claim falsification.

## 1. Answer-first result

No direct contradiction was found between the selected international/transnational legal witness structures and the DSD Formation distinctions after subject, status/right/power, actor, role, authority/support condition, present exercisability, exercise event, time, regime, and downstream effect were typed separately.

The initial roadmap wording was too rigid if read as a universal vocabulary in which `capacity`, `power`, `exercise`, and `effect` are always separate legal concepts.

CRPD doctrine is an important counterpressure: it uses `legal capacity` broadly enough to include both holding rights and acting under law, while separately regulating support and safeguards for exercising legal capacity.

The stronger surviving universal candidate is therefore vocabulary-neutral:

**when a source legal system distinguishes recognition/possession of a status, right, capacity or power from present exercisability, enabling support or authority, actual exercise, and downstream legal effect, those source distinctions must be preserved until a source rule explicitly identifies or transforms them.**

## 2. Witness-family results

### 2.1 CRPD Article 12 — capacity cannot be reduced to unsupported practical execution

Article 12(2) recognizes equal legal capacity. Article 12(3) separately requires access to support that may be needed in exercising legal capacity, and Article 12(4) requires safeguards for measures relating to its exercise.

General Comment No. 1 explains that legal capacity includes legal standing and legal agency.

Therefore the universal analysis must not impose a foreign narrow taxonomy on the source. Yet it must also not encode a need for support as absence of legal capacity.

Judgment: **strong witness for preserving source-defined capacity while separately representing conditions/support of exercise**.

### 2.2 UNIDROIT — right existence, exercisability, exercise, and enforcement are distinct

UNIDROIT provides several independent distinctions.

Chapter 10 explains that a right can exist before it can be exercised or enforced.

Article 7.3.2 provides that a termination right is exercised by notice.

Article 10.9 provides that expiration of a limitation period does not extinguish the right; its enforcement effect arises when the obligor asserts the limitation defence.

Thus:

`right exists`

`!= right presently exercisable`

`!= right actually exercised`

`!= right enforceable in the same way`.

Judgment: **strong direct witness against a single possession/exercise bit**.

### 2.3 Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties — entity capacity, actor authority, act, and effect are separate

Article 6 states that every State possesses capacity to conclude treaties.

Article 7 separately determines when a person is authorized to represent a State for treaty acts.

Article 8 provides that an unauthorized treaty-related act is without legal effect unless later confirmed by the State.

Therefore:

`State capacity`

`!= actor authority`

`!= exercise event`

`!= legal effect`

`!= later confirmation`.

Judgment: **strong public-international-law witness for multi-level power attribution**.

## 3. Main universal result

The witness families do not support a universal legal ontology with one binary status such as:

`HAS_POWER / NO_POWER`.

A source-faithful model may need to preserve a package such as:

`(recognized status/right/capacity, present exercisability, authority/support/prerequisites, exercise event, effect, confirmation/defence status, time, regime)`.

Not every system uses every coordinate or the same terminology. The point is to retain the coordinates that the source itself makes legally relevant.

## 4. Most important negative results

The case rejects these universal predefinitions:

`has right -> can exercise now`;

`can exercise -> has exercised`;

`exercise event -> effective result`;

`not exercised -> no underlying right`;

`needs support -> lacks legal capacity`;

`entity has capacity -> every actor has authority`;

`ineffective attempted act -> no event occurred`;

`time-barred enforcement -> right extinguished`.

## 5. Relation to LAW-003

LAW-003 focused on representation:

`same person != legal role != authority != act != attributed effect`.

LAW-007 begins one step earlier and later at once:

`status/right/capacity exists != presently exercisable != actually exercised != effective downstream`.

The VCLT witness partly overlaps with LAW-003 but is used here for a distinct abstraction: system-level capacity and actor-level authority are separate from the actual exercise and resulting effect.

## 6. Functional prosecution/defence implication

The `P/D/J` model also gains another distinction.

A prosecution/attribution function may need to establish not only that a subject possessed a relevant legal status or power, but also that the specific actor had the required authority, the power was exercisable under the relevant conditions, an exercise event occurred, and the source rule gave that event the asserted effect.

The defence function can attack any one of those links without proving the opposite global proposition.

Thus:

`failure to establish effective exercise`

`!= proof that no underlying status/right/power existed`.

## 7. DSD Formation comparison

Formation is useful as a typed non-totalization discipline. Its staged structure, partial assignments, channel identity and distinction among undefined, defined zero and channel absence provide a language for preserving source-side state separation.

However:

`legal capacity != Formation admission`;

`legal power != Formation realization`;

`nonexercise != undefined assignment`;

`ineffective exercise != channel absence`.

A legally ineffective act may still be an existing event required by the source for later confirmation or legal analysis.

Judgment: **meaningful structural correspondence after explicit typed legal encoding; no semantic identity**.

## 8. Axis-Property, Static Aggregation, and Dynamics

Axis-Property is not required because the source systems do not independently supply realized-axis semantics.

Static Aggregation is not required because capacity, right, authority, exercise and effect are not numerical contributions.

Dynamics is not required for the first-pass distinction. Later confirmation, loss of a right, expiry, restoration, suspension or revocation could be represented as source-supplied legal transitions in a later dynamic analysis.

## 9. Finite witness result

Five source states suffice to defeat a single binary `POWER` encoding:

1. right/capacity recognized but not yet exercisable;
2. right exercisable but not exercised;
3. right exercised and effective;
4. right remains but enforcement is barred;
5. exercise event occurred but effect is withheld pending confirmation.

A binary value loses at least one legally relevant distinction.

## 10. Cross-case generalization

LAW-001 through LAW-006 established that actions, proof states, roles, evidence states, legal defects and normative statuses cannot be silently promoted, demoted or globally normalized.

LAW-007 adds:

**possession of a legal status/right/power is not by itself the exercise or effect of that power, and failure of exercise/effect does not by itself erase the underlying status.**

The domain-level rule becomes:

**preserve source-defined identity, applicability, possession, exercisability, exercise event and effect separately; perform any promotion, demotion, activation, suspension or attribution only through the source rule that actually licenses it.**

## 12. Boundary statement

LAW-007 does not define legal capacity from DSD, does not decide disability-law doctrine, does not claim that all jurisdictions distinguish capacity, competence, authority and exercise with the same vocabulary, and does not derive legal effect from mere possession of a power.

It establishes a narrower cross-system result: the selected source families independently require enough distinction among recognition/possession, exercisability, support or authority, exercise, enforcement and downstream effect to defeat a context-free binary power model, while those distinctions coexist with Formation under an explicit typed bridge.
