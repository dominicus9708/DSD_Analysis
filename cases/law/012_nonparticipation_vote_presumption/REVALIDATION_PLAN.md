# LAW-001 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Plan

Status: active revalidation after completion of LAW-001 through LAW-010 first-pass sequence.

Global case: 012. No new LAW/global case number is created.

## 1. Existing candidate under review

Original scope candidate:

`nonaction != opposite action`

and, in responsibility-attribution settings:

`failure to establish responsibility != proof of the opposite factual proposition`.

The original Korea/parliamentary materials remain historical witness records. They are not treated as the foundation of universality.

## 2. Revalidation questions

1. Do independent institutional voting systems distinguish absence, abstention, affirmative vote, negative vote, and downstream tally effect?
2. Do election systems distinguish nonparticipation from a cast-but-invalid/informal ballot?
3. Can a source rule assign a consequence to nonaction without converting nonaction into the identity of an opposite action?
4. Do independent criminal systems preserve accusation, proof burden, guilty verdict, acquittal/not-guilty result, and factual innocence as non-identical states?
5. Does counterpressure from evidentiary presumptions or affirmative-defence burdens falsify an overbroad claim that the prosecution bears every burden in every criminal issue?

## 3. Witness families

- UN General Assembly Rules of Procedure, Rules 67, 82-87.
- EU Council/TFEU unanimity rule for abstentions.
- Australian Electoral Commission turnout, informality, and compulsory-voting rules.
- UK Electoral Commission rejected-ballot rules.
- UNIDROIT Principles Article 2.1.6 on silence/inactivity and source-supplied exceptions through agreement/practice/usage.
- Rome Statute Article 66.
- ECHR Article 6(2) doctrine, including Salabiaku counterpressure.
- US Ninth Circuit model criminal jury instructions as an independent domestic witness, including affirmative-defence burden exceptions.

## 4. Main falsification targets

Reject or narrow if necessary:

- `nonaction never has legal/institutional consequences`;
- `abstention has the same identity as a NO vote whenever it disadvantages a proposal`;
- `failure to vote = invalid/informal ballot`;
- `silence can never produce assent`;
- `presumption of innocence means every burden on every criminal issue always lies on prosecution`;
- `not guilty = positive proof of factual innocence`;
- `lack of guilty verdict = DSD undefined state`.

## 5. DSD boundary

Formation is used only as a typed/partial-state comparison discipline.

Do not identify:

- absence/nonparticipation with channel absence without an application-specific encoding;
- abstention with defined zero automatically;
- invalid ballot with undefined assignment automatically;
- acquittal/not-guilty with factual innocence;
- legal presumption with a DSD axiom.

Axis-Property, Static Aggregation, and Dynamics are not required for the first-pass revalidation.

## 6. Completion condition

The revalidation is complete when:

1. independent witness families are recorded;
2. counterexamples/default-effect rules are retained rather than explained away;
3. the universal candidate is narrowed if needed;
4. direct contradiction with Formation is audited;
5. LAW-001 generalization status is updated without overwriting the historical original RESULT.md.
