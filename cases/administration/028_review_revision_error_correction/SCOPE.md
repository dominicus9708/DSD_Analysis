# ADMIN-003 Scope

## Included

- review of already approved or executing organizational decisions;
- partial amendment and preservation of unchanged portions;
- error/discrepancy correction;
- suspension/hold and explicit resumption/rejoining;
- supersession and version lineage;
- authorization, implementation, verification, release, and closure distinctions;
- iterative operational planning under changing information;
- responsibility/audit implications of preserving revision history.

## Excluded

- universal claims that every organization uses the same change-control lifecycle;
- treating FAA, Army, FEMA, or NASA terminology as interchangeable;
- deriving organizational policy from DSD axioms;
- treating version numbers, approval levels, or command hierarchy as realized DSD axes by default;
- numerical scoring of correction severity, authority, or organizational quality without an independent model;
- software-specific defect theory beyond what is needed as a NASA change-control witness;
- legal appellate/reconsideration doctrine already belonging to the law-domain sequence.

## Boundary condition

A source-defined distinction is preserved even if two states later lead to the same operational result.

Conversely, no distinction is invented merely to make an external procedure look like DSD.

## Primary DSD interfaces

1. Formation — typed status/provenance discipline.
2. Structural Reorganization Dynamics — identity-sensitive transition and lineage.
3. Static Aggregation — warning against reconstructing history from equal reduced output.

Axis-Property is not required for the core result.
