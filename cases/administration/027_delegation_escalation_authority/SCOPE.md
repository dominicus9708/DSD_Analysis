# Scope — ADMIN-002

## Included

- formal organizational role/rank;
- reporting relationships;
- delegated decision authority;
- delegation scope, limits, duration, and retained decisions;
- responsibility/accountability as distinct from delegated authority;
- independent technical/review authority;
- risk-acceptance authority;
- dissent and escalation paths;
- transfer of command and effective time;
- implementation authority.

## Excluded / deferred

- criminal/civil command liability;
- employment-law disciplinary authority;
- corporate board fiduciary duties;
- detailed procurement approval workflows;
- cybersecurity privilege escalation;
- quantitative organizational-network analysis.

## Boundary

The case does not claim that the Army, FEMA ICS, or NASA governance should be copied by all organizations.

It only tests whether one simple hierarchy is an adequate universal model of authority and responsibility.

The answer is negative.

## DSD boundary

Do not translate organizational hierarchy into Axis-Property without an independently justified realized-axis interpretation.

Do not translate delegated authority into Formation admission, and do not treat responsibility or review authority as scalar weights.
