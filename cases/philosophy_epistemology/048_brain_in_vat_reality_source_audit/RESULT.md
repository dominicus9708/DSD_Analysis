# PHIL-005 / Global 048 — Brain in a Vat: Reality-Source and Regime Audit

Status: **first-pass analysis completed**.

PHIL-005 preserves three distinct questions rather than treating `Brain in a Vat` as one argument.

1. **Skeptical reconstruction problem** — can one internally accessible record uniquely determine the external world?
2. **Implementation/equivalence problem** — what must be equal for a physically realized BIV to be genuinely indistinguishable from ordinary experience?
3. **Putnam semantic problem** — what can an always-envatted speaker's words `brain`, `vat`, and `I am a BIV` refer to, and does that semantic result establish that the speaker is not a BIV?

## 1. Generic skeptical BIV as a reconstruction problem

Let:

- `W_N` = ordinary-world realization;
- `W_B` = BIV-world realization;
- `A_Q(W,t)` = subject-accessible descriptor under declared channel family `Q` at time `t`.

The strong skeptical setup stipulates, for a declared interval `I`,

`A_Q(W_N,t) = A_Q(W_B,t)` for every `t in I`,

while

`W_N != W_B`.

Therefore the projection from full world realization to the accessible descriptor is non-injective:

`A_Q(W_N,.) = A_Q(W_B,.)` with `W_N != W_B`.

Hence the inverse inference

`A_Q(W,.) -> unique full external world W`

is invalid without an additional injectivity/reconstruction theorem.

### Classification

This is not primarily a DSD attack on the skeptical thought experiment. The generic BIV construction itself is a **precedent-convergence structure**: it deliberately instantiates the same reduced/full non-injectivity discipline that DSD later formalizes in other contexts.

What does not follow from this structural fact alone is the broader epistemological thesis `therefore no external-world knowledge is possible`; that requires an additional theory of evidence and knowledge.

## 2. Equivalence-Signature Audit

The phrase `the BIV experience is exactly the same` is under-specified until the comparison signature is declared.

Let `Q_A` be the set of channels or records the subject can actually use for discrimination. It may include, depending on the application:

- visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, and gustatory inputs;
- proprioceptive and vestibular information;
- interoceptive information;
- motor-command/efference-copy or corollary-discharge relations;
- temporal continuity and memory relations;
- agency/self-monitoring cues;
- reality/source-monitoring outputs;
- other empirically justified subject-accessible discriminators.

Define strong subject-level indistinguishability over interval `I` by

`Eq_Q(W_N,W_B;I) : for all t in I and q in Q_A, D_q(W_N,t) = D_q(W_B,t)`.

This is stronger than equality of a selected electrical stimulation vector or one sensory modality.

### Core non-implication

`equal chosen electrical stimulation`

**does not by itself imply**

`Eq_Q(W_N,W_B;I)`.

A constitutive bridge from the stimulation interface to every comparison channel in `Q_A` would be required.

Current neurotechnology can evoke meaningful artificial sensory percepts, but the literature still reports unresolved questions about neural activation mechanisms, percept naturalness, resolution, tissue response, multimodal integration, and bidirectional integration. Therefore the project does not treat full BIV biological equivalence as an established empirical capability.

### Scope

This does **not** refute the logical BIV. A philosophical thought experiment may stipulate `Eq_Q` directly. The result is only that the physical-realization claim and the logical stipulation must not be conflated.

## 3. Reality-Source Monitoring Layer

The user's autobiographical observation suggested a useful search hypothesis: perhaps the discriminability of internally generated and externally driven information depends partly on the state of environmental coupling and on monitoring of source/origin.

The literature supports a related but more cautious structure.

Let:

- `E(t)` = externally driven sensory evidence available at time `t`;
- `I_G(t)` = internally generated perceptual/cognitive activity;
- `C(t)` = contextual, agency, memory, and metacognitive cues;
- `M(t)` = application-level source/reality-monitoring judgment.

The project uses no medical equation asserting causation. It records only the structural possibility

`M(t) = M(E(t), I_G(t), C(t), ...)`

with multiple relevant cues.

Research on perceptual reality monitoring argues that imagination and externally triggered perception share neural mechanisms and that no single factor unambiguously marks internal versus external origin. Higher-level monitoring must evaluate several first-order sensory and cognitive factors.

Source-monitoring studies and meta-analyses also report associations between hallucinations and externalizing/source-attribution errors in several clinical populations, but results are heterogeneous and not a universal single-cause account.

Sleep and sleep-onset research supplies a separate convergence: environmental coupling and processing change across sleep/wake transitions, and hypnagogic/hypnopompic experiences can make reality monitoring difficult. This supports separating `amount of external coupling`, `internal generation`, and `source judgment`; it does not justify the universal rule `low arousal causes hallucination`.

### DSD consequence for BIV

A physically serious BIV must preserve not merely first-order sensory content but also whatever source/reality-monitoring cues are included in the subject's admissible discriminator set.

Thus:

`same apparent sensory scene`

need not imply

`same reality-source classification trajectory`.

If even one admissible discriminator remains systematically different, the declared strong BIV indistinguishability condition fails for that comparison signature.

## 4. Putnam's Always-Envatted Semantic Argument

Putnam's 1981 argument considers a more radical case in which the subjects have always been envatted and lack ordinary causal relations to real trees, brains, and vats.

Under semantic/content externalism, the same surface string can have different truth conditions or referents in different causal environments.

This directly continues the PHIL-004 Twin Earth precedent-convergence structure.

Let:

- `R_E` = ordinary-English semantic/reference regime;
- `R_V` = vat-English semantic/reference regime;
- `u` = the surface string `I am a BIV`.

The project allows:

`surface(u,R_E) = surface(u,R_V)`

while

`Sem_R_E(u) != Sem_R_V(u)`.

If always-envatted speakers lack the relevant causal relations to ordinary brains and vats, Putnam argues that `brain` and `vat` in vat-English cannot have ordinary English reference. His semantic shift is therefore structurally coherent under the externalist signature.

### Precedent convergence

This semantic point is another continuation of the PHIL-004 precedent-convergence family:

`same internal/surface record != same full reference structure`.

DSD does not refute this move.

## 5. Semantic Result versus Anti-Skeptical Object-Level Conclusion

A further inference is required to move from a semantic statement such as

`my utterance of 'I am a BIV' is false/not true in each admissible semantic case`

to the object-level claim

`I am not a BIV`.

The bridge typically has a disquotational form such as

`T_R: 'I am not a BIV' is true in regime R iff I am not a BIV`.

But `T_R` itself is regime-sensitive. If ordinary English and vat-English have different reference conditions, one cannot silently apply the ordinary-English disquotation schema while remaining agnostic about which semantic regime one occupies.

Thus:

`semantic non-truth of the BIV sentence`

**does not by itself yield**

`object-level non-BIV status`

without a justified semantic-regime bridge.

This is not historically new. Brueckner-style literature explicitly identifies the epistemic-circularity/disquotation problem.

### Classification

**Mode-B historical convergence + DSD regime/bridge sharpening; no novelty claim.**

## 6. Recent Envatment Boundary

The Putnamian semantic argument does not automatically eliminate recent-envatment scenarios.

If a normal speaker is envatted only recently, causal-historical reference links may persist for some interval. Then the ordinary-world meanings of `brain`, `vat`, `hand`, and other terms need not instantly change.

This reinforces the DSD requirement that semantic/reference status be time-indexed when the causal history changes:

`R(t0) !=> immediate R(t0 + epsilon) replacement`.

A theory of semantic transition is needed rather than an instantaneous relabeling assumption.

## 7. Finite Witness Structure

The accompanying finite witness checks three independent facts.

1. Two different full worlds can share one declared accessible projection.
2. Adding one previously omitted discriminator can break that projection equality, showing that indistinguishability is signature-relative.
3. The same surface sentence can be assigned different semantic targets under ordinary-English and vat-English regimes, so one disquotation rule cannot be silently treated as regime-neutral.

## 8. Final Verdict

PHIL-005 does not have a single `refuted / not refuted` answer.

### Generic skeptical BIV

The non-injective reconstruction structure survives and is best classified as **precedent convergence** with DSD reduced/full-structure discipline.

### Physical implementation

`electrical interface similarity -> complete biological/experiential indistinguishability` is not established by logic or current neurotechnology. A complete equivalence signature and constitutive bridge are required. This is **DSD formal sharpening / scope restriction**, not a refutation of the stipulated logical scenario.

### Reality/source monitoring

The user's heuristic has meaningful overlap with established reality/source-monitoring, sleep/wake coupling, and self-monitoring research, but it is retained only as a research-compatible hypothesis shape, not as a medical generalization.

### Putnam semantic argument

The reference-shift component strongly survives and continues the Twin Earth precedent-convergence family. The stronger anti-skeptical conclusion `therefore I am not a BIV` still needs a justified regime-sensitive disquotation bridge; the circularity objection is established literature.

### Project classification

**PHIL-005 first pass complete — generic BIV non-injectivity recorded as precedent convergence; physical full-indistinguishability isolated as an unproven constitutive implementation bridge; reality/source-monitoring literature supplies a multi-cue empirical comparison; Putnam's semantic reference shift survives as PHIL-004-family precedent convergence, while the object-level anti-skeptical conclusion remains bridge-dependent in an already established Mode-B objection family. No historical novelty claim.**
