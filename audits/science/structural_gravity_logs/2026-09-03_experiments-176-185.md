# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 176–185

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: conditional causal-completion audit

## Experiment 176 — Single-field hyperbolic completion

Conditionally replace the static elliptic branch by

\[
\mathcal P_{g_{\rm prog}}\psi
=
4\pi\Lambda_t\rho
+
\mathcal S_{\rm str},
\]

with principal part

\[
g_{\rm prog}^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu\psi.
\]

Monopole, quadrupole, and higher multipoles are spatial decompositions of the same scalar field, not distinct propagation channels.

## Experiment 177 — Common characteristic cone

Inside this single-scalar branch, every disturbance in \(\psi\) has the same principal characteristic set, independent of whether the source is mass density or a zero-monopole structural source. Lower-order terms may change waveform or produce inside-cone tails, but do not change the principal cone.

## Experiment 178 — Extra-field warning

If structural corrections are carried by an additional field \(\chi\), the coupled principal symbol may possess multiple cones. Structural corrections then share the \(\psi\) cone only when the reachable coupled modes do. Lower-order coupling alone does not force cone equality.

## Experiment 179 — No instantaneous static substitution

During a source transition, the exterior field cannot be obtained by inserting instantaneous source moments into the static multipole formula at the same coordinate time. The response at \((t,\mathbf x)\) depends only on the source's causal past under the hyperbolic completion.

## Experiment 180 — Event classes

- monopole-changing event: \(\Delta M\neq0\);
- mass- and centroid-preserving rearrangement: \(\Delta M=0\), \(\Delta\mathbf D=0\), so the final static difference begins at quadrupole order;
- other events in which lower moments change.

These event classes differ in final spatial response but may share the same causal onset cone.

## Experiment 181 — Source removal

A source-removal event cannot change an exterior point before the causal domain reaches it. After arrival, the response need not be a step disappearance; waveform and possible inside-cone tails depend on the hyperbolic dynamics.

## Experiment 182 — Internal rearrangement signal

A source can preserve total mass while changing quadrupole or higher structure. Initial and final monopoles can therefore agree even though a transient progression-field disturbance propagates outward.

## Experiment 183 — Static vs dynamic decay separation

Static multipole laws

\[
\delta\psi_l\sim r^{-(l+1)},
\qquad
\delta a_l\sim r^{-(l+2)}
\]

do not determine the radial amplitude law of time-dependent transients. Dynamic falloff requires the hyperbolic Green structure and source-time profile.

## Experiment 184 — Onset vs amplitude

Multipole order changes static spatial amplitude/order but not the principal causal arrival cone in the single-field branch.

## Experiment 185 — Speed identification

If the progression field and probe kinematics use the same metric, the representation is local/support-faithful, and a reachable mode saturates the common cone, then

\[
c_{\rm info}^{(\psi)}=c_{\rm kin}.
\]

Otherwise retain a separate \(c_\psi\).

## Verdict

- instantaneous global update: REJECTED;
- mass-preserving structural transient: ALLOWED;
- static multipole decay implies dynamic transient decay: REJECTED.
