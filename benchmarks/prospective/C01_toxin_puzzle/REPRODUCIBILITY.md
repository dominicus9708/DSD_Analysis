## Verification checklist

A reviewer can reproduce the audit by checking that:

- Kavka rewards intention rather than action;
- Kavka explicitly distinguishes reasons for intending from reasons for acting;
- later literature explicitly discusses formation/execution and stability of intention;
- at least one later account supplies an explicit reason-based bridge from anticipated future agency to present intention formation;
- Mele’s Ted provides a counterexample to a simple universal normative-reason constraint;
- Rudy-Hiller distinguishes normative reasons from motivating/explanatory reasons;
- these facts support the C1/C2/C3 classifications in `RESULT.md`.
