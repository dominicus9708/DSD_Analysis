# Scope — ADMIN-001

## Included

- organizational instructions, orders, clearances, objectives, and assignments;
- issuer intent/purpose;
- communicated wording and constraints;
- receipt, acknowledgement, interpretation, clarification, and verification;
- recipient discretion and initiative;
- execution, feedback, revision, and outcome;
- organizational responsibility implications caused by interface failure.

## Excluded / deferred

- detailed criminal or civil liability for negligent communication;
- employment-law discipline;
- psychological obedience/compliance experiments;
- political propaganda or manipulation;
- software command protocols;
- quantitative information theory of ambiguity;
- detailed human-factors accident reconstruction.

These may become separate administration, psychology, software/security, or empirical-validation cases.

## Domain boundary

This case is not an extension of the closed legal foundation merely because military/aviation/emergency systems contain formal rules.

Its target is the operational organization problem:

`intent -> communication -> interpretation -> coordinated action -> feedback/outcome`.

Legal sources may be used only where they directly define an organizational interface; the primary witnesses here are doctrine/procedure/management systems.

## Generalization boundary

The case does not prove that all organizations should use mission command, readback, unity of command, or any one management method.

It supports only a source-sensitive non-collapse candidate and falsifies several universal simplifications.
