# LAW-007 Scope

## Universal structural claim

The universal candidate is not a fixed vocabulary of `capacity -> competence -> authority -> exercise` shared by every legal system.

It is the source-sensitive non-collapse rule:

**where the source distinguishes recognition/possession of a status, right, capacity or power from present exercisability, enabling authority/support, actual exercise, enforcement or legal effect, preserve those distinctions until the source rule performs the transition.**

## Witness families

- CRPD Article 12 + General Comment No. 1
- UNIDROIT Principles on termination and limitation
- Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties Articles 6-8

## Generalization status

`universal candidate supported by multiple independent witness families; active falsification remains open`.

## Exclusions

- no claim that all jurisdictions define legal capacity identically;
- no disability-law doctrine derived from DSD;
- no automatic identification of capacity with authority or effect;
- no Axis-Property interpretation without realized-axis semantics;
- no numerical aggregation.
