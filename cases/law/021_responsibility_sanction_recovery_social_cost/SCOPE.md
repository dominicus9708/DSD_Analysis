# LAW-010 Scope

## Universal structural claim

When a source legal or institutional regime gives responsibility attribution, sanction, victim restoration/reparation, rehabilitation, prevention/non-repetition, social-emotional response, institutional/social cost, formal legal status, or practical use different actors, recipients, conditions, procedures, temporal states or effects, those distinctions must be preserved until a source or independently justified external rule identifies, combines, prioritizes or transforms them.

## Strong claim rejected

LAW-010 does **not** claim that every legal system doctrinally separates all of the above categories using the same vocabulary or procedure.

It also rejects any universal scalar social-choice formula for punishment.

## Witness families

- ICC Rome Statute Articles 75 and 77: reparations and penalties are distinct routes.
- UN human-rights redress materials: restitution, compensation, rehabilitation, satisfaction and guarantees of non-repetition are distinct remedial forms; victim status may exist independently of perpetrator conviction.
- South Africa `S v Makwanyane`: public opinion may be relevant without controlling constitutional adjudication.
- Germany Article 102 history: Nazi-era capital-punishment abuse is a decisive historical constitutional context while postwar reintroduction pressure also existed.
- Comparative death-penalty reporting: abolition in law and abolition in practice are distinct statuses.
- Victim-family source: bereaved families are heterogeneous in capital-punishment preference.

## Mapping status

- Formation: meaningful correspondence after explicit legal/social encoding; no semantic identity.
- Dynamics: complementary useful layer for source-defined temporal transitions.
- Axis-Property: not required.
- Static Aggregation: not used as punishment, emotion or social-cost calculus.

## Generalization status

`source-sensitive universal candidate supported by multiple independent international, constitutional and institutional witness families; active falsification remains open`.

## Boundary

LAW-010 does not decide the morality or optimality of capital punishment or any theory of punishment. It does not infer legal outcomes directly from public emotion, victim preference, historical abuse or social cost.
