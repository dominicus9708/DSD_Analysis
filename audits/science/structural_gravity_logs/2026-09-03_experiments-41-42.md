# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 41–42

Date: 2026-09-03

## Experiment 41 — Dimensional-basis no-go from \(M,L,c_{\rm info}\)

### Assumption set

Assume the only independent dimensionful transition data are source mass \(M\), source length \(L\), and finite propagation speed \(c_{\rm info}\). Density shape, realized rank, bounded-state label, channel count, and typed describability data are treated as dimensionless or non-numerical modifiers unless an additional dimensional realization is independently supplied.

The target normalization has

\[
[\mu_*]=M/L.
\]

### Dimensional audit

For

\[
\mu\sim M^aL^bc_{\rm info}^d,
\]

the dimensions are

\[
M^aL^{b+d}T^{-d}.
\]

Matching \(ML^{-1}\) forces

\[
a=1,\qquad d=0,\qquad b=-1.
\]

Therefore the dimensionally admissible form is

\[
\boxed{\mu=\frac{M}{L}F(\Pi)}
\]

with \(F\) dimensionless.

Thus \(c_{\rm info}\) cannot directly generate the missing mass-per-length dimension.

For a gravitational-response-like coefficient with

\[
[\chi]=L^3M^{-1}T^{-2},
\]

the corresponding form is

\[
\boxed{\chi=c_{\rm info}^2\frac{L}{M}F(\Pi)}.
\]

### Self-similar scaling

For a fixed-shape 3D family,

\[
L\to\alpha L,
\qquad
M\to\alpha^3M,
\qquad
\Pi\to\Pi,
\]

so

\[
\mu\to\alpha^2\mu,
\qquad
\chi\to\alpha^{-2}\chi.
\]

Hence neither is source-independent.

### Density and radial moments

If

\[
\rho\sim M/L^3,
\]

then

\[
\rho L^2\sim M/L.
\]

Similarly, radial moments \(I_n\sim ML^n\) reduce to the same source \(M/L\) after multiplication by the required power of \(L\).

Thus density or moments do not create a new dimensional basis when they are derived solely from the same \(M,L\).

### Verdict

- \(M,L,c_{\rm info}\) plus dimensionless structure yields universal \(M/L\): **NO-GO UNDER ASSUMPTIONS**
- density or radial moments as independent dimensional anchor when derived from \(M,L\): **REJECTED**
- \(c_{\rm info}\) alone supplies the missing mass scale: **REJECTED**

## Experiment 42 — Transition time, flux, and distortion anchor audit

### Crossing-time transition

If

\[
\tau=\frac{L}{c_{\rm info}}g(\Pi),
\]

then \(c_{\rm info}\tau/L\) is dimensionless.

If a mass-transition rate is

\[
\dot M\sim\frac{M}{\tau}H(\Pi),
\]

then

\[
\frac{\dot M}{c_{\rm info}}
=\frac{M}{L}\frac{H(\Pi)}{g(\Pi)}.
\]

Therefore a crossing-time-derived mass flux reduces to the same source-specific \(M/L\).

### Independent time or flux scale

If a source-independent structural law supplies an independent \(\tau_*\), then \(c_{\rm info}\tau_*/L\) introduces a new scale ratio and can break self-similarity. But \(\tau_*\) is then itself a new dimensionful primitive.

If an independently derived mass flux \(J_*\) has \([J_*]=M/T\), then

\[
\mu_*\sim\frac{J_*}{c_{\rm info}}
\]

is dimensionally viable.

If an independently derived force/tension scale \(\mathcal T_*\) exists, then

\[
\mu_*\sim\frac{\mathcal T_*}{c_{\rm info}^2}
\]

is likewise viable.

These are viable anchor classes only if their absolute amplitudes are derived rather than freely supplied.

### Distortion amplitude

If a potential-like structural distortion has \([X]=L^2/T^2\), then \(X/c_{\rm info}^2\) is dimensionless and

\[
M\frac{|\nabla X|}{c_{\rm info}^2}
\]

has \(M/L\) dimensions.

However, if \(X\) is the structural-gravity response being derived, using it to define its own coupling is circular. It is admissible as an upstream anchor only if an independent pre-response distortion observable is defined.

### Dimensionless typed sectors

Rank, channel count, bounded-component count, normalized density-shape moments, typed relation equivalence, and describability ratios can modify \(F(\Pi)\) but do not supply an absolute dimension by themselves.

### Verdict

- crossing time derived from \(L/c_{\rm info}\): **NOT AN ANCHOR**
- mass flux derived from \(M/\tau\): **REDUCES TO SOURCE \(M/L\)**
- independently derived mass flux / force / impedance / time scale: **DIMENSIONALLY VIABLE ANCHOR CLASS**
- response-side distortion used to define its own coupling: **CIRCULAR / REJECTED**

### Strong conditional no-go

If there is no independent dimensionful primitive beyond source \(M,L\) and \(c_{\rm info}\), and the dimensionless structural profile remains fixed under self-similar scaling, then a source-independent gravitational normalization cannot be derived.
