# LAW-002 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation — Contradiction Audit

Status: completed.

## 1. Strong form under attack

Potentially overbroad reading of the original case:

`W + S + P + D + J + R_E + R_L`

as a universal seven-part legal architecture with one office/person per function.

That strong form does **not** survive.

## 2. Office/function contradiction test

### Prosecutor as guilt-only function

Rejected.

ICC Rome Statute Article 54 and German StPO §160 require prosecutorial investigation of both incriminating and exonerating circumstances.

Therefore:

`prosecutor office = P only`

is false as a universal model.

The office can contain investigation, truth-seeking, preservation and exculpatory duties in addition to any responsibility-attribution function.

### Court as passive terminal decision-maker

Rejected.

German StPO §244(2) gives the court an ex officio duty to extend the taking of evidence to relevant facts and means of proof.

Therefore:

`J office receives finished evidence only`

is false as a universal model.

### Victim/source as prosecution component

Rejected.

ICC Article 68(3) permits victim views and concerns to be presented through a legally distinct participation route.

Therefore:

`victim/source = prosecution regime`

is false as a universal identity.

## 3. Separation counterpressure

The opposite overcorrection is also rejected:

`all functions may always be freely combined`.

ECHR Article 6 requires an independent and impartial tribunal for determination of the criminal charge and separately protects defence rights. ECtHR materials distinguish the deciding tribunal from prosecution representatives as parties.

US APA §554(d), in covered adjudications, restricts employees performing investigative/prosecuting functions from participating in decisional functions in the same or factually related case, subject to statutory exceptions.

Therefore source systems may impose real separation constraints.

The universal result is not fusion or separation; it is **source-indexed function allocation**.

## 4. `R_E` and `R_L` module test

Rejected as universal indivisible modules.

Evidence-use rules can include:

- collection;
- presentation;
- admissibility;
- exclusion;
- relevance;
- burden;
- record rules;
- court-initiated evidence rules.

Decision rules can include:

- burden/standard of proof;
- initial decision;
- recommended decision;
- review;
- final decision;
- sanction/remedy consequence.

Different systems distribute these across provisions, actors and procedural stages.

Therefore `R_E` and `R_L` remain useful shorthand families, not necessarily single rules.

## 5. World/reference layer test

No contradiction found with preserving:

`event/reference != source record != procedural claim != usable record != adjudicative finding`.

None of the witness systems licenses an analyst to identify the historical event automatically with the evidence record or final institutional finding.

The original finite non-identifiability point therefore survives.

## 6. P/D/J functional asymmetry test

The later project convention is retained:

- `P` = responsibility-attribution/prosecution **function**, not prosecutor office;
- `D` = defence/counterargument **function**, not necessarily one defence lawyer;
- `J` = adjudicative judgment **function**, not the whole institutional court/agency.

A concrete actor or institution may instantiate multiple functions, and a function may be distributed across multiple actors.

Thus the mapping is many-to-many:

`Actors <-> Functions`.

## 7. DSD contradiction audit

No direct contradiction with Formation was found after this narrowing.

Formation is used only to preserve typed roles, partial records and non-totalization.

Do not identify:

- legal actor = Formation channel;
- prosecutor = one channel role universally;
- evidentiary admissibility = Formation admission;
- acquittal = undefined value;
- judgment = complete reconstruction of `W`.

Axis-Property is not required for the refreshed core result.

Static Aggregation is not required.

Dynamics is not required.

## 8. Falsification summary

Rejected:

- universal seven-office architecture;
- prosecutor as guilt-only constructor;
- court as passive evidence consumer;
- victim as prosecution sub-role;
- one actor = one procedural function;
- one institution = one procedural function;
- `R_E` and `R_L` as universally indivisible modules.

Survives:

**source-distinct event/reference, records/sources, attribution, response/defence, evidence/ground usability and adjudicative decision states must not be collapsed; the allocation and combination of those functions is itself source-rule indexed.**
