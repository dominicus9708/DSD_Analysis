# LAW-002 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Result

Status: cross-jurisdiction revalidation complete.

Original historical `RESULT.md` is preserved.

## 1. Answer-first result

The original multi-regime insight survives, but the fixed `W + S + P + D + J + R_E + R_L` decomposition must be read as a **functional/interface schema**, not as a universal seven-office architecture.

Rejected strong form:

`every legal system has distinct world/source/prosecutor/defence/judge/evidence-rule/decision-rule modules with one actor per module`.

The surviving universal candidate is:

**when a legal or institutional process gives the historical event/reference, source/record, responsibility-attribution or accusation, response/defence, evidence/ground-use, adjudicative finding, and decision/consequence rules different roles, conditions, procedures, information states or effects, those distinctions and interfaces must be preserved. The source system, not DSD, determines whether functions are separated, combined, distributed or iterated across actors and stages.**

A stronger compact form is:

`event/reference != source record != procedural construction != legally usable ground != adjudicative finding`.

And:

`office identity != procedural-function identity`.

## 2. ICC witness — prosecutor office is not a guilt-only regime

Rome Statute Article 54 requires the Prosecutor, in order to establish the truth, to investigate incriminating and exonerating circumstances equally.

This directly defeats a literal reading of `P` as equivalent to the institutional Prosecutor.

The Prosecutor may instantiate a responsibility-attribution function, but the office also carries investigation, evidence collection, truth-seeking and exculpatory duties.

Therefore:

`Prosecutor_office != P_function_only`.

This strengthens the later project convention that `P` means an abstract attribution/prosecution function rather than one legal office.

## 3. German witness — both prosecution and court can cross the naive regime boundaries

German StPO §160(2) independently requires the public prosecution office to ascertain both incriminating and exonerating circumstances.

German StPO §244(2) requires the court, of its own motion, to extend the taking of evidence to all facts and means of proof relevant to the decision.

Thus:

`prosecutor != one-sided guilt constructor`;

and:

`court/judgment office != passive terminal consumer of party evidence`.

A single office can carry several functions, and evidentiary development can occur inside the adjudicative institution.

## 4. ICC victim-participation witness — source/victim is not prosecution

Rome Statute Article 68(3) permits victims, where their personal interests are affected, to present views and concerns through a route controlled by the Court and bounded by accused rights and fair-trial requirements.

Therefore:

`victim/source != prosecution`;

`victim participation != defence`;

and a procedural actor may have a source-defined route not reducible to the P/D binary.

This confirms the original correction that victim experience or testimony must not be silently absorbed into a prosecution description.

## 5. ECHR witness — real separation can nevertheless be source-required

Article 6 requires determination of a criminal charge by an independent and impartial tribunal and separately guarantees defence rights.

ECtHR guidance distinguishes the deciding tribunal, to which independence/impartiality requirements apply, from prosecution representatives acting as parties.

Therefore some regimes impose a genuine structural separation:

`prosecution function != adjudicative function`.

But this is a source-supplied procedural requirement, not a proof that all legal systems must instantiate exactly the same offices or stage boundaries.

The revalidated model must therefore avoid both extremes:

- universal mandatory fusion;
- universal mandatory one-office-per-function separation.

## 6. US APA witness — functions can coexist within one institution while personnel are separated

5 U.S.C. §554(d), for covered adjudications, generally prevents employees engaged in investigative or prosecuting functions in a case from participating or advising in the decision in that or a factually related case, subject to statutory exceptions.

Sections 556-557 separately structure evidence reception, burden/record issues, initial or recommended decisions, agency review and final decisions.

This provides a useful mixed witness:

- the same **agency** can contain investigation/prosecution and adjudication;
- source rules can nevertheless separate particular employees/functions;
- an initial decision and an agency-level final decision need not be the same procedural state.

Thus:

`institution identity != function identity`;

and:

`decision state != one indivisible terminal event`.

## 7. Revised meaning of `P`, `D`, and `J`

After revalidation:

- `P` = responsibility-attribution / accusation function;
- `D` = defence / counterargument / responsibility-blocking function;
- `J` = adjudicative finding / institutional-decision function.

These are **functional coordinates**, not offices.

The mapping is many-to-many:

`actors/institutions <-> functions`.

Examples:

- a prosecutor may perform investigation and exculpatory inquiry outside the narrow `P` function;
- a court may perform evidence-development functions as well as `J`;
- a victim may present an independent procedural input;
- an agency may contain several functions while internal separation rules constrain which personnel may cross them.

## 8. Revised meaning of `R_E` and `R_L`

The revalidation rejects treating each as one indivisible universal rule.

`R_E` is better understood as a family of source-supplied rules governing some combination of:

- collection;
- preservation;
- presentation;
- relevance;
- admissibility/exclusion;
- burden;
- record formation;
- court-initiated evidence.

`R_L` is better understood as a family of source-supplied rules governing some combination of:

- proof threshold;
- attribution/charge resolution;
- findings;
- initial decision;
- review;
- final decision;
- disposition/consequence.

Therefore:

`R_E` and `R_L` remain analytically useful labels, but not universal atomic modules.

## 9. World/reference versus legal record

The original non-identifiability insight survives intact.

None of the cross-jurisdiction witnesses justifies:

`historical event = source record = admitted material = institutional finding`.

Different records can be incomplete, excluded, contested, or supplemented, and the final legal finding is produced under source-defined procedural rules rather than by omniscient reconstruction of the world state.

Therefore an external analyst must not inject toy-model ground truth into the internal adjudicative record.

## 10. Relation to LAW-001

LAW-001 revalidation established:

`missing/nonperformed input != opposite formed input`,

and that downstream effects of nonaction arise through source rules.

LAW-002 now extends that rule across **regime interfaces**:

`missing/weak state in one procedural function != positive state in another function`.

For example:

`defence fails to prove an affirmative proposition != prosecution burden satisfied`;

and:

`prosecution attribution fails != factual opposite positively proved`.

Which procedural consequence follows is supplied by the governing burden, evidence and decision rules.

## 11. DSD Formation result

Formation remains useful as a typed non-totalization language after explicit legal encoding.

It can preserve:

- role-bearing distinctions;
- partial records;
- source-to-use interfaces;
- separate downstream institutional states.

But do not identify:

`legal actor = Formation channel`;

`P/D/J = Formation stages`;

`legal admissibility = Formation admission`;

`judgment = complete composite description of the historical world`.

No direct contradiction with Formation was found after the office/function distinction was corrected.

Axis-Property is not required for the refreshed core result.

Static Aggregation is not required.

Dynamics is not required.

## 12. Main negative results

Rejected:

- fixed universal seven-office taxonomy;
- prosecutor office as guilt-only `P`;
- court as passive evidence consumer;
- victim/source as prosecution component;
- one person or institution = one function;
- all systems must separate every function into different institutions;
- `R_E` and `R_L` as universally atomic rules;
- final judgment as unique reconstruction of historical truth.

## 13. Generalization status after revalidation

LAW-002 moves from:

`universal candidate under active falsification`

to:

**`cross-jurisdictionally corroborated functional-interface candidate; fixed office/module taxonomy rejected; many-to-many actor/function allocation and source-indexed separation/fusion retained; active falsification remains open`.**

The Korean criminal-procedure analysis remains a valid domestic witness and historical first pass, not the foundation of the universal claim.

## 14. Cross-case significance

LAW-002 now supplies a stronger general rule for later legal analysis:

**do not infer legal/institutional function from office title, and do not infer one regime's informational or legal state from another regime's state. Preserve the source-defined interfaces and the rules that transfer, exclude, transform or decide across them.**

This directly supports later LAW-003 role/authority analysis and LAW-004 evidence-use analysis while keeping their subjects distinct.
