## New result
Let the scalar progression field have second-order principal symbol

\[
P_\psi(x,\xi)=A^{\mu\nu}(x)\xi_\mu\xi_\nu.
\]

Let the progression geometry have Lorentzian inverse metric \(g_{\rm prog}^{\mu\nu}\). A sufficient and, for nondegenerate scalar quadratic symbols with the same Lorentzian null cone, essentially conformal condition for exact cone equality is

\[
A^{\mu\nu}=\Omega(x)g_{\rm prog}^{\mu\nu},\qquad \Omega(x)>0.
\]

The conformal factor changes the principal-symbol normalization but not its characteristic set.

In a local isotropic frame, compare

\[
-\frac{\omega^2}{c_{\rm kin}^2}+|\mathbf k|^2=0
\]

with

\[
-\frac{\omega^2}{c_\psi^2}+|\mathbf k|^2=0.
\]

Then \(c_\psi=c_{\rm kin}\) gives the same cone, \(c_\psi<c_{\rm kin}\) gives a strict subcone, and \(c_\psi>c_{\rm kin}\) gives a supercone.

Equality of only the maximum scalar speed is weaker than equality of characteristic cones. An anisotropic progression principal symbol can satisfy

\[
\sup_n c_\psi(n)=c_{\rm kin}
\]

while remaining slower in other directions.

The current DSD dynamics distinguishes PDE characteristic upper bounds from the component-resolved infimal propagation bound \(c_{\rm info}\). Therefore exact identification \(c_\psi=c_{\rm info}\) additionally requires a propagation-complete covector set, a local/support-faithful component-to-field representation, and a sharp or saturating admissible mode.

## Verdict

- common cone alone implies \(c_\psi=c_{\rm info}\): **REJECTED**
