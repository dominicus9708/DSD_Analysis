## Source selection rule

Prefer, in order:

1. primary statutory or rule text;
2. authoritative court or institutional interpretation where required;
3. official explanatory material;
4. secondary scholarship only for terminology or doctrinal context that cannot be established from the primary materials alone.

For every source record:

- jurisdiction / institution
- source title
- provision or section
- version / effective date
- retrieval date
- exact proposition used in the analysis
- whether the proposition is normative, definitional, procedural, or interpretive

## Terminology guard

Do not treat the following words as synonyms unless the selected source system does so:

- agency
- representation
- mandate
- delegation
- authorization / authority
- attribution
- ratification

Korean-language legal terms must likewise be preserved in their source-law distinctions before an English gloss or DSD comparison is added.
