# LING-008 / Global Case 021 — Plan

## Topic
Institutional language, constitutive rules, role/authority structure, delegation, jurisdiction, procedure, and institutional effect.

## Main questions
1. Does a meaningful institutional utterance determine its institutional effect?
2. Does possessing an institutional role determine every authority associated with that role?
3. Can the same role and same utterance yield different institutional outcomes when delegation, jurisdiction, or procedural conditions differ?
4. Which part is best represented by Formation, which by Axis-Property, and which must remain an external institutional bridge?

## External anchors
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Social Institutions.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Social Ontology.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Speech Acts.

## DSD scope
Use Formation to preserve typed event/channel identity and role. Use Axis-Property only where explicit tag-sensitive authority or relational structure is useful. Treat constitutive/institutional rules as application-supplied bridges; do not derive legal or social authority from DSD.
