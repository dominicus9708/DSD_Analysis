# LAW-006 Scope and Generalization Status

## Universal structural candidate

Normative status is not safely represented as one context-free total value over a bare action.

The surviving candidate is:

**physical possibility, rule applicability, obligation, permission, prohibition, exception/exemption, derogation, and consequence remain distinct unless the governing source rule or source logic licenses an identification.**

## Witness families

- ILO Forced Labour Convention No. 29 — category definition, exclusions, prohibition-related consequence, state obligations.
- ICCPR / Human Rights Committee — right, restriction, derogation, non-derogable residual constraints.
- UNIDROIT Principles — public permission, obligation to seek permission, pending/refusal consequences.
- Formal deontic logic — counterpressure against mutually exclusive or overly simple O/P/F coding.

## Generalization status

**Universal candidate supported by several independent international/transnational witness families; narrowed under formal counterpressure; active falsification remains open.**

This is stronger than a single-jurisdiction witness but weaker than a proof that every legal system uses one deontic algebra.

## DSD status

- Formation: meaningful structural bridge after typed rule-sensitive encoding.
- Axis-Property: not required.
- Static Aggregation: not required.
- Dynamics: optional for temporal norm changes only.
- Direct contradiction found: no.

## Interpretation boundary

Do not read LAW-006 as claiming:

- `O`, `P`, `F`, and exemption are four mutually exclusive universal values;
- every legal permission is merely absence of prohibition;
- every exemption is a positive permission;
- every exception flips a prohibition into its opposite;
- DSD determines which conduct ought to be legal.
