# Linguistics and Formal Semantics — Manuscript Preparation

Canonical scope: `LING-001–010`.

## Central question

How should utterance, implicature, hearer inference, speech-act success, later effect, role, authority, reference, context, and revision be kept structurally distinct, and where does DSD add a useful non-collapse audit to established pragmatics and semantics?

## Recommended angle

**Non-Collapse in Linguistic Interpretation: A DSD Audit of Utterance, Uptake, Context, and Authority**.

## Core mechanisms

- explicit utterance != implicature != hearer inference;
- utterance != illocutionary success != perlocutionary effect;
- role != power != legitimacy != compliance;
- reference/naming/context do not automatically fix every semantic or social effect;
- later correction/context can alter interpretation without rewriting the historical utterance.
