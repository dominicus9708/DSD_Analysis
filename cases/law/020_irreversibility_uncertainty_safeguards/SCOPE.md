# LAW-009 Scope

## Universal structural claim under test

Initial strong hypothesis:

`greater irreversibility -> universally stronger safeguards`.

Verdict: **falsified as stated**.

Surviving candidate:

**where a source legal regime recognizes an irreparable or effectively non-restorable risk, especially one capable of defeating the practical value of later adjudication, that risk may activate or strengthen source-defined ex ante preservation or procedural safeguards. The legal effect is not determined by irreparability alone.**

## Witness status

- Human Rights Committee / ICCPR Article 6: capital punishment + fair-trial/finality/proof safeguards.
- UN ECOSOC death-penalty safeguards: explicit capital-case protections, including protection above non-capital cases.
- ECHR Rule 39: imminent irreparable harm + exceptional interim preservation.
- ICJ provisional measures: irreparable prejudice + urgency before final decision.

These are independent institutional witness families, not one jurisdiction repeated.

## DSD mapping status

- Formation: meaningful structural correspondence after typed legal encoding.
- Structural Reorganization Dynamics: meaningful as a time-indexed carrier when the application separately supplies restoration/irreparability semantics.
- Axis-Property: no justified use.
- Static Aggregation: no justified use.

## Generalization status

- universal monotone safeguard law: rejected;
- cross-system irreparability-relevance candidate: supported, falsification open;
- normative claim that every irreversible consequence morally requires a specified procedural package: not established;
- DSD theorem about legal safeguards: not established.

## Historical significance inside the LAW sequence

LAW-009 differs from LAW-001 through LAW-008 because the initial universal hypothesis is not merely narrowed by terminology or type pressure; its monotone form is explicitly rejected.

The retained result is therefore a stronger falsification-oriented external node rather than a confirmation-only coherence result.
