# MATH-005 Case Summary

Global case: 032

## Primary verdict

**Direct correspondence with forward/inverse and support-tag boundaries.**

## What was established

1. Clause-VII uniqueness is forward definitional uniqueness, not inverse reconstruction.
2. On fixed support, arbitrary two-or-more-coordinate summation has a nontrivial kernel unless the admissible class is restricted.
3. The existing DSD difference-set/kernel theorem is the exact standard injectivity criterion.
4. Direct-sum independence is the standard special case for unique decomposition of variable components in declared channel subspaces.
5. Stage-VII recovery of a selected finite support is exactly distinct-subset-sum uniqueness, equivalently absence of nontrivial signed `{-1,0,1}` term relations.
6. Linear independence is sufficient but not necessary for Stage-VII support recovery.
7. A zero component term immediately destroys support recovery because `emptyset` and `{c}` collide.
8. Support-tagged records correctly retain the DSD distinction between absence and selected zero.
9. No contradiction with the current DSD source papers was found.

## Canonical statement

DSD finite aggregation follows standard additive reconstruction theory, but three notions of uniqueness must remain separate: unique forward closure, unique additive component decomposition, and unique channel-support reconstruction.
