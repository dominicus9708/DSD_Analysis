# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 105–109

Date: 2026-09-03

## Experiment 105 — bounded spectral discreteness

For a homothetic bounded domain

\[
\Omega_L=L\Omega_1,
\]

a Laplace-type eigenproblem has the scaling

\[
\lambda_n(L)=\frac{\xi_n}{L^2},
\]

with dimensionless spectral numbers \(\xi_n\). Hence mode lengths satisfy

\[
\ell_n\sim\frac{L}{\sqrt{\xi_n}}.
\]

Boundedness can discretize mode labels and dimensionless spectral ratios, but the generated dimensional length remains proportional to the source/domain size \(L\). No mass dimension is generated.

**Verdict:** bounded spectral discretization as a source-independent \(L/M\) anchor — **REJECTED**.

---

## Experiment 106 — discrete bounded-component count

If a bounded formation contains \(N_B\in\mathbb N\) components and identical primitive units are assumed,

\[
M=N_Bm_*,\qquad L_\Sigma=N_B\ell_*,
\]

then

\[
\frac{L_\Sigma}{M}=\frac{\ell_*}{m_*}.
\]

The integer count cancels. Discreteness of the number of bounded units does not derive the primitive mass–length ratio; \(m_*\) and \(\ell_*\) must already be physically normalized.

**Verdict:** integer bounded count creates the missing primitive mass–length scale — **REJECTED**.

---

## Experiment 107 — mode/transition spacing

Mode or transition spacing such as

\[
\Delta k\sim L^{-1},\qquad \Delta\omega\sim c/L
\]

is source-size dependent. Converting this to a physical mass scale requires another mass-bearing bridge or universal constant.

**Verdict:** spectral spacing as a standalone absolute anchor — **REJECTED**.

---

## Experiment 108 — scale-free nonlinear bounded dynamics

If the underlying equations admit a dilation symmetry and boundedness merely selects a domain size \(L\), then critical exponents or dimensionless shape data may be universal while dimensional amplitudes scale with \(L\) or with boundary/initial data.

Thus bounded-state selection may choose a branch without selecting a source-independent absolute \(L/M\) amplitude.

**Verdict:** universal exponent/branch selection without dimensional-amplitude selection — **CONFIRMED**.

---

## Experiment 109 — genuine escape routes

A genuinely new dimensionful anchor would require a mechanism not reducible to source \(M,L\), dimensionless bounded-state data, or scale-free spectral structure. Candidate classes include:

- independently fixed dimensionful boundary data;
- primitive physical units with both mass and length normalization;
- nonhomogeneous laws containing independently normalized dimensionful coefficients;
- genuine scale generation such as dimensional-transmutation/anomaly-like behavior.

Current DSD has not independently derived any such mechanism.

## Consolidated verdict

\[
\boxed{\text{bounded discreteness can generate discrete states and dimensionless spectral data, but not a source-independent }L/M\text{ amplitude by itself}.}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\text{discrete state/mode}\neq\text{quantized physical length or mass}\neq\text{universal mass–geometry conversion}.}
\]
