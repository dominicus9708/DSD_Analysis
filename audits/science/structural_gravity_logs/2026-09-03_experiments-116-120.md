# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 116–120

Date: 2026-09-03

## Experiment 116 — progression geometry speed scale

For a progression/time-rate specialization with metric schematic

\[
ds^2=-N(x)^2c_*^2dt^2+h_{ij}dx^idx^j,
\]

and \(\psi=\ln N\), the low-speed progression-gradient response has the conditional form

\[
\mathbf a\simeq-c_*^2\nabla\psi.
\]

Thus

\[
c_{\rm kin}=c_*.
\]

---

## Experiment 117 — exact condition for \(c_{\rm info}=c_{\rm kin}\)

If the structural-information principal characteristic cone coincides with the same metric causal cone and at least one admissible structural mode saturates that cone, then

\[
\boxed{c_{\rm info}=c_{\rm kin}=c_*}.
\]

A common metric alone is insufficient. If all structural modes propagate strictly inside the metric cone, then

\[
c_{\rm info}<c_{\rm kin}.
\]

**Verdict:** equality is CONDITIONAL on common cone + saturating structural mode.

## Experiment 118 — sector-specific information speed

In a multi-channel DSD dynamics the structure relevant to gravity/progression may propagate below the global structural-information upper bound. Define

\[
c_{\rm info}^{(G)}\equiv c_\psi.
\]

Then generally

\[
c_\psi\le c_{\rm info}.
\]

The source-removal bound based only on global \(c_{\rm info}\),

\[
t_{\rm onset}-t_0\ge\frac{d}{c_{\rm info}},
\]

remains a conservative weak bound, but identifying the actual update front with \(d/c_{\rm info}\) requires \(c_\psi=c_{\rm info}\).

## Experiment 119 — common-cone reduction of the static coupling

If the equality above is established and

\[
\psi\simeq\Lambda_t\frac Mr,
\]

then

\[
\mathbf a\simeq-c_{\rm info}^2\nabla\psi
\]

and

\[
K_*=c_{\rm info}^2\Lambda_t.
\]

The independent dimensionful unknown is reduced to \(\Lambda_t[L/M]\).

---

## Experiment 120 — no inference of \(c_{\rm kin}\) from timing without common-cone proof

If common-cone equality is not established, keep

\[
K_*=c_{\rm kin}^2\Lambda_t,
\qquad
v_{\rm update}\le c_\psi\le c_{\rm info}.
\]

Source-removal timing constrains \(c_\psi\), not \(c_{\rm kin}\). Inferring \(c_{\rm kin}\) from update-front speed would assume the equality that is supposed to be tested.

**Verdict:** timing-to-kinematic-speed inference without a common-cone derivation — REJECTED as circular.
