# Source notes

## DSD source lock

### Formation Axiom System
The application must preserve the following distinctions rather than collapse them:
- expression/configuration admission versus realization;
- configuration describability versus candidate existence;
- Stage-V partial assignment versus an assigned zero value;
- admitted operational channel versus channel absence;
- finite composition only after admitted channels have formed.

The paper explicitly distinguishes undefined assignment, defined zero, channel absence, and zero contribution. Domain-specific applications require additional interpretation maps.

### Axis-property system
The axis-property layer is not required for the core result. It becomes relevant only if an institutional application introduces role-sensitive or tag-sensitive properties. The paper itself distinguishes candidate, declared, undefined, defined-zero, and defined-nonzero property states.

## External source lock

### Robert's Rules of Order — abstention
Official FAQ: an abstention is a refusal to vote. Under ordinary majority/two-thirds rules based on votes cast, abstentions are not votes and do not count as votes cast. If a rule instead uses members present or entire membership, the effect on the threshold can differ, but the abstention still is not a vote.
Source: https://robertsrules.com/frequently-asked-questions/

### Robert's Rules of Order — ballot status
Official Interpretation 2006-5 distinguishes abstention-like blank/no-choice ballots from illegal votes cast by legal voters. Official Interpretation 2011-1 states that pre-11th-edition interpretations, except 2006-21, remain valid in substance.
Source: https://robertsrules.com/official-interpretations/

### UN Human Rights Committee — presumption of innocence
General Comment No. 32, paragraph 30: Article 14(2) places the burden of proving the charge on the prosecution, prohibits presuming guilt before proof beyond reasonable doubt, and gives the accused the benefit of doubt.
Source: https://docstore.ohchr.org/

### Republic of Korea Constitution
Article 27(4): the accused shall be presumed innocent until a judgment of guilt has been pronounced.
Source: https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_service/lawViewContent.do?hseq=1

## Scope note
Robert's Rules is a parliamentary authority, not a universal law of all meetings or elections. The criminal-law sources are legal norms, not mathematical consequences. The analysis therefore tests structural correspondence, not universal derivation.
