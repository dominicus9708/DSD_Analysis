# Foundational Framework for Law, Institutions, and Decision Structures

Status: **LAW-001 through LAW-014 legal foundation complete; falsification remains open**.

See `LAW_001_014_FINAL_SYNTHESIS.md` for the closure audit.

## 1. Scope and method

The primary comparison target is not one jurisdiction's positive law. National law, cases, treaties, procedures, institutional rules, scientific evidence about investigation, and official practice serve as witnesses that can support, narrow or falsify structural candidates.

Each case separates:

1. a structural candidate;
2. source/jurisdiction/institutional witnesses;
3. DSD mapping only after source analysis;
4. contradiction audit;
5. generalization status.

Analysis and design are separate. Analysis does not preselect agreement or conflict. Design avoids gratuitous conflict but does not erase actual differences for convenience.

## 2. Final predefinition rule

The strongest recurring rule is two-sided:

**do not infer an upstream identity from a downstream effect, and do not pre-fill either a positive or negative downstream state before the source or independently justified external bridge that performs the transition, transfer, attribution, exclusion, substitution, aggregation or institutional formation is established.**

Predefinition is not rejected merely because it is prior. It becomes suspect when its exclusion or promotion power is not justified for the claim under test.

## 3. Interface-graph rule

Do not force source systems into one universal stage chain.

Use, where useful:

`G = (V, E_R)`

where `V` contains source-distinct typed states and `E_R` only relations licensed by source or justified bridge rules.

Branches, bypasses, later confirmation, evidence-free/proof-substitute routes, review, non-monotonic reconstruction, savings clauses, veto/blocking rules and multi-body formation can all occur when supplied by the source.

**Stage distinction does not imply mandatory stage order.**

## 4. Non-identity rule

`A != B` does not imply `A intersection B = empty`.

Functions, responsibilities, remedies, legal statuses, individual and institutional states may overlap while remaining non-identical.

## 5. Epistemic boundary

The legal campaign preserves:

`historical event != perception != later memory != record/trace != reconstruction != institutional finding`.

`number of reports != number of independent information streams`.

An ideal complete description may be a comparison ideal, but it is not treated as accessible ground truth possessed by witnesses, investigators, courts or the DSD analyst.

Uncertainty can be a legitimate source state rather than a missing value that must be forced into YES/NO/zero.

## 6. Responsibility and institutional boundary

`same event != same responsibility basis`.

Direct conduct, ordering, superior omission, effective control, individual responsibility, official capacity and State attribution use distinct source rules where the source distinguishes them.

Likewise:

`individual position != collective decision != final institutional act`.

Institutional decisions are formed by source-defined constitutive rules such as quorum, threshold, weighting, veto/blocking, concurrence and presentment. Institutional attribution does not homogenize personal member positions.

## 7. Temporal boundary

`historical operation != current force != residual effect`.

Adoption/enactment, commencement, provisional application, event time, retroactivity, amendment/repeal/termination, transition/savings and residual states remain source-rule indexed.

Current legal status must not overwrite historical legal states merely for analytical convenience.

## 8. DSD layer discipline

### Formation

Useful for typed status, role-sensitive identity, partiality, provenance and non-totalization.

Do not identify external legal concepts with Formation admission, channel absence, undefined, defined zero, composition or stage progression without an explicit bridge.

### Axis-Property

Do not invoke merely because the source uses hierarchy, authority, command, capacity, control, weighting, severity or veto language. A realized-axis interpretation requires independent justification.

### Static Aggregation

Do not identify with evidence weight, responsibility magnitude, punishment, social cost, source reliability, collective preference or voting merely because numerical language occurs. A valid application needs an independently justified common codomain, measurement/dependence model and operator.

### Dynamics

May represent source-defined temporal states but does not generate legal, empirical, cognitive or institutional transition rules. Legal retroactivity is not backward physical causation; repeal is not structural deletion; investigation is not a mandatory monotone stage chain.

## 9. Direct contradiction status

No direct contradiction with the currently used DSD core axiomatic layers was established by LAW-001 through LAW-014 after explicit type, role, regime and bridge correction.

This is not proof that DSD is correct or that law confirms DSD. Strong DSD-style application assumptions were repeatedly falsified or narrowed, including fixed office modules, binary authority-effect models, linear evidence pipelines, monotone irreversibility rules, exact-sensor investigation, automatic upward responsibility transfer, temporal overwrite, and generic collective aggregation.
