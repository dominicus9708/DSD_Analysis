# LAW-002 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Plan

Status: active cross-jurisdiction falsification refresh.

Original historical `RESULT.md` is preserved.

## 1. Candidate under test

The earlier case used:

`W + S + P + D + J + R_E + R_L`.

For revalidation these symbols are treated as **functions/interfaces**, not as a universal seven-office architecture:

- `W` — event/reference layer;
- `S` — information/evidence-source or record layer;
- `P` — responsibility-attribution / accusation function;
- `D` — defence / counterargument function;
- `J` — adjudicative finding / decision function;
- `R_E` — source-supplied evidence/ground-use rules;
- `R_L` — source-supplied responsibility/decision/consequence rules.

## 2. Main falsification questions

Test whether any of the following stronger forms fail:

- every legal system has a prosecutor, defence and judge as three separate offices;
- a prosecutor is only a guilt-construction function;
- a court/judge only receives evidence and never helps develop the evidentiary record;
- victims/witnesses belong to the prosecution regime;
- evidence-use rules form one single module;
- responsibility/decision rules form one single module;
- one institutional body cannot contain multiple functions;
- one person cannot occupy more than one source-side procedural role;
- the adjudicative record uniquely reconstructs the historical world state.

## 3. Independent witness families

Use at least:

1. ICC Rome Statute — Prosecutor truth-seeking duties, accused rights, victim participation, Chamber and evidence rules;
2. German StPO — prosecution duty to investigate incriminating and exonerating circumstances; court's ex officio evidence-taking duty;
3. ECHR Article 6 — independent/impartial tribunal and defence guarantees, while prosecution is a party rather than the deciding tribunal;
4. US Administrative Procedure Act — separation of investigative/prosecuting from decisional personnel in covered adjudications while allowing layered agency/presiding-employee decision structures.

Korean criminal procedure remains an earlier domestic witness, not the foundation of universality.

## 4. Expected narrowing

The fixed `5+2` decomposition may be too rigid as universal institutional taxonomy.

A likely surviving form is:

**when a source process distinguishes event/reference, records/sources, attribution or accusation, response/defence, evidentiary usability, adjudicative finding, and decision/consequence rules, preserve those distinctions and their interfaces; do not infer a one-to-one mapping between offices and functions.**

## 5. DSD boundary

Formation may support typed role/regime separation and non-totalization after explicit legal encoding.

Do not identify legal roles with Formation stages or channels.

Axis-Property is not required merely because an actor has procedural powers.

Static Aggregation and Dynamics are not expected to be necessary for this refresh.
