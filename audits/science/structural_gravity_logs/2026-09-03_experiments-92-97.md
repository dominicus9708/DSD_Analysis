# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 92–97

Date: 2026-09-03

## Experiment 92 — Relative progression-rate realization

A possible physical realization of the canonical progression scalar is

\[
\psi(x;S)=\ln\frac{\Gamma(x;S)}{\Gamma_\infty},
\]

where \(\Gamma>0\) is a supplied local progression/update/clock-rate observable and \(\Gamma_\infty\) is its reference exterior value.
This gives a dimensionless \(\psi\) without introducing a normalization coefficient into the field definition.

The current DSD core does not supply a canonical positive rate \(\Gamma\); such a quantity would belong to a structural-gravity physical specialization.

**Verdict:** interpretable candidate, not presently derived.

## Experiment 93 — Dimensionless-field source theorem

If a dimensionless far-field progression scalar has

\[
\psi(r;M)\sim\frac{A(M)}r
\]

and independent-source additivity plus regularity gives

\[
A(M_1+M_2)=A(M_1)+A(M_2)
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
A(M)=\Lambda_tM,
\]

then dimensional consistency forces

\[
[\Lambda_t]=L/M.
\]

Defining \(\psi\) as a relative rate therefore does not remove the missing mass-to-length normalization.

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED.

## Experiment 94 — Bounded-source candidates retain the previous scaling obstruction

Any attempt to construct

\[
\Lambda_t=\frac{L_B}{M_B}F(\Pi)
\]

from source-specific bounded length and mass data inherits the earlier homothetic scaling problem unless the participating mass satisfies a universal linear law \(M_B\propto L_B\).
Dimensionless density-shape or structural factors \(F(\Pi)\) cannot supply the missing absolute \(L/M\) scale.

**Verdict:** previous source-specific \(L/M\) no-go survives the progression-field reformulation.

## Experiment 95 — Structural propagation speed and kinematic conversion speed

Retain two distinct speed scales:

- \(c_{\rm info}\): infimal bound for structural distinguishability-support propagation;
- \(c_{\rm kin}\): speed scale entering geometry-to-probe-motion response.

Write the canonical slow response as

\[
\mathbf a\simeq-c_{\rm kin}^2\nabla\psi.
\]

For source removal or local source change,

\[
t_{\rm onset}(x)-t_0
\ge
\frac{\operatorname{dist}(x,\operatorname{supp}\Delta S)}{c_{\rm info}}.
\]

The static response normalization and update-front timing are logically distinct.

**Verdict:** separation CONFIRMED as the safer default architecture.

## Experiment 96 — Equality of the two speeds

A conditional identification

\[
c_{\rm kin}=c_{\rm info}
\]

requires at least:

1. the same localization/time carrier for structural propagation and probe kinematics;
2. the same causal or characteristic cone;
3. a relevant structural mode saturating the infimal propagation bound.

Without these conditions the two speeds remain distinct.

**Verdict:** UNRESOLVED; common-cone+saturation criterion retained.

## Experiment 97 — Progression-field normalization non-identifiability

With

\[
\psi(r)=\Lambda_t\frac{M}{r}+\cdots,
\]

an abstract rescaling of the physical mass bridge

\[
\mathcal M'=\beta\mathcal M
\]

combined with

\[
\Lambda_t'=\frac{\Lambda_t}{\beta}
\]

leaves \(\Lambda_t\mathcal M\), and therefore \(\psi\), unchanged.
Hence current structural/describability data can determine at most the product unless an independent mass–geometry normalization breaks this degeneracy.

If physical mass is calibrated externally, \(\Lambda_t\) can be measured from geometric response, but that is not an internal derivation.

**Verdict:** strong non-identifiability result.
