# LAW-003 Cross-Jurisdiction Contradiction Audit

Status: complete for current witness set.

## C1 — Authority defect should imply no effect

**Pressure:** UNIDROIT apparent authority and ratification, VCLT Article 8 subsequent confirmation, and ILC Article 7 ultra vires State attribution all show routes where lack/excess of ordinary authority does not force one permanent downstream result.

**Result:** the strong binary rule is falsified.

Surviving rule:

`authority defect != one universal effect consequence`.

The effect/attribution follows the governing source rule.

## C2 — Actual authority should automatically imply every related effect

**Pressure:** UNIDROIT makes authority scope-sensitive; VCLT Article 7 assigns different representative functions different treaty purposes.

**Result:** rejected.

`has some authority != has authority for every act/purpose`.

## C3 — Same human/official should imply same legal role or capacity

**Pressure:** a natural person can act privately, as agent, officer, diplomatic representative, State organ, or in another source-defined capacity.

ILC Article 7 commentary explicitly distinguishes official-capacity conduct from private conduct by a person who happens to be an official.

**Result:** the original non-collapse survives and is strengthened.

## C4 — Unauthorized act should be encoded as absent

**Pressure:** UNIDROIT and VCLT both regulate an act already performed before ratification/confirmation.

**Result:** rejected.

`act occurrence != present legal effect`.

A legally ineffective or not-yet-effective act is not thereby a nonexistent event.

## C5 — State attribution should track valid internal authority exactly

**Pressure:** ILC Article 7 attributes official-capacity conduct even where authority was exceeded or instructions contravened.

**Result:** rejected as a universal rule.

`internal validity/authority != international attribution`.

This is a particularly strong warning against predefining attribution from an authority flag.

## C6 — Later confirmation means the original act was actually authorized

**Pressure:** UNIDROIT Article 2.2.9 and VCLT Article 8 expressly distinguish later ratification/confirmation from original authorization.

**Result:** rejected.

The source may assign retroactive or equivalent legal effects without rewriting the historical authority state.

## C7 — DSD Formation role coordinate should determine legal authority

**Pressure:** Formation only requires an admitted role as part of channel identity. It contains no agency-law, treaty-representation, organ-attribution, apparent-authority, or ratification rule.

**Result:** no direct mapping.

`rho` can preserve an application-supplied role tag, but it does not generate legal authority or attribution.

## C8 — ILC Article 7 contradicts DSD because effect can exist without valid authority

**Result:** no direct contradiction.

The conflict appears only if the application silently identifies Formation channel admission or role with legal authority and then assumes a universal authority-to-effect law. Current Formation does not impose that legal bridge. Its Stage-VI channel identity retains configuration, item, quantity kind, assigned value and role, and distinct roles can yield distinct channels; the legal attribution rule remains external.

## C9 — Ratification requires Dynamics and therefore Formation is insufficient

**Result:** layer distinction, not contradiction.

Formation can describe fixed pre- and post-ratification slices. A time-indexed treatment may use Dynamics for lineage/transition bookkeeping, but the legal rule that ratification changes effect — including any retroactive effect — is source-supplied, not derived by Dynamics.

## Final contradiction judgment

No direct contradiction with the current Formation system was found after actor, role/capacity, authority scope, act occurrence, attribution/effect, rule source and time were typed separately.

The stronger naive application model was materially falsified in multiple places. This is a design correction, not a repair of the external law to fit DSD.
