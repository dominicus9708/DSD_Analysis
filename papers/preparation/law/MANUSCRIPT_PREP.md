# Law, Institutions, and Decision — Manuscript Preparation

Canonical scope: `LAW-001–014`.

## Central question

How should formation, validity, legal effect, authority, evidential state, presumption, rule conflict, responsibility, remedy, and collective decision attribution be structurally separated, and what can DSD add as an audit framework without pretending to derive substantive law?

## Recommended angle

**A Structural Audit of Legal Formation, Authority, Evidence, and Decision Attribution with DSD**.

## Core mechanisms

- procedural formation != validity != effect;
- role != authority != resulting legal effect;
- lack of established evidence != establishment of the opposite fact;
- later judgment does not retroactively create the historical state it evaluates;
- rule conflict, exception, priority, and invalidity are distinct relations;
- individual vote/opinion != institutional decision != attribution;
- responsibility, sanction, restoration, emotion, and social cost must not be collapsed.

## Source freeze

Pin jurisdiction, clause, case, date, and official source. Keep Korean, U.S., international, and supranational sources jurisdiction-specific unless a separate comparative-law argument is supplied.

## Overclaim guards

DSD does not itself determine legality, guilt, constitutionality, or the universal validity of one jurisdiction's doctrine.
