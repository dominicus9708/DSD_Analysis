## Experiment 345 — Dimensionless spherical reduction

Let the bounded spherical source have radius \(R\), total matter mass \(M\), mean density

\[
\bar\rho=\frac{3M}{4\pi R^3},
\]

and normalized radial density shape

\[
d(s)=\rho(Rs)/\bar\rho,
\qquad s=r/R,
\]

so that

\[
3\int_0^1d(s)s^2\,ds=1.
\]

Define the dimensionless compactness parameter

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon=\frac{M}{4\pi\mu_0R}
}.
\]

For a regular fundamental solution write

\[
u(s)=\frac{w(s)}s,
\qquad
w(0)=0,\quad w'(0)=1.
\]

The interior equation becomes

\[
\boxed{
w''+\frac{3\epsilon}{2}d(s)w=0
}.
\]

Outside the source, \(U\) is harmonic and normalized by \(U\to1\):

\[
U_{\rm out}=1+\frac{c}{s},
\qquad c=C/R.

If the physical interior solution is \(U=A\,u\), matching at \(s=1\) gives

\[
A\,w'(1)=1.
\]

Therefore, with

\[
D(\epsilon):=w'(1;\epsilon),
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{A=1/D},
\qquad
\boxed{c=\frac{w(1)}{D}-1}.

## Experiment 346 — Criticality is the first weighted Sturm–Liouville eigenvalue

The normalization fails exactly when

\[
D(\epsilon)=w'(1)=0.
\]

Set

\[
\lambda=\frac{3\epsilon}{2}.
\]

The critical equation is

\[
-w''=\lambda d(s)w,
\qquad
w(0)=0,
\qquad
w'(1)=0.
\]

Thus the first critical point is the first weighted Dirichlet–Neumann Sturm–Liouville eigenvalue \(\lambda_1[d]\):

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon_c[d]=\frac{2}{3}\lambda_1[d]
}.
\]

The first eigenfunction is strictly positive on \(0<s\le1\). Consequently the positive branch \(U=e^{-\psi/2}>0\) exists below the first threshold and cannot be continued through it as a finite positive normalized solution.

### Variational form

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_1[d]
=
\min_{w(0)=0}
\frac{\int_0^1(w')^2ds}
{\int_0^1d(s)w^2ds}

\]

The Neumann condition at \(s=1\) is the natural boundary condition of the minimizer.

### Verdict

- finite compactness threshold: **CONFIRMED mathematically within the branch**
- threshold depends only on total mass: **REJECTED**
- density profile enters through the first weighted eigenvalue: **CONFIRMED**

The sign is therefore profile/mode dependent; `greater central concentration always lowers the threshold` is not claimed universally. It is confirmed for the present two-zone family.

## Experiments 356–358 — Axis geometry and first-order shift of the collapse threshold

The exact transform also works with a static axis metric:

\[
\Delta_{h_A}\psi
=\frac\rho{\mu_0}+\frac12|\nabla\psi|_{h_A}^2
\]

implies

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{h_A}U+\frac\rho{2\mu_0}U=0
}.
\]

For a localized metric that becomes Euclidean outside the source, the critical state can be viewed as a zero-energy resonance: a regular interior solution matched to an exterior \(U\propto1/r\) with no constant term.

A corresponding whole-space Rayleigh quotient is schematically

\[
\eta_1[h_A,\hat\rho]
=
\inf_u
\frac{\int h_A^{ij}\partial_i u\partial_j u\,dV_{h_A}}
{\int \hat\rho\,u^2\,dV_{h_A}},
\]

where \(\hat\rho\) is the dimensionless fixed-mass source shape and \(u\) decays at infinity.

For

\[
h_A=e^{\beta_A\mathcal A},
\qquad
\det h_A=1,
\]

the first-order metric contribution is

\[
\delta\eta_1
\propto
-\beta_A
\int \mathcal A^{ij}\partial_i u_0\partial_j u_0\,d^3x.
\]

If the matter source is spherical and

\[
\mathcal A^{ij}=a(r)S^{ij},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}S=0,
\]

then \(u_0=u_0(r)\) and

\[
\int_{S^2}S_{ij}n^in^j\,d\Omega
=\frac{4\pi}{3}\operatorname{tr}S=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\delta\epsilon_c^{(1)}=0
}
\]

for a pure quadrupolar axis anisotropy around a spherical baseline.

This creates a clean separation:

- axis anisotropy can generate an exterior quadrupole at **first order** in \(\beta_A\);
- the spherical monopole-collapse threshold is unchanged at **first order** and can shift only at second order or through coupling to a non-spherical/radially modified source.

The same orthogonality argument applies to a pure \(l=2\) density perturbation around a spherical baseline: it can change external multipoles at first order while its first-order shift of the s-wave critical eigenvalue vanishes.

### Verdict

- axis geometry can shift the nonlinear critical threshold in general: **CONDITIONAL**
- pure traceless quadrupolar axis anisotropy shifts the spherical threshold at first order: **REJECTED**
- far-field quadrupole and collapse-threshold shift are the same observable sector: **REJECTED**
