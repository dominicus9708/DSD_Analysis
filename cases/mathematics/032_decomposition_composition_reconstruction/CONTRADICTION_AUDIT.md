# Contradiction Audit

## Checks

1. **Forward closure vs inverse reconstruction**
   - Preserved. Clause-VII uniqueness is not reinterpreted as injectivity.

2. **Fixed-support theorem**
   - Preserved. The exact DSD criterion remains `(A_F-A_F) intersect ker S_F={0}`.

3. **Direct-sum language**
   - Treated only as an additional structured specialization where channel coordinates are constrained to declared subspaces `U_c`.
   - Not attributed to the Formation or Static Aggregation axioms.

4. **Stage-VII support recovery**
   - Derived from the actual finite-set carrier and fixed term map.
   - Exact condition is absence of nontrivial signed `{-1,0,1}` relations, not full linear independence.

5. **Channel identity**
   - Retained. Equal term values do not identify channels.

6. **Absence vs zero contribution**
   - Retained. A zero term yields the explicit collision `emptyset` versus `{c}` and therefore demonstrates why support tags are required.

7. **Varying support**
   - No fixed-support injectivity theorem is improperly extended across different domains.

8. **Typed property records**
   - Multi-input property records remain distinct from formation-channel terms; their aggregate reconstruction uses the separate property map.

## Result

No contradiction with the current Formation Axiom System, Axis Property Axiom System, or Channel-Indexed Static Aggregation paper was found.

The main clarification is terminological and structural: forward definitional uniqueness, unique additive decomposition, and support reconstruction are three distinct notions and require different hypotheses.
