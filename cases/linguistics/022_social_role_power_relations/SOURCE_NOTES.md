# LING-009 / Global Case 022 — Source Notes

## External sources

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Social Institutions
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/social-institutions/

Relevant points:
- institutions contain differentiated roles, rules, interdependence, hierarchy, different degrees of authority, and power relations;
- institutional structure can both constrain and enable action;
- role occupants retain discretion and rules require interpretation/application;
- institutional relations need not reduce to individual role labels.

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Authority
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/authority/

Relevant points:
- de facto authority is distinguished from normative authority;
- normative authority itself can be analyzed in different ways, including justified direction/enforcement, normative power to impose duties, and a right to rule;
- observed obedience therefore does not by itself establish normative legitimacy.

## DSD source basis

### Formation Axiom System
Operational channels retain `(p,a,lambda,v,rho)`, so role is part of channel identity.

### Axis-Property Axiom System
Section 6 defines binary tagged-axis relations on ordered pairs and higher-order/mixed property records. Symmetry or antisymmetry requires an additional declared condition; binary profile length alone does not make a relation symmetric.

This is important for social authority because `Authority(s,h)` and `Authority(h,s)` must not be identified merely because the same two actors participate.

### Static Aggregation
The downstream static paper explicitly preserves ordered input tuples unless symmetry/antisymmetry has been independently established. Binary/higher-order property records remain attached to their complete typed inputs unless an extra allocation rule is supplied.

## Boundary
The external sources establish that authority/power and institutional roles are relation-sensitive and theoretically plural. They do not establish that DSD is the correct social ontology. DSD is tested only as a structure-preserving host for those distinctions.
