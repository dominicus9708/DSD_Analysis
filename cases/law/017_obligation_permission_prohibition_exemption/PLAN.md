# LAW-006 / Global Case 017 — Obligation, Permission, Prohibition, and Exemption

Status: analysis in progress.

## 1. Universal-candidate question

Test whether legal/normative status should be modeled by a single global value such as `OBLIGATORY / PERMITTED / PROHIBITED / EXEMPT`, or whether the source structure requires a rule-, actor-, context-, time-, and scope-indexed normative relation.

The initial candidate is deliberately weak:

**physical/causal possibility, legal permission, legal obligation, legal prohibition, and exception/exemption status must not be identified without an explicit normative rule.**

This does not assume that the deontic categories are pairwise disjoint.

## 2. Counterpressure before witness selection

Check at least the following:

1. Standard deontic systems often validate `O(A) -> P(A)`, so `obligatory` and `permitted` cannot universally be treated as mutually exclusive values.
2. Strong permission may cover acts that are permitted but not obligatory.
3. A legal exception can work by removing an act from a prohibition's scope rather than by assigning an opposite global value.
4. An exemption/waiver may remove an obligation without creating a positive duty to perform the opposite act.
5. One action token can have different statuses for different actors, purposes, jurisdictions, times, or rule sources.
6. Normative conflict is possible in real or formal systems; DSD Analysis must not repair an inconsistent source regime by silently choosing one value.

## 3. Witness families

Use independent witness families rather than one country's positive law:

- ILO Forced Labour Convention No. 29: prohibition/definition, exclusions/exceptions, state obligations, punishability.
- ICCPR / Human Rights Committee: rights, restrictions, derogations, non-derogable boundaries.
- UNIDROIT Principles on public permission: permission requirement, party obligation to obtain permission, no-decision/refusal consequences.
- Formal deontic logic literature as counterpressure on naive mutually-exclusive coding.

## 4. DSD questions

- Does Formation help preserve unformed, inapplicable, or rule-conditioned statuses without equating them with zero or absence?
- Must the application carrier be a typed normative-act instance rather than the bare physical action?
- Is Axis-Property required? Only if a realized-axis semantics is independently supplied.
- Is Static Aggregation relevant? Presumptively no.
- Is Dynamics needed? Only if norm changes, derogations, waivers, temporal permissions, or revocations are modeled explicitly.

## 5. Falsification target

Attempt to falsify these stronger claims:

- every act has exactly one global deontic value;
- permission is simply absence of prohibition in every legal system;
- obligation and permission are universally disjoint;
- exception/exemption is identical to positive permission;
- prohibited means physically impossible;
- lack of permission is identical to prohibition;
- a source conflict can be normalized by choosing one status without a priority rule.

## 6. Completion condition

The case is complete only after:

1. source-side witnesses are recorded;
2. the universal claim is narrowed under counterpressure;
3. a typed model and finite witness are built;
4. direct contradiction with DSD is audited;
5. generalization status is stated separately from witness completion.
