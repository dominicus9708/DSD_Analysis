## BENCH-C02 — Hume's Missing Shade of Blue

Path: `benchmarks/prospective/C02_hume_missing_shade/`

Branch: `benchmark/c02-hume-missing-shade-prospective`

Prediction seal: `0a47759cd395a5d7ebd6c265d11a180d917e27c4`

Blind integrity: **clean under the project retrieval protocol**.

Before the seal only Hume's original relevant passage and project-internal DSD sources were used. Dedicated Missing-Shade objection/reply literature was opened only after the commit.

### Sealed predictions

DSD predicted that the mature issue would require distinctions among:

- absence of the exact corresponding impression and availability of neighboring/relational formation support;
- detection of the gap and possession of the exact qualitative missing idea;
- the ordered gradation as substantive relational input;
- simple content and non-derived formation history;
- a restricted counterexample and wholesale rejection of Hume's broader empirical project.

### Post-seal result

The literature independently contains close or direct versions of all of these fault lines:

- SEP's mental-mixing treatment;
- Fogelin's resemblance/color-space gap treatment;
- Cummins' recognitional-capacity interpretation;
- Garrett's natural-resemblance route;
- Kelahan's theoretical-posits treatment of simple ideas;
- Finch's empirical test of actual shade reconstruction;
- Earp's defense of the larger empirical project even if the case is a true exception.

Validation classification:

- Prediction 1: **C1**;
- Prediction 2: **C1/C2**;
- Prediction 3: **C1**;
- Prediction 4: **C1/C2**;
- Prediction 5: **C1**;
- major miss: **C3** — the seal did not explicitly distinguish the resemblance thesis from the causal thesis within Hume's first principle, a distinction found in later scholarship;
- C4: **none**;
- new philosophical solution: **no**.

Overall classification:

**positive clean prospective validation**.

BENCH-C02 is the project's **first clean Mode-C validation record under the explicit retrieval protocol**. It demonstrates successful pre-literature problem localization, not novelty and not proof of the DSD axioms.
