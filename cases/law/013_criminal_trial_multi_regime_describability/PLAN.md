# LAW-002 / Global Case 013 — Criminal-Trial Multi-Regime Describability

## Primary purpose

Structural reinterpretation / application.

## Secondary purposes

- coherence
- predefinition / premature-promotion audit
- regime-boundary analysis

## Question

Can a criminal trial be decomposed into distinct descriptive regimes without collapsing the world, evidence sources, prosecution, defence, and court into one omniscient description?

## Minimal 5+2 model

1. `W` — world/event reference layer; not itself a describer.
2. `S` — victim / witness / physical-trace evidence-source layer.
3. `P` — prosecution descriptive regime.
4. `D` — defendant/defence descriptive regime.
5. `J` — judicial descriptive regime.
6. `R_E` — evidence-admissibility / evidentiary-use rules.
7. `R_L` — legal decision rules, including presumption of innocence and proof standard where supplied by the legal system.

## Anti-overreach rules

- Do not identify truth in `W` with a legal finding in `J`.
- Do not identify a victim statement with truth merely because it is sincere or legally receivable.
- Do not treat the victim as a party symmetric to prosecutor and defendant; preserve the legal regime's actual procedural role.
- Do not infer factual innocence from failure to prove guilt.
- Do not claim DSD derives the burden of proof, beyond-reasonable-doubt standard, or evidence rules.
- Do not identify legal admissibility with DSD structural admission; the correspondence is application-specific.
- Do not treat cross-regime information transfer as already supplied by the Formation Axiom System. Any bridge must be explicit.

## DSD questions

1. Does the Formation system's staged structure help separate candidate material, realized submission, legally usable evidence, fact finding, and judgment?
2. Does the role coordinate in channel identity help preserve prosecutor/defendant/victim/court distinctions without requiring Axis-Property machinery?
3. Where do tag-sensitive Axis-Property records become useful, and where are they unnecessary?
4. Can one regime's nonformation or missing description be illegitimately totalized into another regime's positive conclusion?
5. Can two distinct world states yield the same admitted evidentiary state, establishing non-identifiability from the court record alone?

## Expected result classes

- direct structural correspondence
- correspondence after explicit application encoding
- non-correspondence / DSD boundary
- actual conflict

## Reproducibility

Use a finite witness table rather than code unless calculation becomes necessary. The witness must preserve:

- world state
- evidence-source output
- prosecution description
- defence challenge/description
- evidence-filter result
- judicial evidentiary state
- verdict state

No empirical claim is inferred from the finite witness.
