# PHIL-001 Follow-up — Serious Refutation Targets for the Zombie Argument

Status: **attack roadmap only; no refutation result is precommitted**.

This document records the post-audit cleanup after PHIL-001. The first-pass DSD-friendly premise-loading attack failed against Chalmers's mature formulation. A serious follow-up must therefore target the surviving inferential bridges rather than the narrative label "zombie" or implementation details that alter the physical base.

## 1. Attack axes explicitly retired

The following should not be used as primary objections to Chalmers's mature zombie argument.

### 1.1 Naming objection

The term `zombie` is historically vivid but philosophically nonessential. Whether the same model is called a zombie, unconscious physical duplicate, or another label does not change the argument's logical structure.

**Status:** stylistic/historical observation only; not an inferential objection.

### 1.2 Shared-neural-network / hive-mind replacement

A body whose behavior is produced through a shared nervous system, remote neural link, colony-level controller, parasite, external AI controller, or other extra causal structure is not an exact physical duplicate of an ordinary human world.

Such models are useful for intuition about the difference between behavioral output and the locus of experience, but they modify the physical/causal base and therefore do not directly refute the exact-duplicate zombie premise.

**Status:** explanatory comparison model only; not a direct countermodel.

### 1.3 Transmission speed, latency, signal mechanism, or why the network is connected

Questions about hormonal/electrical transmission, communication delay, bandwidth, synchronization, or network formation concern implementation details of a different physical system. They do not engage the core modal argument once exact physical duplication is stipulated.

**Status:** meta-level implementation questions; excluded from the core refutation route.

### 1.4 Simple premise-loading charge

The mature Chalmers formulation does not merely define a zombie and infer metaphysical possibility from the definition. It distinguishes prima facie from ideal conceivability, primary from secondary evaluation, and explicitly defends modal bridge principles.

**Status:** rejected by PHIL-001 as a blanket criticism.

## 2. Surviving target structure

A more faithful attack must distinguish the following transitions:

`P & ~Q scenario`

`-> a priori / ideal coherence`

`-> epistemically possible scenario`

`-> corresponding metaphysically possible world`

`-> anti-materialist consequence`.

The strongest targets are not the scenario label but the soundness of these promotions.

## 3. Primary target A — ideal conceivability of P & ~Q

Let `P` be the relevant exhaustive physical description and `Q` a phenomenal truth.

The mature argument requires more than the weak claim that `P & ~Q` is easy to imagine. It requires an idealized a priori coherence claim.

A serious refutation can therefore ask:

1. What independent criterion establishes that `P & ~Q` remains coherent under ideal rational reflection?
2. Does our inability to derive `Q` from a physical description show genuine ideal coherence, or only a limitation of our concepts and inferential access?
3. Could phenomenal and physical concepts be cognitively non-interderivable while still co-referring to one underlying property/state?
4. What would count as discovering a hidden contradiction in `P & ~Q`, and is that test operationally or formally specified?

### DSD-compatible structural pressure

A difference between two descriptions, channels, or inferential routes does not by itself establish a difference in the underlying structure they concern.

Therefore the inference pattern

`descriptive / epistemic non-derivability -> structural / ontological independence`

must be separately justified.

This is only a methodological comparison. DSD describability must not be identified with phenomenal conceivability.

## 4. Primary target B — modal rationalism bridge

A central bridge in the 2D reconstruction is roughly:

`epistemically possible coherent scenario -> corresponding metaphysically possible world`.

This is a major attack point.

A serious refutation should demand either:

- a soundness theorem or independent argument showing why the relevant epistemic scenario space maps into metaphysical possibility space; or
- a reason why counterexamples based on necessary a posteriori identities, strong necessities, causal essentialism, or reference-fixing do not apply.

The relevant question is not whether a bridge is explicitly stated — PHIL-001 established that it is — but whether the bridge is truth-preserving.

## 5. Primary target C — epistemic gap versus ontological gap

A physicalist may concede:

`P does not a priori yield Q for us`

while denying:

`there is a metaphysically possible world with P and ~Q`.

This is the general Type-B materialist / phenomenal-concept pressure.

A DSD-style audit should test whether the argument illegitimately promotes

`different mode of access / description`

into

`different admissible underlying structure`.

A useful counter-architecture would preserve one underlying state while allowing two representational systems that are not a priori mutually recoverable.

If such architectures are coherent in general, Chalmers must show why phenomenal concepts are special enough that epistemic separation in this case tracks metaphysical separation.

## 6. Secondary target D — semantic stability in the 2D route

The two-dimensional argument relies on relations between epistemic/primary and subjunctive/secondary intensions.

A serious attack can ask whether the relevant physical and phenomenal terms are sufficiently semantically stable across the scenarios/worlds used in the inference.

This is a secondary target because Chalmers has developed fallback arguments involving diagonalized phenomenal properties, so defeating one simple stability formulation may not defeat the whole anti-materialist route.

## 7. Secondary target E — what physicalism is required to entail

Another route is to challenge the claim that physicalism must provide the relevant a priori entailment from the physical base to phenomenal truths.

A physicalist may instead hold that the dependence is metaphysically necessary but epistemically a posteriori.

The attack question becomes:

`Why must metaphysical dependence be mirrored by ideal a priori deducibility from the chosen description base?`

This target connects the zombie debate to broader disputes over scrutability, conceptual analysis, and necessary a posteriori identities.

## 8. Candidate DSD-specific refutation schema

The most promising DSD-specific contribution is not a new theory of consciousness. It is a general separation test.

Construct a class of cases with:

- one underlying structure `S`;
- two admissible descriptors or access modes `D1(S)` and `D2(S)`;
- no a priori reconstruction procedure from `D1` to `D2` available within the chosen descriptive regime;
- nevertheless no second underlying structure `S'` is thereby licensed.

Then test the inference schema:

`D1 does not a priori determine D2`

`therefore`

`there is a possible S' with the same D1 but different D2`.

If the schema fails in a controlled structural setting, it does **not** refute Chalmers by itself. It instead shifts the burden to an additional premise explaining why phenomenal description is a special case where the promotion from epistemic separation to metaphysical variation is valid.

This would be a cleaner DSD attack than trying to engineer a biologically different zombie.

## 9. Falsification conditions for the follow-up attack

The DSD follow-up should be abandoned or narrowed if any of the following is established:

1. Chalmers supplies a sufficiently independent argument that ideal primary conceivability is a reliable and complete guide to the relevant metaphysical possibility in this case.
2. The epistemic-gap/ontological-gap counter-architecture cannot be made coherent without already assuming physicalism.
3. The physicalist alternative merely asserts necessary identity without explaining why the modal epistemology is misleading.
4. A DSD structural countermodel depends on features absent from the zombie argument and therefore only changes the subject.

## 10. Recommended order of attack

1. **Attack Premise 2 / ideal coherence of `P & ~Q`.**
2. **Attack the modal-rationalist bridge from epistemic scenario to metaphysical world.**
3. **Construct a descriptive-nonrecoverability-without-ontological-variation counter-architecture.**
4. **Compare the result with phenomenal concept strategy / Type-B materialism.**
5. Only then test semantic stability and the definition/requirements of physicalism.

This order keeps the attack on the actual inferential burden and avoids narrative, biological, or implementation-level distractions.

## 11. Current non-verdict

No claim that the zombie argument has been refuted is recorded here.

The established result is narrower:

- the first premise-loading attack failed;
- several tempting implementation objections are off-level;
- the next serious pressure point is the promotion from epistemic/descriptive separation to metaphysical/ontological separation.
