# LAW-013 Source Notes

## 1. Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties

### Article 24 — entry into force

Treaty entry into force occurs in the manner/date the treaty provides or States agree; a State joining later can have a different effective date. Some treaty provisions concerning authentication, consent, entry into force and related pre-entry matters operate from adoption.

Witness:

`text adopted != consent established != treaty in force for every State at same time`.

### Article 25 — provisional application

A treaty or part may apply provisionally before entry into force if the treaty or negotiating States so provide.

Witness:

`not yet fully in force != no legal operation of any kind`.

### Article 28 — non-retroactivity

Unless a different intention appears, treaty provisions do not bind a party regarding acts/facts before entry into force or situations that ceased before that date.

Witness:

`current treaty rule != automatic past applicability`.

### Article 70 — termination

Termination releases future performance but, absent a different treaty rule/agreement, does not affect rights, obligations or legal situations created through prior execution.

Witness:

`terminated now != historical operation erased`.

## 2. Canada Interpretation Act

Section 43 states that repeal does not ordinarily revive prior law, affect previous operation, accrued rights/privileges/obligations/liabilities, offences/penalties, or related investigations/proceedings/remedies; such proceedings can continue as if the enactment had not been repealed.

Section 44 supplies separate rules for repeal and substitution, including continuation of proceedings and, in specified circumstances, mitigation where a new punishment is reduced.

Witness:

`repeal != deletion of all prior legal states`.

## 3. United States Ex Post Facto doctrine

Congress's Constitution Annotated explains that the Ex Post Facto Clauses prohibit retroactive criminal/penal legislation of specified disadvantageous kinds, but do not prohibit retroactive legislation generally. The doctrine is bounded by field and effect.

Witness:

`non-retroactivity rule != one universal all-law prohibition`.

## 4. ECHR Article 7

The 2026 Article 7 Guide states that criminal law may not be applied retrospectively to an accused's disadvantage and applies the principle to offence definitions and penalties; later legal change must be compared in the specific temporal context.

Witness:

`later law != automatic governing law for earlier conduct`.

## 5. Combined temporal distinctions

- later-law mitigation/favourability rule where supplied.
