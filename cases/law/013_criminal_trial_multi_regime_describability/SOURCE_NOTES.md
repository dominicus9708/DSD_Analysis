## External legal sources

### Republic of Korea Constitution, Article 27(4)-(5)

- Article 27(4): an accused is presumed innocent until a judgment of guilt has been pronounced.
- Article 27(5): a crime victim has a right to make a statement during the trial under conditions prescribed by law.

Source: Korean Legal Research Institute English statute service / Constitution of the Republic of Korea.

### Korean Criminal Procedure Act — current evidence structure

Current text effective 2026-07-01:

- Article 293: the presiding judge asks the defendant's opinion on evidence examination results and informs the defendant of the right to request evidence examination needed to protect rights.
- Article 294: prosecutor, defendant, or defence counsel may submit documentary/physical evidence and request examination of witnesses, experts, interpreters, or translators.
- Article 294-2: victim or legal representative may, on request and subject to statutory exceptions, be examined and given an opportunity to state views including harm and punishment.
- Article 295: court decides evidence applications and may conduct evidence examination ex officio.
- Article 307(1): fact finding must be based on evidence.
- Article 307(2): criminal facts require proof to the degree of no reasonable doubt.
- Article 308: probative value is determined by the judge's free evaluation.
- Article 308-2: evidence collected without due process cannot be used as evidence.
- Articles 309-310: coerced confession or a defendant's confession as the sole disadvantageous evidence cannot support guilt under the stated conditions.

These provisions support a non-symmetric procedural structure: source material, party submissions, admissibility/use filters, judicial assessment, and final legal judgment are not one state.

### ICCPR / UN Human Rights Committee

General Comment No. 32 as repeatedly quoted by the Committee states that the presumption of innocence places the burden of proving the charge on the prosecution, prohibits presuming guilt before proof beyond reasonable doubt, gives the accused the benefit of doubt, and requires treatment consistent with that principle.

Use in this case: this supplies the normative burden allocation. It is external legal data, not a DSD theorem.

## Scope decision

This case uses South Korean criminal procedure as a concrete legal instantiation and the ICCPR standard as a general rights reference. It does not claim that every criminal procedure system has identical roles or evidence rules.
