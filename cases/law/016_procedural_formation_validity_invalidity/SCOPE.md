# LAW-005 Scope and Generalization Status

## Universal structural claim

The case does not universalize one taxonomy of `void`, `voidable`, `invalid`, `unenforceable`, or `ineffective`.

The universal candidate is:

**when the source system distinguishes legal formation, present effect, defect, remedial/defeasibility status, recognition/enforcement, or later status change, those coordinates must be preserved unless the governing rule explicitly identifies them.**

A companion candidate is:

**a defect does not determine its legal consequence without the rule that assigns that consequence.**

## Witness families

- CISG / UNCITRAL — treaty architecture separating formation from validity;
- UNIDROIT Principles — harmonized private-law rules separating conclusion, binding force, avoidance, conditions, and rule-dependent illegality consequences;
- UNCITRAL Model Law on International Commercial Arbitration — procedural structure separating award, binding recognition, enforcement, refusal, suspension, and setting-aside-related status.

These are cross-system witnesses, not proof of a universal natural law.

## Generalization status

**Universal structural candidate — supported by multiple independent international/transnational witness families; active falsification remains open.**

## DSD status

Formation provides a meaningful non-collapse and partial-status comparison language.

Legal validity is not identified with Formation realization, legal invalidity is not identified with undefinedness, and legal nullity is not identified with channel absence.
