# Source Notes — LING-002 / Global Case 015

## External sources

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Dynamic Semantics
https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2016/entries/dynamic-semantics/

Relevant points:
- dynamic semantics treats meaning partly through context/state change;
- presupposition can be represented through preconditions on updates;
- presupposition failure can be represented by error/failure transitions;
- accommodation is an explicit operation that adjusts context so interpretation may proceed.

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Negation
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/negation/

Relevant point:
- Frege-Strawson-style analyses can treat failed presupposition as a truth-value gap rather than ordinary falsity;
- alternative bivalent/Russellian analyses remain available.

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Common Ground in Pragmatics
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/common-ground-pragmatics/

Relevant point:
- common ground is information presumed shared by participants and is central to presupposition and discourse interpretation.

## DSD source
Formation Axiom System: Dimensional-Structural Describability.

Relevant source-backed distinctions:
- staged formation rather than automatic promotion;
- Primitive Axiom V uses regime-global partial assignment;
- outside the assignment domain means undefined, not defined zero;
- failure of a Stage-VI condition gives channel absence, not a zero-valued channel;
- downstream application requires an explicit interpretation/bridge in domain applications.

## Scope discipline
Do not identify linguistic presupposition with DSD admission, and do not identify accommodation with a DSD primitive operation. They are application-level structural analogues only.
