# Synthetic Control Set 01 — Blinded Cases

Analyst instruction: evaluate each statement only from the text below. Ground truth is withheld. Do not assume every case contains a defect.

## S-001

For this test, a batch is defined to be “complete” exactly when every required compartment in that batch contains its required item. Inspectors verify each required compartment separately and find every one occupied by its required item. They conclude that the batch is complete under the stated test definition.

## S-002

Two production lines are tested using the same five external quality measurements. Every reported measurement is identical for the two lines over the test period. No information about internal machinery, staffing, or process order is collected. The audit concludes that the two lines therefore use the same internal production process.

## S-003

A historical database contains a field for annual donations. For years before the organization began recording donation amounts, the field is blank. During analysis all blank entries are replaced by 0, and the report concludes that the organization received no donations in every pre-recording year.

## S-004

A coding scheme is restricted to a declared domain D. Before the comparison, it is proved that the encoder f is injective on D. Two records x and y are both verified to lie in D, and their encoded outputs satisfy f(x)=f(y). The analyst concludes x=y.

## S-005

A registry assigns each active permit a unique serial number and publishes that uniqueness rule. Permit A and Permit B are both confirmed active in the same registry at the same time and display the same serial number. Under the registry’s uniqueness rule, the reviewer concludes that A and B are the same permit record.

## S-006

A laboratory proposes a new screening rule for “robust” samples. For the evaluation, a sample is entered into the robust group exactly when it passes that new screening rule. After processing 600 samples, every member of the robust group is found to have passed the screening rule. The laboratory concludes that the screening rule has experimentally demonstrated that it selects genuinely robust samples.

## S-007

A sealed-room navigation test gives a participant only the current corridor junction and the labels on the doors visible at that junction. The experimenter, who has the complete building plan, notes that the participant is presently two turns from the exit. The report then treats “being two turns from the exit” as part of the participant’s available evidence and uses it to show that the participant can identify which door leads out.

## S-008

A distributed forecasting system contains twelve local agents. None of the twelve agents individually stores a nationwide forecast; each stores only its assigned region. From this fact alone, an evaluator concludes that the assembled forecasting system cannot possess or output a nationwide forecast.
