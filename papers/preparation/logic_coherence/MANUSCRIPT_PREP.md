# Logic and Coherence — Manuscript Preparation

Canonical scope: main analyses 001–010 + `COH-001`.

## Central question

Across partial functions, free logic, many-valued logic, Institution Theory, modal/epistemic logic, linear logic, and related systems, where do DSD distinctions concerning undefined/defined states, staged formation, typing, and explicit bridges converge with established logical structures, and where do they not correspond?

## Recommended angle

**DSD as a Typed Partiality and Formation Audit Across Logical Systems**.

Do not claim a new logic. The candidate contribution is a cross-system audit discipline that prevents false identification of different partiality/semantic mechanisms.

## Core mechanisms

- missing / unavailable / undefined / defined zero / defined nonzero are not automatically identical;
- candidate / admitted / realized are distinct stages;
- an external logic's truth value is not DSD `undefined` without an interpretation bridge;
- strong equivalence claims require an explicit interpretation/bridge;
- `COH-001` is an internal coherence audit and should be separated from external convergence evidence.
