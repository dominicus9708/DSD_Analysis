# Korean `자유` — Translation, Lexicalization, and Concept-History Source Notes

Status: focused Korean-language source supplement to `LEXICAL_SEMANTIC_AUDIT.md`.

This file does **not** equate modern Korean usage with one dictionary definition or one historical genealogy. It records independent source families showing that `자유` has changed across translation, political, journalistic, literary, and lexical contexts.

## 1. Meiji translation and Korean reception

### 최경옥 (2018)
「메이지기, 번역한자어의 성립과 한국 수용 고찰 — [liberty]가 [자유(自由)]로 번역되기까지」
*비교일본학* 42, 353–372.

Metadata/source page:
https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE10809058

Reported source contribution:
- pre-Meiji `自由` included a traditional sense close to acting as one pleases / self-indulgence;
- in nineteenth-century Japan, Western `liberty` and related European terms were translated through multiple candidates, including `自由`, `自主`, `自在`, etc.;
- modern political `自由` emerged through translation competition rather than as a timeless one-to-one equivalent;
- the modern term entered late-Joseon/Korean usage and only gradually stabilized as an independent noun, after uses such as `자유권리` and `자유권`.

DSD relevance:
`modern translation identity != earlier lexical identity != Korean reception state`.

### 최재목 (2005)
「근대기 번역어 ‘자유’ 개념의 성립과 중국 유입에 대하여」
*남명학연구* 19, 385–404.

Source page:
https://kiss.kstudy.com/Detail/Ar?key=2525939

Use: comparative East-Asian concept-history control for the translation of `liberty/freedom` into `自由`.

## 3. Lexical polysemy and word formation

### 유경민 (2021)
「‘自由’의 용언형과 다의화에 대한 어휘론적 접근」
*언어와언어학* 94, 45–72.

Source page:
https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE11306797

Reported contribution:
- analyzes traditional and modern meanings of `自由` in relation to modernization;
- tracks the Korean forms `자유`, `자유하-`, `자유롭-`, `자유스럽-` and their usage;
- provides a lexical/corpus-oriented control against treating `자유` as one invariant semantic unit.

DSD relevance:
`headword identity != invariant sense inventory`.

## 4. 1950s semantic competition and cultural use

### 정보람 (2016)
「1950년대 ‘자유’ 개념의 번역과 『자유부인』」
*이화어문논집* 38, 5–24.

Source page:
https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE11384403

Reported contribution:
- discusses normative senses linked to autonomy/subjectivity;
- also identifies period usage in which `자유` could be framed as unstable excess, deviation, or something requiring control;
- useful for distinguishing philosophical/normative freedom from pejorative or disciplinary uses of the same lexical form.

### 권보드래 (2008)
「실존, 자유부인, 프래그머티즘 — 1950년대의 두 가지 ‘자유’ 개념과 문화」
*한국문학연구* 35, 101–147.

Source page:
https://kiss.kstudy.com/Detail/Ar?key=3733641

Use: coexistence of heterogeneous freedom concepts associated with existentialism and U.S.-influenced social-scientific/democratic discourse in postwar Korea.

## 6. Provisional semantic-history result

The current evidence supports **plural historical pathways**, not one eternal Korean definition:

`전통적 自由/자의적 의미`  
`!= modern liberty translation`  
`!= 권리/자주/독립 계열 정치용법`  
`!= 자율/주체성 계열 철학용법`  
`!= 일탈/방종에 가까운 사회적·문학적 용법`.

These categories can overlap in actual historical texts. They are not mutually exclusive dictionary bins.

## 7. Publication caution

Before manuscript use:
1. retrieve the exact official National Institute of Korean Language headword entry for `자유` and pin access/version;
2. inspect the full papers above, not just metadata/abstracts, for exact pages supporting each semantic-history claim;
3. distinguish Korean, Japanese, and classical-Chinese transmission rather than calling all of them simply “Korean meaning”;
4. separate translation history from contemporary ordinary-language meaning;
5. do not infer national psychology or political values from lexical history;
6. cross-check modern constitutional/legal uses with primary Korean legal texts.
