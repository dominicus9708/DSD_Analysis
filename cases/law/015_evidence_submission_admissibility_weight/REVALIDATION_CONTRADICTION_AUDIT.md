# LAW-004 Cross-Jurisdiction Contradiction Audit

Status: complete for first multi-jurisdiction refresh.

## Candidate C1 — every factual finding must come from party-submitted evidence

**Rejected.**

US federal judicial notice permits source-defined adjudicative facts to be noticed without ordinary formal proof on that point.

German ZPO §291 makes notorious facts proof-free, and §288 makes admitted facts proof-free within its conditions.

German ZPO §144 also permits the court itself to order inspection/expert involvement.

Therefore a universal party-submission pipeline is false.

## Candidate C2 — relevance implies use/admission

**Rejected.**

US FRE Rule 402 makes relevance only a general admissibility route subject to overriding law/rules; Rule 403 permits exclusion of relevant evidence for specified risks and costs.

England and Wales CPR 32.1(2) goes further procedurally by permitting the court to exclude evidence that would otherwise be admissible.

Therefore:

`relevant -> used`

is false universally.

## Candidate C3 — admissible means sufficiently probative to establish the fact

**Rejected.**

Admissibility/use status and final evaluation remain distinct in the sources. German ZPO §286 requires judicial evaluation of the whole proceeding/evidence-taking result. US relevance/admissibility rules do not themselves establish sufficiency of proof.

Thus:

`admissible != proved`.

## Candidate C4 — inadmissible evidence is equivalent to nonexistence

**Rejected.**

An item can exist, be possessed, disclosed, served, offered or considered for a limited procedural purpose while being excluded from another use.

Encoding inadmissibility as source-item absence destroys source-side information.

No direct Formation contradiction follows once existence and legal-use status are typed separately.

## Candidate C5 — every legal system has the same admissibility/weight/finding doctrinal stages

**Rejected as too strong.**

ECHR Article 6 doctrine expressly leaves admissibility and evidentiary assessment primarily to national law and reviews overall fairness rather than supplying one transnational evidence code.

German civil procedure uses free evaluation plus specific statutory evidence rules; US federal procedure uses a more rule-articulated admissibility architecture; England and Wales adds strong judicial case-management control.

The universal object cannot be one mandatory vocabulary or one exact stage count.

## Candidate C6 — unlawfully obtained evidence has one universal downstream result

**Rejected.**

The original Korean civil/criminal comparison already showed different results across regimes. ECHR Article 6 doctrine independently confirms that the Convention itself does not impose a universal admissibility result for unlawfully obtained evidence; fairness of proceedings as a whole is the relevant Strasbourg question.

Therefore:

`unlawful collection -> universal inadmissibility`

is false.

## Candidate C7 — probative force should map to Static Aggregation weight

**Rejected.**

None of the source systems supplies a universal scalar coefficient or linear aggregation operator corresponding to DSD static aggregation weights.

`probative value`, `weight`, judicial conviction and proof sufficiency are source-law concepts and cannot be imported into Static Aggregation by word similarity.

## Candidate C8 — judicial notice/notorious facts license DSD to pre-fill truth

**Rejected.**

Judicial notice and notoriety are source-defined procedural routes with conditions and consequences. They do not establish a general DSD rule that any analyst-known proposition may bypass formation.

This is especially important for predefinition discipline:

`analyst knows fact != source proceeding may treat fact as noticed/proved`.

## Candidate C9 — final finding reconstructs the evidence path uniquely

**Rejected.**

The same final finding can arise through different evidence sets, admissions, judicial notice, presumptions, or combinations of source-defined routes. Conversely, the same material can receive different use/evaluation treatment across regimes.

Therefore:

`same final finding -> same evidence structure`

is false.

## DSD contradiction judgment

No direct contradiction with Formation was found after replacing the fixed evidence pipeline with typed source-defined interfaces and routes.

Formation is useful only for preserving:

- source item versus legal-use instance;
- not-yet-formed versus formed status;
- role/proposition/proceeding-sensitive use;
- different downstream states.

It does not supply the legal transitions.

Axis-Property is not needed.

Static Aggregation is explicitly not used as legal proof arithmetic.

Dynamics is optional only for procedural-time transitions such as later exclusion, reopening, appeal or changed use status.

## Final audit status

The fixed five-stage model is **falsified as a universal architecture**.

The narrower source-indexed evidence/fact-interface non-totalization candidate survives.
