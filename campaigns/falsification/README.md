## Boundary rule

A discovered limitation in a signature, type, or design choice is not automatically an internal contradiction. Record separately:

- contradiction in a legal model;
- excluded candidate outside the current signature;
- optional design extension;
- empirical/interpretive issue outside the formal claim.
