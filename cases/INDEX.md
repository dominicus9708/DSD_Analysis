## Numbering rule

- A **Global Case** number is unique only in this reconciled cumulative table.
- Domain-local IDs such as `LAW-`, `ADMIN-`, `MATH-`, and `LING-` remain the stable identifiers within their domains.
- A historical branch may retain older numeric directory prefixes for provenance; those prefixes do not override this table.
- The next newly opened reconciled Global Case is **033**.
- Global Case 011 remains reserved for its already prepared logic case until that case is analyzed or explicitly abandoned; it is not reused.
