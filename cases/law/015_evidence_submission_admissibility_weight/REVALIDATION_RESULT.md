# LAW-004 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Result

Status: cross-jurisdiction revalidation complete.

Original historical `RESULT.md` is preserved.

## 1. Answer-first result

The original non-collapse insight survives, but the linear chain

`existence -> submission -> admissibility/use -> probative evaluation -> factual finding`

is **not** a universal five-stage architecture.

Rejected strong forms include:

- every factual finding must pass through party-submitted evidence;
- every relevant item is necessarily admitted/used;
- every admissible item contributes enough to establish a fact;
- every source system separates admissibility, weight and finding with the same doctrinal boundaries;
- every fact finding is generated from evidentiary items rather than admission, judicial notice, notoriety, presumption or another source-defined route;
- every unlawfully obtained item has the same admissibility consequence.

The surviving universal candidate is:

**when a source legal or institutional regime distinguishes information/material existence, procedural production or acquisition, legal relevance/use conditions, exclusion or limitation, evaluative/probative status, proof-free routes, and final factual determination, preserve those distinctions and the source-defined interfaces among them. Do not assume one mandatory linear path or infer a downstream status from the existence, relevance, submission, exclusion, or use status of an item alone.**

Compactly:

`source/material state != procedural-use state != evaluative state != final factual determination`.

But these are source-sensitive coordinates in an interface graph, not universal mandatory stages.

## 2. United States witness — relevance is not final admissibility or proof

Federal Rule of Evidence 401 defines relevance relationally to a fact of consequence.

Rule 402 makes relevant evidence generally admissible but subject to the Constitution, statutes, the Evidence Rules and other Supreme Court rules.

Rule 403 permits a court to exclude relevant evidence when probative value is substantially outweighed by specified dangers such as unfair prejudice, confusion, delay, waste or cumulative presentation.

Therefore:

`relevant != necessarily used`;

and:

`admitted/usable != fact proved`.

Federal judicial-notice practice adds a stronger counterexample: under Rule 201 conditions a court can notice an adjudicative fact without ordinary formal proof on that point.

Thus:

`factual determination route != party-submitted evidence route only`.

## 3. German witness — proof-free facts and court-initiated evidence routes

German ZPO §144 permits the court to order inspection and expert involvement and to require production of an object for that purpose, subject to statutory limits.

Therefore a pure party-submission model is false.

ZPO §288 provides that properly admitted facts need no proof.

ZPO §291 provides that facts notorious to the court need no proof.

ZPO §286 directs the court to decide factual truth or falsity by free conviction considering the whole content of proceedings and the result of any evidence-taking, subject to source-defined statutory evidence rules.

These provisions jointly show:

`factual finding != mandatory passage through one submitted-evidence pipeline`.

They also show that final evaluation is not reducible to one universal item-by-item weight arithmetic.

## 4. England and Wales witness — otherwise admissible evidence can still be excluded

CPR Part 32.1 gives the court power to control:

- the issues on which evidence is required;
- the nature of evidence required;
- the way evidence is placed before the court.

Rule 32.1(2) permits the court to exclude evidence that would otherwise be admissible.

Therefore:

`otherwise admissible != necessarily received/used`.

The same Part separately regulates service and actual trial use of witness statements, showing that possession/existence, service, reliance, oral use and court control can occupy different procedural states.

This independently defeats a simple one-bit `admissible/inadmissible` model.

## 5. ECHR witness — no universal Convention evidence code

Article 6 doctrine states that the Convention guarantees fairness but does not itself lay down universal admissibility rules. Admissibility, evidentiary assessment and probative weight are primarily matters of national law and national courts; Strasbourg asks whether proceedings as a whole were fair.

This is important counterpressure because it prevents LAW-004 from promoting one national evidence taxonomy into a universal legal architecture.

In particular:

`unlawfully obtained -> inadmissible`

is not an ECHR-level universal theorem.

The governing domestic rules and overall fairness analysis must be retained.

## 6. Revised structural model

A source-faithful evidence/fact record may require coordinates such as:

`(source item/information, proposition, proceeding, actor/source, acquisition route, submission/service status, court-initiated route, relevance status, legal-use/exclusion status, purpose/limitation, admission/judicial-notice/presumption route, evaluative status, proof/sufficiency status, final finding, governing rule, time, regime)`.

Not every source system uses every coordinate.

The point is to preserve whichever distinctions the source actually makes and not to manufacture missing stages merely to fit the model.

The structure is therefore better represented as a directed interface graph with multiple legal routes than as one mandatory chain.

## 7. Relation to LAW-001 through LAW-003

LAW-001 established that missing/nonperformed input does not acquire the identity of a formed opposite input merely because of downstream consequence.

LAW-002 established that office identity does not fix procedural-function identity and that source-defined interfaces govern transfer across functions.

LAW-003 established that role, authority, act and attribution cannot be inferred from one another without source rules.

LAW-004 extends the same restraint to information and proof:

`item exists != legally usable for every purpose`;

`item is relevant != necessarily admitted/used`;

`item is admissible != fact established`;

`fact treated as established != proof that one ordinary evidence route occurred`.

## 8. Predefinition significance

LAW-004 supplies an especially direct criticism of unjustified predefinition.

Do not predefine:

`exists -> submitted`;

`submitted -> admissible`;

`relevant -> admitted`;

`admissible -> persuasive`;

`persuasive item -> fact proved`;

`unlawful collection -> one universal exclusion result`.

But the opposite mistake is also forbidden:

`no ordinary submitted evidence -> fact cannot be procedurally established`.

Judicial notice, admissions, notoriety, presumptions and court-initiated evidence routes show that source systems may supply other paths.

The surviving rule is therefore:

**do not pre-fill either a positive or negative downstream evidence/fact status. Preserve the source rule and route that actually creates, blocks, limits, or substitutes for that status.**

## 9. DSD Formation result

Formation remains useful only as a typed non-totalization discipline.

It can preserve:

- source item versus legal-use instance;
- proceeding/proposition/purpose-sensitive status;
- not-yet-formed versus formed downstream state;
- distinct routes and role tags.

But do not identify:

`legal evidence != Formation channel`;

`legal admissibility != Formation admission`;

`inadmissibility != channel absence`;

`judicial notice != DSD analyst-defined truth`;

`probative value != Static Aggregation weight`;

`factual finding != DSD finite sum`.

No direct contradiction with Formation was found after removing the fixed linear pipeline assumption.

Axis-Property is not required.

Static Aggregation is explicitly not required for legal proof evaluation.

Dynamics is optional only for time-indexed procedural transitions and does not supply evidence law.

## 10. Main negative results

Rejected:

- universal mandatory five-stage evidence pipeline;
- party-submission-only route to factual findings;
- relevance as sufficient for actual use;
- admissibility as sufficient for proof;
- inadmissibility as source-item nonexistence;
- one universal exclusion result for unlawfully obtained evidence;
- universal doctrinal boundary among admissibility, weight and fairness;
- legal probative value as a DSD scalar weight;
- final finding as unique reconstruction of the evidentiary path.

## 11. Generalization status after revalidation

LAW-004 moves from:

`universal candidate under active falsification`

to:

**`cross-jurisdictionally corroborated source-indexed evidence/fact-interface non-totalization candidate; fixed five-stage pipeline falsified by judicial notice, admissions, court-initiated evidence, otherwise-admissible exclusion and regime-variable admissibility rules; active falsification remains open`.**

The Korean first-pass analysis remains a valid domestic witness, not the basis of universality.

## 12. Cross-case significance

LAW-004 completes the cross-jurisdiction refresh of LAW-001 through LAW-004.

Its strongest common rule is:

**do not infer the identity or downstream legal state of information merely from its existence, submission, relevance, apparent truth, or later factual effect. Preserve the source-defined rule and route that authorizes use, exclusion, evaluation, proof-free treatment, or final factual determination.**

This also confirms the analysis/design separation rule: external evidence systems are not forced into DSD stages, and the DSD application schema is narrowed when source law provides alternative paths.
