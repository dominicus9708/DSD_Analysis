# LAW-013 Contradiction Audit

## 1. Enactment/adoption automatically means immediate operation

Rejected.

VCLT Article 24 distinguishes adoption/consent and entry into force, while Article 25 allows a separately authorized provisional-application path.

## 2. Present rule automatically governs the past

Rejected.

VCLT Article 28, US Ex Post Facto doctrine and ECHR Article 7 all supply source-bounded non-retroactivity rules in their respective regimes.

The stronger claim `all law is universally non-retroactive in the same way` is also rejected: the US Ex Post Facto prohibition is specifically criminal/penal, and source systems can expressly provide different temporal rules.

## 3. Repeal/termination erases earlier legal operation

Rejected.

VCLT Article 70 preserves rights/obligations/legal situations created before termination, while Canada's Interpretation Act preserves previous operation, accrued rights/liabilities, offences, penalties and proceedings subject to source rules.

## 4. Repeal means all old proceedings stop

Rejected.

Canadian Interpretation Act s. 43 expressly permits specified investigations/proceedings/remedies to continue despite repeal.

## 5. Temporal change is simple state replacement

Rejected.

Sources preserve overlapping historical, current, residual and transitional states. The correct abstraction is a time-indexed graph, not deletion and overwrite.

## 6. DSD comparison

### Formation

Can preserve typed time-indexed states and distinguish not-yet-formed/current/residual application records, but does not determine a source rule's retroactive scope.

### Dynamics

Potentially relevant as a representation layer for temporal change, but the source supplies commencement, repeal, savings, retroactivity, mitigation and residual-effect rules.

Do not identify:

`legal commencement != DSD formation event`;

`repeal != structural deletion`;

`residual legal effect != persistence inferred by Dynamics`;

`retroactivity != backward causal dynamics`.

### Axis-Property

Not required.

### Static Aggregation

Not required.

## 7. Direct contradiction

No direct DSD axiom contradiction was found.

The strongest DSD-side risk is confusing a clean mathematical state transition with legal temporal replacement and thereby deleting source-recognized historical or residual states.
