# ADMIN-002 / Global Case 027 — Delegation, Escalation, Approval, and Authority

Status: first-pass cross-domain falsification plan.

## Question

How should an organization distinguish formal position, delegated authority, retained responsibility, decision authority, technical/review authority, escalation rights, transfer of command, and actual implementation?

## Strong hypotheses to attack

1. Higher formal rank always determines the operative decision authority.
2. Delegating authority necessarily delegates responsibility/accountability in the same way.
3. One organization has one simple authority chain for every issue.
4. A dissenting or escalation path is equivalent to disobedience or bypassing the system.
5. Transfer of command is equivalent to replacement of all prior responsibility and state.
6. Approval/review authority and execution authority are the same function.
7. A delegated authority has unlimited scope unless explicitly revoked.

## Witness families

- U.S. Army ADP 6-0 on delegation and retained command responsibility.
- FEMA/NIMS ICS delegation-of-authority and transfer-of-command procedures.
- NASA governance, Technical Authority, independent review, dissenting-opinion escalation, and risk-acceptance authority.
