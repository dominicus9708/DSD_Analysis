# LAW-001 through LAW-014 Final Legal-Domain Synthesis

Status: legal-domain foundation and closing sequence complete; extension work remains optional.

## 1. Answer-first conclusion

The completed legal campaign does **not** establish that DSD is a foundation of law, that law proves DSD axioms, or that every legal system shares one universal procedure.

What repeatedly survives across independent legal and institutional witness families is narrower:

**when a source system distinguishes states, functions, roles, authority, evidence status, temporal status, responsibility basis, individual inputs, collective outputs, or institutional effects, do not collapse or transfer them merely because they are connected by one event, office, hierarchy, chronology, numerical count, or downstream consequence. Preserve the source rule that performs each transition, substitution, attribution, exclusion, aggregation, or institutional formation.**

The strongest DSD-compatible result is therefore an operating discipline of **typed non-totalization, source-indexed interfaces, provenance preservation, and predefinition auditing**.

## 2. Refined predefinition rule

The legal campaign sharpens predefinition criticism into a two-sided rule:

**do not pre-fill either a positive or a negative downstream status before the source rule or independently justified bridge that creates that status is established.**

Rejected positive promotions include:

- nonaction -> opposition/assent;
- submitted evidence -> admissible evidence;
- admissible evidence -> fact proved;
- formal title -> effective authority/control;
- individual guilt -> superior/organizational/State responsibility;
- enacted/current law -> past applicability;
- individual YES -> collective adoption.

Rejected negative promotions include:

- no ordinary authority -> no possible attributed effect;
- no ordinary evidence submission -> no possible procedural fact treatment;
- failure of one proof route -> opposite fact established;
- superior did not directly perpetrate -> no possible superior responsibility;
- official violated instructions -> no possible State attribution;
- law repealed -> all historical legal effects gone;
- abstention -> no possible institutional effect.

Predefinition is not wrong merely because it is prior. It is analytically unjustified when its exclusion or promotion power is not independently warranted for the claim being tested.

## 3. Repeated cross-case structures

### 3.1 Identity is not downstream effect

`input identity != source-assigned consequence`.

LAW-001, LAW-003, LAW-004, LAW-013 and LAW-014 independently reinforce this.

Examples include nonaction with default consequences, unauthorized acts later ratified, evidence excluded despite existing, repealed law retaining residual effects, and abstention receiving different counting effects under different voting regimes.

### 3.2 Role, office, authority and function are separate coordinates

`actor/office != role/function != authority/control != attributed effect`.

LAW-002, LAW-003 and LAW-012 show that one actor may carry several functions, one function may be distributed, formal title may not establish effective control, and responsibility/attribution can arise through different source rules.

### 3.3 Source systems are often graphs, not mandatory chains

A safer abstraction is:

`G = (V, E_R)`

where vertices are source-distinct states and edges are source-authorized transitions/relations.

LAW-003 adds apparent-authority and ratification paths; LAW-004 adds judicial notice, admissions and court-initiated evidence; LAW-011 adds non-monotonic reconstruction; LAW-013 adds commencement, savings and residual-effect paths; LAW-014 adds thresholds, veto/blocking, bicameral concurrence and presentment.

Stage distinction therefore does not imply one universal real-world stage order.

### 3.4 Non-identity is not mutual exclusion

`A != B` does not imply `A intersection B = empty`.

One actor can carry several functions. Sanction and reparation can coexist. Individual and State responsibility can coexist. Institutional decisions can coexist with dissenting member positions.

### 3.5 Historical, epistemic and institutional states are distinct

LAW-002, LAW-004 and LAW-011 jointly support:

`historical event != perception/record/trace != reconstruction != institutional finding`.

An ideal complete historical description can be a comparison ideal but is not treated as investigator-, witness-, court- or analyst-accessible ground truth in real cases.

LAW-011 further adds:

`report count != independent-information count`.

Source dependence, contamination and uncertainty must be preserved where relevant.

### 3.6 Legal time is rule-indexed

LAW-005, LAW-008, LAW-009 and LAW-013 show:

`historical operation != current force != residual effect`.

Chronological order alone does not create legal applicability, repeal consequences, retroactivity, savings, finality or review relations.

### 3.7 Individual inputs do not automatically become collective acts

LAW-001 and LAW-014 show:

`individual input != collective decision != final institutional act`.

The collective status is formed by source-defined constitutive operators such as quorum, threshold, weighting, veto/blocking, inter-body concurrence and presentment.

The institutional result does not rewrite dissenting, abstaining or nonparticipating members' individual input identities.

## 4. What was actually falsified or narrowed

The campaign did not merely accumulate confirming examples. Strong application hypotheses were repeatedly rejected or narrowed:

- `nonaction never has consequences` — rejected;
- fixed one-office-per-function legal architecture — rejected;
- `authority yes/no -> effect yes/no` — rejected;
- mandatory five-stage evidence pipeline — rejected;
- one universal defect -> invalidity result — rejected;
- one global deontic value model — narrowed/rejected;
- `right/power possessed -> exercise/effect` — rejected;
- one universal rule-conflict resolution path — rejected;
- `greater irreversibility -> universally stronger safeguards` — falsified;
- rigid universal punishment/remedy taxonomy — rejected;
- exact-sensor/monotone investigative triangulation — rejected;
- formal-rank/upward responsibility transfer — rejected;
- enactment/current force/repeal as simple temporal overwrite — rejected;
- universal simple-majority collective-decision model — rejected.

These negative results are important because they show the analysis was capable of cutting DSD-style application assumptions rather than preserving them by reinterpretation.

## 5. DSD layer audit after LAW-001 through LAW-014

### Formation

Useful for:

- typed state separation;
- role-sensitive identity;
- partial/not-yet-formed status;
- source/provenance-sensitive records;
- non-totalization and interface auditing.

Not licensed by the legal campaign:

- legal admissibility = Formation admission;
- legal invalidity/exclusion/acquittal = DSD absence/undefined/zero;
- legal role = Formation role by identity;
- collective vote result = Formation composition automatically;
- investigative reconstruction = Formation composition;
- investigative updating = Formation stage chain.

### Axis-Property

Not required by the legal foundation sequence.

Hierarchy, authority, command, capacity, control, weighting, severity or veto language alone does not establish a realized-axis interpretation.

### Static Aggregation

Not required for legal proof, responsibility, punishment, social cost, source reliability or collective voting merely because numerical or evaluative terms occur.

A valid use requires an independently justified common codomain, measurement rule, dependence model and aggregation/decision operator.

Even where a voting system defines a numerical threshold, that source operator is not DSD Static Aggregation by identity.

### Dynamics

Useful only as a representation layer for source-defined time-indexed changes.

It does not generate:

- legal ratification;
- review/finality;
- memory distortion;
- responsibility transfer;
- commencement/retroactivity/repeal/savings;
- quorum/veto/presentment;
- any other source legal or cognitive rule.

## 6. Direct contradiction status

No direct contradiction with the currently used DSD core axiomatic layers was established by the legal campaign after explicit type, role, regime and bridge correction.

This is **not** proof that DSD is correct or legally universal.

The result is narrower: the tested legal witnesses did not force a contradiction once application overreach was removed, while several overstrong DSD-style application assumptions were themselves falsified.

## 7. Highest-risk DSD application failures exposed by law

1. Treating distinguishability as proof that reality must be divided exactly that way.
2. Turning a source graph into a linear stage chain.
3. Overloading one role tag with office, authority, function and effect.
4. Mapping external invalidity, exclusion, uncertainty or nonexercise directly to DSD absence/undefined/zero.
5. Treating DSD as the generator of source transition rules.
6. Invoking Axis-Property from metaphorical hierarchy/power/control language.
7. Scalarizing proof, responsibility, social value, harm, reliability or voting through Static Aggregation without a source-supported bridge.
8. Treating non-identity as mutual exclusion.
9. Importing analyst-owned or toy-model ground truth into real historical/institutional analysis.
10. Counting agreeing reports as independent corroboration without provenance/dependence analysis.
11. Treating hierarchy or organizational membership as automatic responsibility transfer.
12. Treating current legal status as historical overwrite.
13. Treating collective institutional attribution as personal endorsement by all members.

## 8. What law contributed back to DSD

Law was not merely a confirmation set. It forced DSD application discipline to become narrower and more realistic:

- fixed modules became source-sensitive interfaces;
- linear stages became graphs;
- authority became distinct from role and effect;
- uncertainty became preservable rather than a value to fill;
- investigation became potentially non-monotonic;
- responsibility became multi-route rather than vertically transferable;
- legal time became historical/current/residual rather than overwrite;
- collective decision became a source-defined constitutive operation rather than generic summation.

This is an external constraint on DSD application, not a legal theorem derived from DSD.

## 9. What DSD contributed to legal analysis

The reusable DSD-side audit questions are:

- Which source states are being silently identified?
- Which positive or negative downstream value is being filled before the source rule is known?
- Is an office/actor being confused with role, function, authority or responsibility?
- Is a later effect being used to reconstruct an earlier identity?
- Is one source route being treated as the only possible route?
- Is a source graph being rewritten as a chain?
- Is uncertainty being forced into YES/NO/zero?
- Are dependent sources being counted as independent evidence?
- Is evaluative language being converted to a scalar without measurement and aggregation rules?
- Is current legal status being used to erase historical states?
- Is institutional attribution being mistaken for every constituent's personal state?

The best current characterization is **typed non-totalization, source-indexed interface/provenance auditing, and predefinition justification analysis**.
