# DSD Free-Speech Structural Analysis — Exploratory Draft

Status: exploratory synthesis; no formal case ID assigned.

This draft does **not** begin from a fixed pro-censorship or anti-censorship conclusion. It starts from freedom of expression and asks how much structure is lost when speech, receipt, interpretation, publicness, power, age, education, economic dependence, representation, and responsibility are collapsed into one binary value.

DSD does not by itself derive legal permission, prohibition, criminal liability, or moral legitimacy. Any such conclusion requires an external legal, ethical, or institutional bridge.

## 1. Speech is not one state

At minimum:

`speech liberty != forced audience != channel ownership != credibility != representation != immunity`

A speech event should therefore be tracked as something richer than `S -> m -> R`.

Provisional event descriptor:

`U = <S,R,m,t,l,C,I_S,E_S,I_R,E_R,R_SR,A_R,U_R,J_R,X>`

where:
- `S`, `R`: speaker and receiver;
- `m`: utterance/content;
- `t`, `l`: time and physical/communicative location;
- `C`: delivery channel;
- `I_S`, `E_S`: speaker internal/external state;
- `I_R`, `E_R`: receiver internal/external state;
- `R_SR`: relational profile (authority, dependency, age, employment, sanction, exit cost, targeting, etc.);
- `A_R`: audience role;
- `U_R`: uptake;
- `J_R`: interpretation/judgment/attribution;
- `X`: downstream choices such as receive, refuse, avoid, rebut, agree, suspend judgment, report, or exit.

## 2. Formation-style decomposition

Application correspondence only:

`candidate speech -> admission/restriction -> realized utterance -> channel formation -> receipt -> uptake -> interpretation -> attribution -> response -> effect`

No transition is automatic.

- `uttered != heard`
- `heard != understood`
- `understood != believed`
- `interpreted != fact established`

This extends the already recorded linguistic distinctions in LING-003 (explicit content / implication / hearer inference), LING-007 (utterance / speech act / success conditions / later effect), and LING-009 (role / authority / legitimacy / compliance).

## 3. Axis-Property-style non-equivalence

A speaker is not represented by one global social-rank scalar.

`SpeakerProfile = (Internal, External, Relational)`

### Internal non-equivalence
Knowledge, belief, intention, uncertainty awareness, judgment capacity, coercion state, and formative history may differ across time.

Unobserved internal states remain unfilled:

`unknown intent != benign intent != malicious intent`

### External non-equivalence
Age, legal status, office, authority, resources, economic power, alternative channels, livelihood dependence, amplification capacity, channel occupancy, and accountability are distinct.

### Relational non-equivalence
Authority, dependency, age asymmetry, employment power, sanction ability, exit cost, targeting, and prior refusal are relation-sensitive and direction-sensitive.

`equal basic rights != equal speaker state != equal epistemic weight`

Relevant equality is relative to the rule/question under audit, not identity of every property.

## 4. Receiver non-equivalence and interpretive transformation

The receiver is an active participant:

`utterance -> receipt -> uptake -> interpretation -> attribution`

The same historical utterance can be reinterpreted after later information without rewriting the historical utterance or auto-filling unverified properties.

Example:

`mayor + positive feedback about company != bribery established`

Learning that the speaker was mayor can open a conflict-of-interest question. It does not itself establish bribery.

## 5. Publicness is a channel property, not merely a physical place

`Publicness(C) = f(access, audience, persistence, searchability, replicability, amplification, targeting, avoidability, ...)`

A public social-media post written in a private bedroom can be communicatively public. A one-to-one phone call made in a public square can be communicatively narrow.

`content existence != access != recommendation != targeted delivery`

## 6. Recipient capability profile

For basic-right peers, keep separate rights/capabilities to:

- speak / remain silent / refuse to answer;
- listen / not listen / see / not see;
- receive partially / later / again;
- agree / rebut / ask / request evidence / suspend judgment;
- start or end a relation;
- avoid / exit;
- block or unblock one's own input channel;
- share / not share / rebroadcast with counter-speech;
- affiliate / disaffiliate / reject representation;
- correct / request correction;
- report through platform, institution, or law;
- appeal or contest a decision.

Key boundary:

`self-channel blocking != censorship of another person's public channel`

## 7. Avoidability and the right not to listen

Use explicit `Avoidability(R,C)` so that a right not to listen does not silently become a power to censor everyone else.

Higher avoidability may support self-protective avoidance/blocking as the least intrusive response. Lower avoidability can arise from targeting, repetition, movement obstruction, continued contact after refusal, private-space intrusion, or other captive conditions.

`offense alone != general censorship bridge`

Comparative legal examples such as *Rowan v. Post Office Department* and *Cohen v. California* are useful counterpressure cases, not universal law.

## 8. Provisional structural labels

These are analytical candidates, not legal elements.

### Right
A valid active capability concerning one's own input, output, relation, judgment, or exit.

### Protection
An intervention that reduces a specified risk while preserving the protected person's present and future active capabilities as far as possible.

### License/indulgence candidate
A transition in which one person's claimed freedom automatically nullifies another person's independent capability, e.g.:

- `speech right -> forced listening right` (invalid without bridge)
- `livelihood need -> refusal invalidated` (invalid without bridge)
- `right not to listen -> general censorship authority` (invalid without bridge)

### Infringement candidate
Reduction of another person's valid active capability without a separately justified bridge.

### Oppression candidate
A pattern combining power asymmetry, repetition/institutionality, capability reduction, and weakening of exit, rebuttal, or appeal.

## 9. Justification-burden direction

Not a quantitative law; an audit heuristic:

`power asymmetry ↑ + irreversibility ↑ + capability loss ↑ => justification burden ↑`

The stronger actor's intervention into the weaker actor's speech, receipt, exit, or future autonomy requires more explicit external justification.

## 10. Age-sensitive protection without collapsing into general censorship

`content existence != access != recommendation != targeted delivery`

Age-sensitive analysis should track age, developmental capacity, dependency, content type, targeting, avoidability, and potential irreversibility.

Protection and participation are not automatic opposites. A child can possess expression/information/participation rights while also requiring protection against targeted manipulation that may undermine developing autonomy.

Exposure, persuasion, inducement, conditioning, manipulation, and coercion must remain distinct.

## 11. Education as a formation condition, not a speech license

`access != comprehension != competence != judgment`

Education, critical thinking, and media literacy can support discernment, verification, and correction. They should not be converted into a rule that people with less education have less basic standing to speak.

Translation can increase communication compatibility without equalizing background knowledge or epistemic premises.

## 12. Groups, visibility, representation, and responsibility

`visibility != representation`

`expertise != political representation`

`silence != consent != delegation`

Citizen organizations, victim organizations, agencies, expert groups, media outlets, and the wider public are separate channels. Observed volume of speech is not automatically the population distribution of opinion.

Policy influence and decision responsibility must remain separated:

`proposal != pressure != expert advice != legal decision != execution responsibility`

## 13. Economic non-equivalence

A large corporation and a livelihood-dependent street seller can perform superficially similar advertising while having very different resources, alternatives, amplification capacity, and dependence on the channel.

`same restriction != same capability loss`

But:

`livelihood need != forced listening right`

and

`right not to listen != livelihood-removal authority`

## 14. Lexical non-equivalence of freedom concepts

Do not treat translation identity as conceptual identity.

`same translation != same lexical field != same legal concept != same political theory`

The audit will compare English `freedom/liberty`, German `Freiheit`, French `liberté`, Spanish `libertad`, Japanese `自由`, Korean `자유`, and related terms (`autonomy`, `license`, `independence`, `방종`, `자율`, etc.) by source type and historical/legal/philosophical context.

This is not a claim about national essence.

## 15. Undetermined is a legitimate output

`insufficient bridge or evidence => UNDETERMINED`

Unverified intent, representation, corruption, consensus, or harm must not be back-filled from downstream reactions.

## 16. Provisional principles

1. Calling an act freedom does not establish that it is freedom.
2. Calling an intervention protection does not establish that it protects the target's capabilities.
3. A speech right does not automatically include forced listening, universal channel access, or immunity from counter-speech.
4. A right not to listen does not automatically include general censorship authority.
5. Equal basic rights do not imply identical social position, epistemic weight, resources, or reach.
6. Greater structural non-equivalence requires finer preservation of relation, direction, scope, and responsibility.
7. Greater power, irreversibility, and capability loss increase the audit burden for an external justification bridge.
8. Age-sensitive protection should preserve developing future autonomy and present participation rights.
9. Education is a basis for discernment, not a speech permit.
10. Visibility, organizational power, and speech volume do not automatically establish representation.
11. Later information can change interpretation conditions without rewriting prior states or creating unverified properties.
12. `UNDETERMINED` is a valid result, not an analytical failure.

## 17. Non-claims

- This is not yet a new theory of free speech.
- It is not a legal test.
- It is not a universal moral calculus.
- It does not claim DSD invented speech-act, audience, power, public-sphere, child-rights, or autonomy distinctions already developed in external fields.
- The candidate contribution is an integrated DSD audit protocol that preserves typed state, ordered relation, partial definition, non-retroactivity, and explicit bridge requirements across those independent literatures.
