Global case: 028

Primary purpose: coherence / reinterpretation
Secondary purpose: falsification of over-strong algebraic identification / aggregation-boundary audit

## One-line verdict

DSD Stage-VII finite composition is directly characterized by finite additivity on disjoint finite channel supports, not by a full homomorphism from finite-set union into ordinary vector addition except when all component terms vanish.

## Correspondence classification

- full finite-set union homomorphism: non-correspondence except trivial zero regime,
- disjoint finite additivity: direct correspondence,
- free commutative monoid / finite multiset linearization: additional encoding,
- aggregate equality => source equality: falsified,
- DSD axiom contradiction: none.

## Minimal witness

One nonzero channel `c` and `F=G={c}` suffice to separate union idempotence from vector addition.

## Cross-domain value
