# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 152–156

## Experiment 152 — Non-spherical multipole hierarchy

Assume the conditional weak-field progression specialization

\[
\nabla^2\psi = 4\pi\Lambda_t\rho
\]

for a compact source. Outside the support, the Green-kernel expansion gives

\[
\psi(\mathbf x)
=
-\Lambda_t\left[
\frac{M}{r}
+\frac{\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf D}{r^2}
+\frac{1}{2}\frac{Q_{ij}n_i n_j}{r^3}
+\cdots
\right],
\]

with

\[
\mathbf D=\int \rho\,\mathbf x'\,d^3x',
\qquad
Q_{ij}=\int \rho\left(3x'_i x'_j-r'^2\delta_{ij}\right)d^3x'.
\]

Thus the associated acceleration-type response has monopole/dipole/quadrupole orders

\[
r^{-2},\quad r^{-3},\quad r^{-4},\ldots
\]

Verdict: CONDITIONAL theorem inside the linear progression-field branch.

## Experiment 153 — Centroid pruning

For positive mass, choose the source centroid as origin:

\[
\mathbf x_c=\frac{1}{M}\int \rho\mathbf x'\,d^3x'.
\]

Then

\[
\mathbf D=0.
\]

Therefore the leading anisotropic correction for a centered isolated source is generically quadrupolar, not dipolar.

Verdict: CONFIRMED as a coordinate-centering result.

## Experiment 154 — Far-field suppression scale

For support radius \(R\),

\[
\left|Q_{ij}n_i n_j\right|
\le 2MR^2.
\]

Hence the quadrupole potential correction is at most order

\[
\frac{|\psi_Q|}{|\psi_M|}=O\!\left((R/r)^2\right).
\]

For the gradient/acceleration response, the quadrupole contribution decays one additional power of \(r\), while its relative suppression to the monopole remains order \((R/r)^2\).

Verdict: CONFIRMED scaling control.

## Experiment 155 — Exact density factorization

If two structural states satisfy

\[
\rho_A(\mathbf x)=\rho_B(\mathbf x)
\]

and use the same boundary condition, uniqueness of the linear elliptic problem gives

\[
\psi_A=\psi_B.
\]

Therefore relation/property differences that leave the physical density field unchanged are invisible to \(\psi\) in this branch.

Equivalently, the field map factors through the density descriptor:

\[
S\longrightarrow \rho(\mathbf x)\longrightarrow \psi.
\]

Verdict: CONFIRMED inside the conditional scalar density-only branch.

## Experiment 156 — Bounded-partition pruning

Changing bounded-component labels or partitions without changing \(\rho(\mathbf x)\) cannot change \(\psi\) in the density-only scalar branch.

Bounded-partition information can affect the progression field only if it:

1. changes the effective density field,
2. enters an explicit interface/surface source term, or
3. couples to an additional structural field.

Verdict: CONFIRMED pruning result.

## Impact

The earlier broad descriptor lattice narrows sharply in this specialization. Full density is a sufficient upstream descriptor for \(\psi\); relation/property sectors require an explicit additional coupling if they are to survive in the gravitational response. For centered compact sources, anisotropic density information appears first at quadrupole order and becomes relatively suppressed as \((R/r)^2\).
