## Scope

The domain does not treat one jurisdiction's positive law as a universal foundation. Each case separates a structural candidate, jurisdictional/institutional witnesses, and generalization status.

Analysis and design remain separate: source law is not redesigned to fit DSD, and DSD is not altered merely to force agreement.

## Final recurring rule

The strongest recurring result is not a universal legal pipeline or taxonomy.

It is a source-sensitive operating discipline:

**do not infer a source state, function, authority, responsibility, evidentiary status, temporal status or collective state from a downstream effect, office/title, chronology, raw count, or later institutional outcome; and do not pre-fill either a positive or negative downstream state without the source or independently justified bridge rule that performs the relevant transition, transfer, attribution, exclusion, substitution, aggregation or institutional formation.**

`A != B` does not imply mutual exclusion.

Source structures may be represented as:

`G = (V, E_R)`

where each edge is licensed by a source or independently justified bridge rule `R`.

## Closing extensions

### LAW-011 — investigative reconstruction

`historical event != perception != later memory != record/trace != reconstruction != institutional finding`.

`report count != independent-information count`.

Investigation can be non-monotonic; it is not identified with a mandatory Formation stage chain.

### LAW-012 — distributed responsibility

`same event != same responsibility basis`.

`direct perpetration != ordering != superior omission`.

`formal command != effective control`.

`individual liability != State attribution/responsibility`.

### LAW-013 — legal temporality

`historical operation != current force != residual effect`.

Enactment, commencement, provisional application, retroactivity, repeal, transition and residual effects are source-rule indexed.

### LAW-014 — collective decision formation

`individual position != collective decision != final institutional act`.

Quorum, voting thresholds, weighting, veto/blocking, multi-body concurrence and presentment are constitutive source rules. Institutional attribution does not rewrite each member's personal position.

## DSD layer discipline

- **Formation:** typed status, role, partiality, provenance and non-totalization only; it does not generate law.
- **Axis-Property:** not invoked merely because law uses hierarchy, authority, control, power, weighting or severity language.
- **Static Aggregation:** not identified with proof, responsibility, social value, reliability or voting operators without an independent common codomain, measurement/dependence model and operator.
- **Dynamics:** may represent source-defined time states but does not create legal, cognitive or institutional transition rules.

## Closure judgment

No direct DSD axiom contradiction was established by the legal campaign after explicit type, role, regime and bridge correction. This is not proof of DSD or legal universality; multiple overstrong DSD-style application assumptions were themselves falsified.

Further law work is now optional extension/maintenance. The higher-value next test is whether the refined non-totalization, provenance, uncertainty and source-rule disciplines recur independently in another roadmap domain.
