# LING-009 / Global Case 022 — Plan

## Topic
Social roles, authority/power relations, directionality, scope, and conflict.

## Primary question
Does a role label or observed compliance determine the direction, legitimacy, scope, and effect of an authority relation?

## External comparison
Use social-institution and authority literature to separate:

- role occupancy;
- ordered authority/power relation;
- de facto obedience/effective power;
- normative authority;
- target/action scope;
- conflicting authority claims;
- downstream compliance or institutional effect.

## DSD pressure test
1. Formation retains role in operational-channel identity.
2. Axis-Property permits binary and higher-order typed relation records.
3. Ordered inputs must remain ordered unless symmetry is independently established.
4. No property name such as `authority` supplies its social/normative semantics automatically.

## Finite witnesses
- A supervisor `s` and subordinate `h`: `Authority(s,h,submit_report,C)=1` while the reverse ordered relation is 0.
- The same directive uttered in the reverse relation need not generate the same institutional obligation.
- A subordinate may comply with an actor lacking normative authority; compliance therefore does not reconstruct legitimacy.
- Two superiors may each hold authority in different scopes, so conflict resolution requires an additional priority/jurisdiction rule.

## Expected classification
Primary: structural reinterpretation.
Secondary: coherence / predefinition restraint / relation-direction audit.
