# LAW-003 Scope Reclassification

Status: cross-jurisdiction revalidation complete; original `RESULT.md` preserved.

## Universal structural claim

The original shorthand

`underlying person/entity != operative role != authority != act != attributed effect`

survives only as a source-sensitive non-totalization rule, not as a rigid universal one-way pipeline.

The revalidated candidate is:

**when a source regime distinguishes the underlying person/entity, operative role or capacity, actual authority and its scope, apparent or official capacity, performed act, attribution/effect, and later ratification/confirmation, preserve those coordinates until the source rule links or transforms them. Authority status alone does not universally determine attribution or effect.**

## Independent witnesses

The current cross-jurisdiction witness set includes:

- UNIDROIT Principles Chapter 2 Section 2 — ordinary authority, apparent authority, unauthorized acts and ratification;
- Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties Articles 7-8 — purpose-sensitive State representation and subsequent confirmation;
- ILC Articles on State Responsibility Articles 4 and 7 — organ attribution and ultra vires/contrary-instruction conduct in official capacity;
- Korean Civil Act and Supreme Court materials — retained as the historical domestic witness.

## Counterpressure result

The binary model

`authority yes -> effect yes`

`authority no -> effect no`

is falsified as a universal model.

Apparent authority, later ratification/confirmation, and international attribution of ultra vires official conduct each supply a different source-defined route.

At the same time, official status or some authority does not totalize into every act or effect.

## Generalization status

**`cross-jurisdictionally corroborated role-authority-attribution non-totalization candidate; binary authority-effect model falsified; active falsification remains open`.**
