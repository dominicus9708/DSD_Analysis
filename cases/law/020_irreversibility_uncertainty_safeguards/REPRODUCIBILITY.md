# LAW-009 Reproducibility Protocol

## 1. Re-run order

1. Fix the universal hypothesis before selecting witnesses.
2. Separate the strong monotone hypothesis from the weaker irreparability-relevance candidate.
3. Record source-side terms exactly before DSD mapping.
4. Test at least one irreversible-sanction witness and at least one interim-preservation witness family.
5. Search explicitly for conditions showing irreparability is not sufficient.
6. Type legal uncertainty and review status as defined source states rather than DSD undefinedness.
7. Map to Formation only after source distinctions are fixed.
8. Invoke Dynamics only if a time-transition/recoverability relation is explicitly supplied by the application.

## 2. Minimum source checks

### Capital cases

Confirm from official UN sources:

- capital-specific safeguards;
- any comparison with non-capital protection;
- final judgment / appeal-review / clemency structure;
- fair-trial and proof constraints.

### ECHR

Confirm from the current official Rule 39 material:

- imminent risk;
- irreparable harm;
- exceptional nature / rigorous examination;
- preservation of practical and effective adjudication.

### ICJ

Confirm from an official provisional-measures order:

- risk of irreparable prejudice;
- urgency / real and imminent risk before final decision.

## 3. Falsification checklist

Reject a universal monotone theorem if any of the following remain unresolved:

- no common scalar ordering of irreversibility;
- no common scalar ordering of safeguards;
- irreparability requires additional legal conditions;
- safeguard form varies by institution;
- a source treats severity, right type, urgency, jurisdiction or procedure independently of irreversibility.

## 4. DSD checks

Verify from the Formation source that domain-specific applications require interpretation maps and that undefined assignment, defined zero and channel absence are distinct.

Verify from Structural Reorganization Dynamics that time-indexed states and succession are available but no universal conservation/recoverability law is imposed.

Do not infer legal irreversibility directly from DSD time direction.

## 5. Reproducibility verdict

A successful reproduction should reach both conclusions:

1. `more irreversible -> universally stronger safeguards` is too strong and must be rejected as a universal theorem;
2. multiple source systems nevertheless use irreparable/irreversible risk as a legally relevant condition for heightened or anticipatory protection.
