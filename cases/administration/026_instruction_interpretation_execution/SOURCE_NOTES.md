# Source Notes — ADMIN-001

## 1. U.S. Army mission command

Primary/official doctrine family: ADP 6-0, Mission Command: Command and Control of Army Forces.

Relevant structural points:

- mission command empowers subordinate decision making and decentralized execution appropriate to the situation;
- commander's intent states purpose, key tasks, and desired end state;
- mission orders provide enough direction for coordination without necessarily specifying exactly how subordinates must execute;
- disciplined initiative is expected when existing orders no longer fit unforeseen conditions;
- dialogue/shared understanding are part of the command relationship.

Counterpressure supplied:

`more procedural detail -> always better execution` is false as a universal organizational rule.

## 2. FAA air traffic control

Official FAA ATC/AIM materials distinguish issuance, acknowledgement, readback, correction, clarification, acceptance/refusal, and execution.

Relevant structural points:

- controllers ensure pilot acknowledgement of clearances/instructions;
- when read back, critical items must be checked and incorrect/incomplete readbacks corrected;
- hold-short instructions require explicit readback;
- pilots should request clarification/amendment when a clearance is not fully understood or is unacceptable from a safety standpoint;
- mutual verification is used to reduce communication errors caused by mishearing or incorrect numbers.

Counterpressure supplied:

`instruction issued -> instruction understood` is false.

`acknowledgement -> complete shared understanding` is too strong.

For safety-critical coordinates, explicit closed-loop confirmation can be source-required.

## 3. FEMA/NIMS Incident Command System

Official FEMA training/materials distinguish objectives, strategies, tactics, assignments, communication/reporting relationships, execution, evaluation, and revision.

Relevant structural points:

- unity of command gives each individual one designated supervisor and is intended to reduce confusion from conflicting directives;
- Incident Action Plans communicate objectives, actions/resources, and organizational relationships;
- objectives, strategies, and tactics are not identical;
- plans are executed, evaluated, and revised across operational periods;
- incident planning may begin under incomplete situation information and become more detailed as information/resources improve.

Counterpressure supplied:

`one original directive -> fixed execution path` is false.

`multiple supervisors/directives -> merely more information` is false under a source system that treats conflicting directives as a management hazard.

## Source-side synthesis

The three witness families do not support one universal specificity scale.

Instead they support regime-sensitive separation among:

- purpose/intent;
- encoded directive/objective;
- task/method constraints;
- recipient identity/authority;
- receipt;
- acknowledgement;
- interpretation/shared understanding;
- clarification/readback/backbrief/feedback;
- permitted discretion;
- execution;
- outcome;
- later evaluation/revision.

Different regimes place different verification requirements on different coordinates.
