# LAW-002 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Source Notes

Status: source packet for universal-first refresh.

## 1. ICC Rome Statute — office/function non-identity

Official source:
- Rome Statute, ICC: https://www.icc-cpi.int/sites/default/files/2024-05/Rome-Statute-eng.pdf

Relevant provisions:

### Article 54

The Prosecutor must investigate incriminating **and exonerating** circumstances in order to establish the truth and may collect/examine evidence.

Use:

`prosecutor office != guilt-only attribution function`.

The Prosecutor can instantiate responsibility-attribution functions while also carrying source-mandated truth-seeking and exculpatory investigative duties.

### Articles 67 and 68(3)

The accused has independent defence rights. Victims whose personal interests are affected may present views and concerns under conditions set by the Court, subject to fair-trial and accused-right safeguards.

Use:

`victim/source != prosecution`;

`victim participation != defence`;

`procedural actor != one globally exclusive regime`.

### Article 69

Evidence presentation, relevance/admissibility and Chamber control are separately regulated.

Use:

`source information != automatically judicially usable material`.

## 2. Germany — StPO §§160 and 244

Official sources:
- §160 StPO: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stpo/__160.html
- §244 StPO: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stpo/__244.html
- English official translation: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_stpo/englisch_stpo.pdf

### §160(2)

The public prosecution office must ascertain both incriminating and exonerating circumstances.

Use:

This independently defeats the simple equation:

`prosecutor = one-sided guilt constructor`.

### §244(2)

The court must, of its own motion, extend the taking of evidence to all facts and means of proof relevant to the decision in order to establish the truth.

Use:

`judgment function != passive terminal consumer of party-supplied evidence`.

A court can have an active source-side evidentiary function while retaining adjudicative responsibility.

Therefore office/function mapping is many-to-many rather than universally one-to-one.

## 3. ECHR Article 6 — tribunal, defence and prosecution relation

Official source:
- ECtHR, Guide on Article 6 (criminal limb), updated 28 February 2026: https://ks.echr.coe.int/documents/d/echr-ks/guide_art_6_criminal_eng

Relevant structure:

- Article 6(1): determination of a criminal charge by an independent and impartial tribunal established by law;
- Article 6(3): minimum defence rights;
- ECtHR guide: independence/impartiality requirements attach to the deciding tribunal, while representatives of the prosecution are parties rather than the deciding body.

Use:

Where the governing regime demands such separation:

`prosecution function != adjudicative function`.

But this does not prove a universal three-office adversarial architecture.

## 4. United States — Administrative Procedure Act

Official source:
- 5 U.S.C. §554: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&f=treesort&jumpTo=true&num=0&req=%28title%3A5+section%3A554+edition%3Aprelim%29+OR+%28granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title5-section554%29
- 5 U.S.C. §556: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=2023&req=granuleid%3AUSC-2023-title5-chapter5-subchapter2
- 5 U.S.C. §557: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title5-section557

Relevant structure:

### §554(d)

In covered adjudications, an employee engaged in investigative or prosecuting functions in a case generally may not participate or advise in the decision in that or a factually related case, subject to statutory exceptions.

Use:

The statute itself distinguishes investigative/prosecuting and decisional functions.

At the same time, the rule is source-specific and contains exceptions; it therefore does not justify an absolute universal separation theorem.

### §§556-557

The APA separately regulates reception of evidence, burden/evidence/record and initial/recommended/final agency decisions. The agency, members of the agency or presiding employees may occupy different decisional configurations.

Use:

`institutional body identity != one procedural function`;

`evidence reception != final decision`;

`initial decision != agency review/final decision`.

## 5. Cross-source counterpressure

The witness families jointly challenge the original 5+2 model if it is interpreted too literally:

1. prosecutors can have exculpatory/truth-seeking duties;
2. courts can actively develop evidence;
3. victims can participate independently of prosecution and defence;
4. administrative systems can place several procedural functions inside one agency while source rules separate particular personnel/functions;
5. evidence and decision rules may be distributed across multiple provisions and stages rather than one `R_E` and one `R_L` module.

Therefore the revalidated model must treat `P`, `D`, `J`, `R_E`, `R_L` as functional/interface labels, not universal offices or indivisible modules.

## 6. Source discipline

These materials do not establish one globally mandatory criminal-procedure architecture.

They are used to test whether distinctions among event/reference, source record, attribution, response, evidentiary usability and institutional finding recur while the **allocation of those functions varies**.
