# LAW-007 Source Notes

## 1. CRPD Article 12

Official OHCHR Convention text:
- Article 12(2): persons with disabilities enjoy legal capacity on an equal basis with others in all aspects of life.
- Article 12(3): States Parties must provide access to support persons may require in exercising legal capacity.
- Article 12(4): measures relating to the exercise of legal capacity must contain safeguards against abuse and respect rights, will and preferences.

General Comment No. 1 clarifies that legal capacity includes both legal standing (holding rights/duties) and legal agency (acting under law to create, modify or end legal relationships).

Source implication: do not universalize one narrow dictionary split between `capacity` and `exercise`; the source itself can use capacity broadly while separately regulating support and safeguards for exercise.

Official sources:
- https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Ch_IV_15.pdf
- OHCHR CRPD General Comment No. 1 materials.

## 2. UNIDROIT Principles

Relevant rules:
- Article 7.3.2: a right to terminate is exercised by notice; failure to give timely notice can cause loss of that right.
- Chapter 10: limitation rules distinguish a right from when it can be exercised and from whether it can be enforced.
- Article 10.9: expiry of a limitation period does not extinguish the right; its effect depends on the obligor asserting the limitation defence.

Source implication:

`right exists != right presently exercisable != right actually exercised != right enforceable`.

Official sources:
- https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/commercial-contracts/unidroit-principles-2010/chapter-7-section-3/
- https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/commercial-contracts/unidroit-principles-2010/chapter-10/

## 3. Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties

Official UN Treaty Collection text:
- Article 6: every State possesses capacity to conclude treaties.
- Article 7: a person represents the State for treaty purposes only under the stated full-powers/function conditions.
- Article 8: an act relating to treaty conclusion performed by a person not authorized under Article 7 is without legal effect unless afterwards confirmed by the State.

Source implication:

`State treaty capacity != particular actor authority != attempted exercise != legal effect != later confirmation`.

This overlaps partially with LAW-003 but serves here as public-international-law evidence that system-level capacity, actor-level authority, exercise event, and effect are not one state.

Official source:
- https://treaties.un.org/doc/treaties/1980/01/19800127%2000-52%20am/ch_xxiii_01.pdf
