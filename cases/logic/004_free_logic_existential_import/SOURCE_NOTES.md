# Case 004 — Source Notes

## 1. DSD Formation source

The current Formation Axiom System is a typed set-theoretic staged system, not a proof calculus for singular terms.

Relevant facts:

- `E^L_B` is the candidate structural-expression class.
- `P^L_B` is the candidate configuration class.
- Primitive Axiom I requires `Desexpr_L(x) => Admexpr_L(x)` and explicitly does not impose the converse.
- Primitive Axiom II requires sound restrictions and identity restrictions for admitted expressions.
- Primitive Axiom III constrains a declared realization relation but explicitly states that realization alone does not imply configuration describability.
- Closure Clause IV defines configuration describability by the full witness formula `Psi_L(p)`.
- Stage V assignment is restricted to the structural domain generated by describable configurations.

The paper also retains unsuccessful candidate configurations in the full formation descriptor. Thus candidacy is intentionally broader than successful formation.

## 2. Free Logic source discipline

### Meyer and Lambert
Robert K. Meyer and Karel Lambert describe free logics by the criterion that **no existence assumptions are made with respect to individual constants**. They distinguish this criterion from the independent issue of whether empty quantificational domains are allowed.

This is the primary external point used in Case 004.

### Lambert
Lambert's work on existential import is part of the historical development of logics that do not automatically attach existential import to singular terms.

### Modern negative free logic
Modern negative-free-logic work likewise treats singular terms as not necessarily referring; additional semantic/proof-theoretic rules determine the consequences of non-reference.

## 3. What is not being identified

The following identifications are rejected:

- DSD candidate expression = free-logical singular term;
- DSD admission = existence;
- DSD realization = denotation;
- DSD configuration describability = truth;
- DSD channel admission = existential quantification.

Those pairs live in different formal systems and have different semantics.

## 4. Legitimate comparison

The legitimate shared structural principle is narrower:

> A symbol, term, or candidate record being available at an earlier level does not by itself justify a stronger existence/formation status at a later level.

Free Logic provides an independent external example of rejecting an automatic existence presupposition. DSD independently rejects several automatic formation promotions.
