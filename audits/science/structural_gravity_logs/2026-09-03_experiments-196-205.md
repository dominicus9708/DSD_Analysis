# Structural Gravity Research Log — Experiments 196–205

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: conditional self-source audit

## Experiment 196 — Energy-label restraint

The progression-field equation alone does not define a physical field energy or its mass contribution. Self/backreaction is therefore first audited as a mathematical self-source term without calling it energy.

## Experiment 197 — Minimal dimensionless self-source

Test

\[
\nabla^2\psi
=
4\pi\Lambda_t\rho
+
\gamma|\nabla\psi|^2,
\]

with dimensionless \(\gamma\). This introduces nonlinear self-coupling without adding a new dimensionful anchor.

## Experiment 198 — Exact spherical exterior transform

Outside matter,

\[
\nabla^2\psi
=
\gamma|\nabla\psi|^2.
\]

Define

\[
U=e^{-\gamma\psi}.
\]

Then

\[
\nabla^2U=0.
\]

With spherical symmetry and \(U\to1\),

\[
U=1+\frac{A}{r},
\qquad
\psi
=
-\frac1\gamma\ln\left(1+\frac{A}{r}\right).
\]

## Experiment 199 — Weak-field matching

Matching the leading term to

\[
-\Lambda_t\frac Mr
\]

gives

\[
A=\gamma\Lambda_tM,
\]

hence

\[
\psi
=
-\frac1\gamma
\ln\left(1+\gamma\frac{\Lambda_tM}{r}\right).
\]

The nonlinear corrections depend on the dimensionless combination \(\Lambda_tM/r\); they do not determine \(\Lambda_t\).

## Experiment 200 — Acceleration correction

The spherical slope is

\[
\frac{d\psi}{dr}
=
\frac{\Lambda_tM}{r^2(1+\gamma\Lambda_tM/r)}.
\]

Thus the point response acquires compactness-dependent dimensionless corrections while retaining the same leading normalization.

## Experiment 201 — Self-source monopole

Integrating the field equation shows that

\[
\gamma\int |\nabla\psi|^2d^3x
\]

contributes to the far-field monopole unless canceled. Exact mass-only universality therefore requires one of the following:

1. absorb the contribution into an independently defined physical source mass;
2. cancel it with another term;
3. reject this self-source form.

## Experiment 202 — Nonlinear additivity failure

For two fields,

\[
|\nabla(\psi_A+\psi_B)|^2
=
|\nabla\psi_A|^2
+
|\nabla\psi_B|^2
+
2\nabla\psi_A\cdot\nabla\psi_B.
\]

Thus disjoint matter sectors can acquire a field-mediated cross contribution even when their bare densities add linearly.

## Experiment 203 — Separated-source cross scale

In the weak, well-separated compact-source limit,

\[
\int \nabla\psi_A\cdot\nabla\psi_B\,d^3x
\approx
4\pi\Lambda_t^2\frac{M_AM_B}{d}.
\]

The corresponding monopole-length correction is therefore of order

\[
\delta A_{\rm cross}
\approx
2\gamma\Lambda_t^2\frac{M_AM_B}{d}.
\]

This supplies a concrete realization of the previously abstract cross/binding correction term. Its sign and physical interpretation remain model-dependent.

## Experiment 204 — No-go persistence

A dimensionless nonlinear coefficient \(\gamma\) can generate source-dependent higher-order corrections but cannot create the missing \(L/M\) normalization. All corrections remain functions of combinations that already contain \(\Lambda_t\).

## Experiment 205 — Weak-field control parameter

Define

\[
\epsilon_S
=
|\Lambda_t|\frac{M}{R}.
\]

This is the natural dimensionless source-strength parameter for the branch. Linear progression theory is the \(\epsilon_S\ll1\) limit; nonlinear corrections can be organized in powers of \(\epsilon_S\) only after \(\Lambda_t\) is supplied.

## Verdict

- nonlinear self-correction without a new dimensionful anchor: CONDITIONAL YES;
- generation of the absolute \(\Lambda_t\): NO;
- field-mediated source cross term: CONDITIONAL YES;
- identification with physical binding or field energy: OPEN.
