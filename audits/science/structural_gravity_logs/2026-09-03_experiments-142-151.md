## New result
A compact weak-field structural-gravity specialization can be built from a dimensionless progression field \(\psi\), one explicit mass-to-progression coupling \(\Lambda_t[L/M]\), and a linear local source equation

\[
\nabla^2\psi=4\pi\Lambda_t\rho.
\]

For a compact source, the exterior Green representation is

\[
\psi(\mathbf x)=-\Lambda_t\int\frac{\rho(\mathbf x')}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|}\,d^3x'
\]

up to the sign convention chosen for the source equation.

For \(r\gg R\), the multipole expansion of this proposed progression equation gives a monopole \(M/r\) term followed by faster-decaying structural moments. Thus far-field detail erasure is no longer an independent assumption in this linear specialization; it follows partially from the exterior operator and source localization.

For an exactly spherically symmetric compact source, all non-monopole moments vanish and

\[
\psi(r)=-\Lambda_t\frac{M}{r},\qquad r>R,
\]

so any two spherical sources with the same total mass have exactly the same exterior progression field in this specialization, regardless of radial density profile.

Inside a spherical source,

\[
\frac1{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\frac{d\psi}{dr}\right)=4\pi\Lambda_t\rho(r)
\]

gives

\[
\frac{d\psi}{dr}=\Lambda_t\frac{M(<r)}{r^2}.
\]

With the single-metric low-speed response,

\[
\mathbf a(r)=-c_*^2\Lambda_t\frac{M(<r)}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf r}.
\]

Therefore local density and the acceleration-type slope need not have the same radial ordering; the slope reads the cumulative source in this specialization.

For the uniform/core-heavy/envelope-heavy control family at \(r=3R/4\),

\[
\frac{|a|}{c_*^2\Lambda_t M/R^2}
=(0.75,\;1.19048,\;0.66190)
\]

for uniform, core-heavy, and envelope-heavy respectively, while the local density ordering at that radius is envelope-heavy \(>\) uniform \(>\) core-heavy.

At a smooth center,

\[
M(<r)=\frac{4\pi}{3}\rho(0)r^3+O(r^5),
\]

so

\[
\frac{d\psi}{dr}=\frac{4\pi}{3}\Lambda_t\rho(0)r+O(r^3).
\]

Hence

\[
\mathbf a(0)=0,
\qquad
\nabla\nabla\psi(0)=\frac{4\pi}{3}\Lambda_t\rho(0)I.
\]

The center can therefore have zero absolute acceleration-type response but nonzero distortion curvature/relative-response gradient. In the control family the central Hessian magnitude ratio is core-heavy : uniform : envelope-heavy = \(4:1:0.4\).

Exterior same-mass density corrections would require a nonlinear source law, an additional structural field, nonspherical multipoles, or an interface/surface contribution.
