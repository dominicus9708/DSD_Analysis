# LAW-004 Cross-Jurisdiction Revalidation Plan

Status: active cross-jurisdiction falsification refresh.

## 1. Universal candidate under test

Initial candidate:

`information/evidence existence != submission != legal usability/admissibility != probative evaluation != factual finding`.

This is not assumed to be a universal five-stage pipeline.

## 2. Main falsification targets

Test whether any of the following stronger readings fail:

- every fact finding must pass through party submission of evidence;
- every legally usable item has been submitted by a party;
- relevant evidence is necessarily admissible;
- admissible evidence is necessarily persuasive or sufficient;
- inadmissible evidence is equivalent to nonexistence;
- evidence weight and admissibility are always sharply separated in the same doctrinal vocabulary;
- every factual finding results only from evidentiary items rather than judicial notice, admission, presumption, or another source-defined route;
- an unlawfully obtained or procedurally defective item has one universal admissibility result.

## 3. Witness families

Use independent source systems that put pressure on the fixed pipeline:

1. United States Federal Rules of Evidence and federal judicial-notice practice;
2. German ZPO, including free evaluation, court-ordered inspection/expert evidence, admissions, and notorious facts;
3. England and Wales Civil Procedure Rules Part 32, including judicial control over evidence and exclusion of otherwise admissible material;
4. ECHR Article 6 doctrine, which leaves admissibility and evidentiary weight primarily to domestic law while reviewing overall fairness.

Korean criminal/civil evidence law remains the original domestic witness and historical first pass.

## 4. Questions

- Does a universal five-step evidence pipeline survive?
- Can the court itself create or request an evidentiary route?
- Can facts be accepted without ordinary proof?
- Can relevant or otherwise admissible material still be excluded?
- Is legal usability distinct from logical relevance and factual truth?
- Does a final finding preserve the complete source-evidence structure?
- Which DSD layers are actually needed after counterpressure?

## 5. DSD discipline

Do not redesign evidence law to fit Formation.

Do not treat:

- legal admissibility as Formation admission;
- evidence exclusion as channel absence;
- probative force as Static Aggregation weight;
- fact finding as a DSD sum;
- judicial notice as pre-filled DSD truth.

The goal is to test whether source-defined status distinctions and alternative routes can be preserved without importing a DSD result into law.

## 6. Completion criterion

Conclude only after:

`universal candidate -> counterpressure -> independent witnesses -> DSD mapping -> contradiction audit -> generalization status`.
