# Case 009 — Primitive PII Direct Attack

## Target
Primitive Axis-Property Axiom PII in the current Axis-Property Axiom System.

## Main falsification questions
1. Can a bilinear-dependent property application be defined while the model lacks the required symmetric bilinear datum and still pass the surrounding typing rules?
2. Does PII over-trigger on declarations that have no actual local application?
3. Does PII guarantee semantic agreement between bilinear-dependent values/closures and the supplied symmetric bilinear form, or only the presence of such data?
4. Are there hidden circularities between the P3 bilinear layer, the P4 normal carrier, P5 property assignments, and P7 closure declarations?
5. Can PII become impossible to satisfy because of ambient dimension, degeneracy, or cardinality?

## Source discipline
The test is grounded in the current English Axis-Property paper, especially Definitions 3.1–3.3, Primitive PII and Definition 4.1, Definition 8.1, Closure Clause 8.5, Countermodel 9.7, and Remark 3.12.

## Success criterion for falsification
A genuine internal counterexample would be a legal layered primitive presentation that satisfies all typing/coherence requirements yet makes the intended PII condition contradictory, impossible, circular, or semantically stronger/weaker than what the formal theorem claims.

A scope limitation or pre-axiom declaration choice is recorded separately and is not treated as a contradiction.
