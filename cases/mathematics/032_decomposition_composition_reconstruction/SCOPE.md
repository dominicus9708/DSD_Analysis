# Scope

## Included

- finite Stage-VII channel families;
- fixed-support channel records and their summation map;
- support-tagged varying-support records;
- direct sums and unique additive decomposition;
- subset-sum uniqueness and dissociated-family criteria;
- exact injectivity/reconstruction conditions;
- absence versus selected-zero support fidelity.

## Excluded

- temporal dynamics except optional corroboration;
- physical interpretation of component values;
- infinite decompositions beyond remarks already present in the static paper;
- claims that DSD itself supplies channel-specific direct-summand subspaces;
- claims that direct-sum independence is necessary for every restricted admissible record class.

## Source discipline

The DSD papers are read on their own terms first. Standard mathematics is used only to classify the structures that the DSD maps instantiate. Additional subspace or additive-combinatorial assumptions are marked explicitly as extra conditions rather than DSD axioms.
