# LAW-001 Scope Reclassification

Status: cross-jurisdiction revalidation complete; original `RESULT.md` preserved as historical first pass.

## Universal structural claim after revalidation

The primary candidate is now:

**a nonformed/nonperformed act does not by itself carry the identity or value of an affirmative, negative, opposite, invalid, or otherwise formed act. If a legal or institutional regime assigns nonaction a consequence, default, sanction, assent effect, quorum effect, or other downstream result, that consequence is source-rule indexed and must not be read back into the identity of the missing act.**

In responsibility-attribution settings:

**failure to establish responsibility under the governing burden and proof threshold blocks the source-defined responsibility/guilt result, but does not by itself prove the opposite factual proposition.**

## Counterpressure incorporated

The following stronger claims are rejected:

- `nonaction never has legal/institutional consequences`;
- `silence can never produce assent effect`;
- `presumption of innocence means every burden on every subsidiary criminal issue always lies on prosecution`.

Australian compulsory voting and UNIDROIT silence/practice rules show that nonaction can receive source-defined consequences.

ECHR and US affirmative-defence materials show that source-defined presumptions or defence burdens can coexist with the presumption of innocence.

## Cross-jurisdiction / cross-institution witnesses

- UN General Assembly Rules of Procedure: presence, abstention, YES/NO vote, and downstream majority/quorum effects are distinct.
- EU Council/TFEU: abstention can be non-blocking under unanimity without becoming a YES vote.
- Australian Electoral Commission: turnout/non-voting and informal ballot status are separately tracked; non-voting can also be separately sanctionable.
- UK Electoral Commission: rejected ballots are formed ballot-paper objects subject to adjudication.
- UNIDROIT Principles: silence/inactivity does not in itself equal acceptance, while agreement/practice/usage can create a source-defined consequence.
- ICC Rome Statute Article 66: accusation, prosecution burden, proof threshold, and conviction are distinct.
- ECHR: presumption of innocence survives bounded presumptions of fact/law.
- US Ninth Circuit: government bears the offence-element burden; some affirmative defences may carry source-defined defence burdens.
- UK CPS: acquittal/not guilty reflects failure to meet conviction threshold and is not a positive contrary factual finding about the complainant.

## Generalization status

`cross-jurisdictionally corroborated universal non-totalization candidate; narrowed under explicit-default and burden-shift counterpressure; active falsification remains open`.

This is stronger than the previous single-/limited-witness status but remains short of a proof of legal universality.

## DSD status

Formation can preserve nonformation, formed action, validity/admissibility, source-default consequence, and downstream result as separately typed states.

Do not identify legal states literally with Formation primitives:

- absence is not automatically channel absence;
- abstention is not automatically defined zero;
- invalid ballot is not automatically undefined assignment;
- acquittal/not guilty is not factual innocence;
- presumption of innocence is not a Formation theorem.

No direct contradiction with Formation was found after cross-jurisdiction revalidation.
