# LAW-009 Contradiction Audit

## 1. Candidate claims attacked

### C1. Greater irreversibility universally implies a strictly stronger safeguard package

Rejected as too strong.

The selected sources do not supply a universal scalar ordering of irreversibility or safeguards.

### C2. Irreparable harm alone is sufficient for interim protection

Rejected.

ECHR Rule 39 operates only in exceptional circumstances and requires an imminent risk of irreparable harm plus rigorous source-side examination.

ICJ provisional measures require irreparable prejudice and urgency, including a real and imminent risk before final decision.

### C3. A final or existing legal decision automatically authorizes the irreversible act

Rejected.

Capital-case safeguards preserve additional stages such as appeal/review and clemency/pardon before execution.

### C4. Failure to authorize an irreversible consequence proves the underlying responsibility did not exist

Rejected.

A safeguard or review gate can block the consequence without establishing the global opposite factual proposition.

### C5. Legal uncertainty is DSD undefinedness

Rejected.

A pending appeal, unproved charge, unresolved review, or asserted risk can be a fully defined legal-procedural status.

### C6. Irreversibility is supplied by Formation or Dynamics

Rejected.

Formation is static and application-dependent. Dynamics supplies time-indexed states and succession but no universal legal recoverability relation. The legal application must supply the relevant restoration, equivalence, and safeguard rules.

### C7. Death-penalty safeguards prove a universal moral theorem about punishment

Rejected.

The sources provide positive legal and human-rights structures. DSD Analysis may compare them structurally but cannot derive a universal moral ordering of punishments from those sources or from DSD.

## 2. Direct contradiction test with Formation

After typing separately:

- source legal status;
- factual/proof status;
- review status;
- threatened or completed act;
- source-recognized irreparable risk;
- time;
- decision body;
- safeguard rule;
- downstream effect;

no direct contradiction with Formation was found.

Formation's distinction among undefined assignment, defined zero, channel absence and admitted channels helps avoid encoding legal review or uncertainty as absence.

## 3. Direct contradiction test with Dynamics

No direct contradiction was found after treating Dynamics only as a carrier for time-indexed structural states and succession.

The application must not reinterpret lineage succession as legal causation, and must not infer legal irreversibility from time-direction alone.

## 4. Strongest counterpressure result

The original research hypothesis

`more irreversible -> more safeguards`

survives only in narrowed form:

**multiple independent legal regimes treat irreparable or irreversible consequences as legally relevant to ex ante protection or heightened safeguards, but irreparability operates together with other source conditions and does not itself define one universal safeguard scale.**

## 5. Audit verdict

- monotone universal theorem: falsified as stated;
- narrower irreparability-relevance candidate: survives first-pass counterpressure;
- direct contradiction with Formation: none found;
- direct contradiction with Dynamics: none found under source-sensitive transition encoding;
- normative derivation from DSD: not established.
